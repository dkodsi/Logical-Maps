# Probability bridge audit

22 October 2026. Direct research source: the linked local notes; no publication attribution or database-status change.

## Exact intended scopes

The global candidate uses **C + boxed Plenitude + quantified Infinity_e**. It uses admitted graph-valued internal numbers and reals over the original N_e, which may be externally nonstandard. Neither unboxed Plenitude nor the finite Infinity schema is an equivalent replacement. The external Lean `Nat`, `List` and scalar structures in namespace `Classicism.Research.Probability` have not been interpreted in this language.

The weaker local candidate uses **C + Actuality + Atomicity_t + Boolean Completeness_t + quantified Infinity_e**, for a current graph state on current propositions in the actual-world core. It does not assert a globally rigid real-code field, an ambient operation realizing every graph, future validity, lifted higher-type probabilities, or global symmetry results. Event top is ambient top; the local truth unit is the actual-world atom. These must not be identified. See [the local note](../proof-refinements/local-propositional-probability.md).

The `ProbabilityCuts` source modules use the fixed C language: one admitted code type σ, predicates σt, families (σt)t, binary comparison/reflection graphs and ternary addition graphs. No type quantification occurs inside a formula, and no operation returns e. Every hypothesis and conclusion is current; none is boxed. No infinitary language extension has been introduced.

## What the cut lemmas actually provide

The union of a represented nonempty bounded family of proper rounded lower cuts is itself a cut and a least upper bound for **current inclusion**. Two such LUBs are currently coextensional. Literal identity and a single-valued coded supremum require the stated restricted extensional-code field and a representative of the union in that field. Those are explicit interface premises, not conclusions about bare C.

The order/addition modules prove linear comparison, principal bounds and injectivity, addition closure, union distribution, associativity/commutativity transport, the principal-cut addition law and additive zero. The premises are actual graph laws on the proposed rational codes. The reflection module proves cut closure, antitonicity, involution up to coextension, and one inclusion in the additive-inverse law.

**A dense ordered-group graph alone is insufficient for the missing additive-inverse inclusion.** The internal rational construction must supply an Archimedean small-gap argument: cuts can be approximated by an inside and outside rational separated by any prescribed positive rational tolerance. This needs the appropriate internal arithmetic/induction resources. No inverse/group/field claim has been inferred merely from density. Multiplication, positive real division and the remaining real-field laws are also unfinished.

## External analytic argument and finite algebra

The [canonical atomic note](../proof-refinements/canonical-atomic-probability.md) is reflected in the finite-support envelope modules. Its split/merge resource is explicit. Its complete-additivity theorem additionally assumes finite subjoins lie below the represented join and every finite atom support there is covered by a finite subjoin. The analytical supremum-of-finite-subsums proof is drafted; the admitted atom-support coding, atom-to-family-member lemma and finite-index image construction are not silently supplied by it.

The greatest-minorant claim is about **complete** additivity. It is not the greatest countably additive minorant on an arbitrary uncountable atom field. A proof that complete additivity yields the represented atomic expansion is still needed for that application. The unnormalized atomic part preserves exact atom weights and lies below the original charge. Normalization preserves ratios; it need not preserve those raw weights or domination. The normalization module assumes a positive additive order isomorphism taking the total to one. It does not construct division by an internal real.

The [finite-class average](../proof-refinements/canonical-finite-class-symmetrization.md) has a unique graph from existential finite enumerations, exact finite class-mass preservation and idempotence at the weight level. For saturated-event mass preservation the module still requires finite saturation/cofinality. For invariant normalized probabilities, the event action and complete atomic-sum interpretation remain necessary. Equal atom weights do not determine an arbitrary finitely additive charge on infinite events.

The [finite-fibre recipe](../proof-refinements/choice-free-faithful-probability.md) supplies a unique weight graph and finite-budget estimate relative to a supplied budget sequence. The estimate explicitly takes a function realizing the graph. The infinite geometric budget and the internal graph/operation realization remain obligations. A countable union of finite fields is not an injection into N without additional finite-choice assumptions. The conditional finite-pair-world example does not prove the existence or consistency of a choiceless metatheory.

## Axiom audit and ownership

The first integration report checked the original five algebra modules successfully as ordinary Lean. Envelope totality/additivity/residual and saturation comparison had no axioms. Enumeration-based average and finite-fibre results depended on `Classical.choice`, `propext` and `Quot.sound` through library finite lemmas. This report is not evidence for their choice-free metatheory.

A fresh constructor-based finite module is a targeted repair candidate, with no DecidableEq or standard counting/erase lemmas. It must itself compile and pass its axiom reports. The integrator may replace the old two primitive proofs while keeping their signatures, then rerun the larger axiom reports. Imported external finite proofs never become object-language C proofs just because their axiom list shrinks.

Supported-core operations belong to the world-representation author; Frege finite-cardinality realization and number-isomorphism interfaces to the infinity author. Atom coordinates/splitter and evaluator/uniform-half/ND-power modules belong to other lanes. This lane has preserved their files, frozen research notes, record YAML and generated statements.
