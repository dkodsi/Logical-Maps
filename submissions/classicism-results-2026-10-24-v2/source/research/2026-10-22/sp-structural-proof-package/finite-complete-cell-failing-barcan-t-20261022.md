# A finite pointed-complete cell failing propositional Barcan

22 October 2026. Original construction and proof by the construction research subagent, independently audited mathematically by the root and local-strengthening research subagents. No human check or Lean verification claimed. No repository records edited. Direct source for the new argument: misc, this note.

## 1. Exact statement and antecedent results

Work in the unchanged pure language and logic C. All types except e are relational; a relation type can be written sigma_1 ... sigma_k t. Let E_n^sigma assert exactly n entities of type sigma. Let ND_sigma be necessity of distinctness at that type. Put

    B := Box(E_1^e and E_4^t and ND_e and ND_t).

The finite rigid reconstruction in section 15 of profile-root-construction-20261022.md proves that B fixes the full finite typed hierarchy over a singleton individual set and the four-element propositional Boolean algebra. All transports in a B-cone are bijective, every type has crisp equality, every function into the propositional algebra is represented, and the actual truth ultrafilter is one of the two Boolean atoms. The automorphism group of the typed logical algebra WITHOUT a chosen actual-truth ultrafilter is the two-element group generated by swapping those atoms. This swap changes the chosen world's truth ultrafilter; it nevertheless preserves term denotations and all typed logical operations. It extends recursively to all types.

Define the single closed pure sentence

    A_swap := E_1^e and E_4^t and exists p^t [
      p and Atom(p) and Box(p)=bottom and Box(not p)=not p
      and Box(p -> (E_1^e and E_4^t))
      and Box(not p -> B)].                                  (1)

Here Atom(p) says p is a nonzero Boolean atom; p itself asserts its actual truth. This is finite and uses no nonlogical constants. The theorem is that C+A_swap is consistent and complete for pure sentences and for pure formulas with one individual parameter, while

    C proves A_swap -> not BF_t.

It also refutes Functionality at tt. No Extensionality claim is made.

## 2. Classification of arrows from an A_swap world

Fix a world r satisfying (1), and choose its true atom p. Write b=not p. The source propositional algebra is {bottom,p,b,top}, with Box(p)=bottom and Box(b)=b. Its actual truth ultrafilter is {p,top}.

At every target v of an arrow h:r→v, either h(p) is actually true or h(b) is actually true.

In the first case, the boxed clause in (1) gives singleton individuals and exactly four propositions at v. The image h(p) is nonzero because actually true. The image h(b) cannot be zero: that would make h(p)=top, contradicting Box(h(p))=h(Box(p))=bottom. Thus h is injective on the four-element propositional algebra and, by the target cardinality, is bijective. It preserves the chosen truth ultrafilter. Consequently EVERY formula with source parameters preserves its actual truth along h, since its proposition is transported by this truth-preserving isomorphism. Call such targets rootlike.

In the second case v satisfies B. At a B-world the only Box-fixed propositions are bottom and top: equality is crisp, so Box(q)=(q=top) is top precisely when q=top. Since h(b) is true and Box-fixed, h(b)=top and h(p)=bottom. Therefore the image of the entire source propositional algebra at every such target is exactly {bottom,top}. Call these targets leaflike. In particular, the truth value of a transported source proposition at any leaflike target is determined by whether its b-coordinate is 1, independently of the target arrow.

At least one leaflike target exists, since b is nonzero. Every reachable world is one of these two classes. No Barcan assumption has been used.

## 3. Naming the finite rigid target hierarchy with an atom

Fix a B-world, and call its two propositional atoms u and v=not u. Every entity of every relational type is denoted by a pure term T(u) with the sole parameter u. The unique individual may equivalently be added as an unused individual parameter if desired.

Proof is by finite type induction. The base propositions are bottom,u,not u,top. The individual sort is singleton. At a function type, enumerate all current inputs by the terms already obtained at relational input types, treating an individual input as its only possible case. Enumerate the desired output table in the same way. Crisp equality and finite lambda case distinctions represent every table. This gives an explicit finite term for every target entity.

Let s denote the atom-swap automorphism of this typed logical algebra, without requiring preservation of the chosen actual-truth ultrafilter. If F=T(u), equivariance of term denotations gives T(v)=s(F). Thus:

* If F is not fixed by s, exactly one atom z satisfies F=T(z).
* If F is fixed by s, both atoms z satisfy T(z)=F. In this case F has a parameter-free term name

      U_F := lambda x_bar, exists z^t [Atom(z) and T(z)[x_bar]].   (2)

  At type t the lambda prefix is empty. Atom(z) is crisp in the rigid hierarchy, and the two selected outputs are both F, so (2) denotes F.

These constructions require only finite terms. The existential operation in (2) is the ordinary Boolean join over the finite target domain, and uses no choice operation.

## 4. Every transported source profile is atom-swap fixed

Let f be a relational entity at r and h:r→v a leaflike arrow. Suppose F=h(f) were not fixed by the target atom-swap. Choose T(z) as in section 3. The source term

    Q(f) := exists z^t [Atom(z) and f=T(z) and z]              (3)

denotes a source proposition. At v, exactly one atom z satisfies F=T(z), and every other conjunct selecting z is crisp. Therefore h(Q(f)) equals that selected atom, rather than bottom or top. This contradicts section 2: every transported source proposition at a leaflike target lies in {bottom,top}. Hence h(f) is fixed.

Conversely every fixed target entity has the pure name U_F from (2), so it is the transport of the same pure term at r. All fixed target profiles therefore occur.

For uniformity, enumerate the finitely many fixed entities by pure terms U_1,...,U_l. Fix one leaflike arrow and suppose h(f)=U_i. The source proposition f=U_i is true at that leaflike target, so its b-coordinate is 1. It is therefore true at every leaflike target. Thus f has the SAME named fixed profile at all leaves, after identifying their canonical finite rigid algebras. The names may be redundant syntactically, but their denoted fixed entities are distinct and form a finite list.

## 5. Exact reconstruction of every source domain

Induct over types. The source individual domain is singleton. Suppose the source input domains of tau=sigma_1...sigma_k t have already been reconstructed as finite sets.

A tau-entity f is determined by two pieces of data:

1. its actual truth extension on the finite product of the source input domains;
2. its fixed target profile from section 4.

To prove this, suppose f and g agree on these data. The proposition expressing their actual coextension is true at r, and is therefore true at every rootlike target by section 2, even without presupposing surjectivity at the input types. At every leaflike target their profiles are identical, so they are coextensive there as well. Hence they are necessarily coextensive, and C's Intensionality yields f=g.

Every such pair of data is realized. Given an actual extension X, finitely enumerate its rows by source entities and define a lambda case table L_X that has actual truth extension X. Equality tests suffice for this actual table; they need not be rigid. Let U_F name the desired fixed leaf profile. Then

    lambda x_bar, [p and L_X(x_bar)] or [b and U_F(x_bar)]     (4)

has actual extension X, since p is actually true and b false, and has profile F at every leaf, since p becomes bottom and b top there. Thus

    D_tau(r) is exactly P(product_i D_sigma_i(r)) × Fix_s(D_tau(B)).

This establishes finiteness at the new type and finishes the induction. The p-world transports at every type are bijective: source equality truth is preserved there, so they are injective; finiteness and the preservation of the source exact-cardinality formula give equal cardinalities and therefore surjectivity.

Application is determined by taking fibers of actual extensions and applying fixed target profiles to fixed target argument profiles. Equality is the proposition with actual coordinate determined by equality of these pairs, and leaf coordinate determined by equality of their profiles. Boolean operations are pointwise. A quantifier's actual coordinate ranges over the reconstructed source domain, while its leaf coordinate ranges over the full target domain. The latter is the original B-quantifier, not a quantifier restricted to the fixed profiles. Lambda abstraction is computed similarly. Thus all logical operations and all pure term denotations are reconstructed, not merely the sizes of the domains.

The source actual truth map is its actual-extension bit at type t. Consequently all realizations of (1) have isomorphic local typed logical structures. C plus (1) decides every closed pure sentence. Since the individual set is singleton, it also decides every pure formula with one individual parameter. This is precisely the pointed completeness used by the profile-root atom criterion.

## 6. Explicit consistency model

Take the full finite rigid S5 component on two worlds and one individual, realizing B. Its typed domains and transports are identical at the two worlds, while their truth maps select the two different propositional atoms. Add a singleton-individual root r, with one arrow to each component world, composed with the S5 arrows in the evident way.

At every root relational type tau=sigma_1...sigma_k t set

    D_tau(r) := P(product_i D_sigma_i(r)) × Fix_s(D_tau(B)).

Actual truth is given by the extension, and transport to either old world is the fixed profile. Application takes fibers and applies the old profiles. Terms are closed: at an old target, a pure term with fixed-profile parameters has a fixed value by equivariance under the atom swap of the typed logical algebra, including when it contains a quantifier over the FULL old domain. This uses preservation of term denotations, not preservation of the chosen world's truth ultrafilter. The root actual extension is unrestricted. Equality and the classical clauses agree with these complete intensions, giving C in the standard action semantics.

The root has four propositions because the swap-fixed old propositions are precisely bottom and top. Let p be true only at the root and b true throughout the old component. They satisfy the four-element chain Box table. Every p-world is just the root, and every b-world belongs to the B-component, so (1) holds.

At tt the old full function space has 4^4=256 entities. Exactly 16 are swap-fixed: values at bottom and top each have two fixed choices; the value at one atom has four choices and determines the value at the other atom. Therefore the root tt-domain has 2^4 times 16 =256 entities as well. Its transport image nevertheless has size only 16. Equal finite domain sizes do not imply surjectivity.

## 7. Barcan and Functionality fail explicitly

At r use the predicate

    X(q) := p or q=bottom or q=top.

For every source proposition q, X(q) holds at every rootlike target because p is true, and at every leaflike target because q transports to bottom or top. Thus forall q Box(X(q)) holds. At a leaf there are two other propositions, its atoms, and X is false of them. Consequently Box(forall q X(q)) fails. This is an explicit counterexample to BF_t in every A_swap model.

For Functionality at tt, put

    f := lambda q, bottom,
    g := lambda q, b and q and not Box(q).

For every source q, g(q) has false actual coordinate because b is actually false, and false leaf coordinate because each source q becomes bottom or top. Hence g(q)=f(q) for every actual q. But at a leaf, g sends an unseen atom u to u, whereas f sends it to bottom. Therefore f!=g. This is failure of Functionality by entity-valued agreement, not a statement about Extensionality.

By the separately established pointed-complete-cell criterion, pure Max+Atom(A_swap) is consistent and pure Strong Possibility entails Atom(A_swap). The new cell refutes the proposed classification of all finitely axiomatized complete cells as satisfying base-type Barcan.

## 8. A complete cell failing both base-type Barcan sentences

Replace B in (1) by

    B' := Box(E_2^e and E_4^t and ND_e and ND_t),

and call the resulting pure sentence A_mix. All other clauses remain unchanged. This single change yields a consistent pointed-complete cell refuting both BF_e and BF_t.

The proof above extends with one precise modification to section 3. Use the root's unique individual a as a parameter. At a B'-leaf there are two individuals: h(a), and the unique individual satisfying x!=h(a). With one propositional atom u also supplied, every relational leaf entity has a term name T(a,u). The finite type induction uses x=a and x!=a as the two individual input cases, and finite equality cases on already named relational inputs. No term of type e naming the other individual is required. Let s fix both individuals and swap the two propositional atoms, extending recursively to the entire typed logical algebra. If F is not s-fixed, the two terms T(a,u) at the two atoms distinguish its orientation, so the selector (3), with the additional a parameter, again yields a forbidden nontrivial leaf proposition. Conversely every s-fixed entity is named by symmetrizing T(a,u) over the two atoms. The root supplies the a parameter, so all these names lift.

Profile uniformity and the exact domain reconstruction are unchanged. The finite rigid two-individual leaf with a distinguished individual is unique up to isomorphism, and the actual root is singleton; hence the resulting cell is pointed-complete. For existence, use the two-world rigid S5 leaf component with two individuals and a root whose one individual maps to a chosen leaf individual, taking arbitrary root actual extensions paired with s-fixed leaf profiles. The same equivariance proof gives term closure. The atom swap here fixes the individual set; it is not the additional automorphism that interchanges the two individuals.

The BF_t counterpredicate from section 7 still works. For BF_e use X(x):=(x=a). The sole current individual necessarily satisfies X, whereas a leaf has its second individual, so Box(forall x X(x)) is false. Thus A_mix entails both negated Barcan sentences.
