# Safe normalization of an actual partial transversal

22 October 2026. Coordinating mathematical observation, no record edit.

Given any relation R:σσt and property F:σt, define

N_R(F)(x) := Fx ∧ ∀y(Fy ∧ (Rxy ∨ Ryx) → y=x).

H proves, uniformly in R,F,

∀uv(N_R(F)(u) ∧ N_R(F)(v) ∧ Ruv → u=v).

Hence C proves its necessity: N_R(F) has necessarily universal pairwise safety, with the box outside the quantifiers. If R is actually an equivalence relation and F is actually a partial transversal, then N_R(F) is actually coextensive with F. Thus any actual transversal can be replaced by a necessarily safe one without additional assumptions.

This does NOT replace the BC step. Normalization need not preserve algebraic inclusion: N_R(F)≤F, but old F-members can be removed at a future world where F ceases to be a partial transversal. A material union of an actual algebraic chain may forget actual index membership later; normalizing it does not turn it into an algebraic upper bound. Nor does normalization identify codes of actually coextensive F and G. The stronger safety subclass need not be closed under the available algebraic chain joins, whose audit only gives the pointwise-boxed ∀uv□ condition.

The observation is a harmless strengthening of the actual-transversal conclusion, not a proof of Relational Choice⇒Transversal Choice alone.
