**Standard numbers.** For a fixed theory enumerable in a hyperarithmetical oracle, containing the arithmetic premise I_sigma, **if a standard-N_sigma model exists**, every adequate standard code computes each HYP real, yet some standard codes are strictly below Kleene O. Exactly HYP is common information; there is no least solution degree. Standard existence is established for the specified B/F Infinity packages and original Max; it remains unproved for SP and higher stages. I_sigma is Diamond I*_sigma, not quantified Infinity of the counting domain. With I_e and externally standard N_e, finite-condition satisfiability for B/F and Max is Sigma-1-1 complete; Max's pure identity fragment nevertheless remains degree 0′.

**Stronger bare-code bound.** Possible weak Infinity alone already forces ordinary PA degree from application/equality, without Actuality, using finite trace propositions and the nonbottom test. Bare Max codes compute 0′, and their common information is exactly the sets below 0′. Whether a 0′-computable bare Max presentation exists remains unresolved. Do not transfer the PA-over-0′ bound without actual valuation.

**Finite effective complete theories.** Every consistent c.e. pure-complete extension of C fixes finite actual domains at every type, with recursive exact sizes and recursive necessary bounds. It determines a unique actual typed structure, including term interpretation, and that structure has a computable adequate copy. Necessary Actuality, Atomicity, Boolean Completeness and all Relational Choice instances follow. A finite coalesced model shows that C5 and even current FC_(t,t) need not follow. This closes the local-finiteness question in the profile note §18.

**Concrete positive separations.** The moving-world finite-support model satisfies B, both boxed quantified Infinity axioms, standard numbers and full ordinary core Choice, while every nontrivial unary ambient FC instance fails necessarily. The moving-individual/fixed-two-world variant satisfies B, boxed individual Infinity, standard N_e, all unary e-input FC instances and every FC instance with a t-only input or output type, but fails FC_(et,et). Its individual domain is internally amorphous and lacks numerical coding despite external countability.
