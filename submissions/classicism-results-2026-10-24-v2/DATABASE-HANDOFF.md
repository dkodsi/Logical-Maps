# Review guide for the database owners

Start with [Main Results](MAIN-RESULTS.md), [Candidates_DK](CANDIDATES_DK.md) and the [integration table](DATABASE-INTEGRATION.md). Each detailed result links its mathematical proof and exact certificate scope. Catalogue keys identify claims in this package; they are not official database IDs or counts of independent theorems.

Review the [database connections and existing-result formalizations](DATABASE-INTEGRATION.md#database-connections), then the [further results about established principles](DATABASE-INTEGRATION.md#established-principles) and [extensions and new directions](DATABASE-INTEGRATION.md#extensions-and-new-directions).

Preserve the fixed framework C, its relational type grammar, and the theoremhood restrictions on the schemata. Distinguish unprefixed and ☐ premises, pure and named signatures, propositional and all-type instances, and conditional results. Independence belongs in model records. Retain original attribution and published theorem/page references; new connecting arguments use `misc`.

The CH countermodel and Max–CH incompatibility are Lean certified with constructed opposite-CH components at type e in Lean’s ambient metatheory. The separate first-order transfer retains its explicit satisfiability and type guards. The result index distinguishes these from the further rigidity and well-ordering conclusions.

A successful helper, a matched database record or a certificate for one component does not certify every claim in the same family. Use the [certificate index](evidence/CERTIFICATES.json) and [reproduction instructions](REPRODUCING.md) to check exact sources and declarations. Database `lean: verified` flags require the database’s own verification workflow.
