# Review guide for the database owners

[Cumulative integration register](../integration-register/README.md) · [25 October update](../next-update/README.md) · [Changes since this post and database integration guide](../next-update/DATABASE-HANDOFF.md).

[Main Results](MAIN-RESULTS.md) gives the principal results in three groups: database connections, further results about established principles, and extensions. [Conjectures](CONJECTURES.md) separates unresolved questions and claims awaiting review from the resolved database questions, grouped by family. It distinguishes new proofs from formalizations of existing results.

[Candidates_DK](CANDIDATES_DK.md) presents proposed principles and their reasons for inclusion. The [logical-observer guide](LOGICAL-OBSERVERS.md) gives its C and H maps, common object-language definitions, and simplifications over C/C5. The [structured observer register](OBSERVER-PRINCIPLES.json) supplies the proposed formulas; the [full connection catalogue](proofs/h-observer-space/SUMMARY.md) retains the supporting comparisons.

The [integration table](DATABASE-INTEGRATION.md) matches the results to existing database records and proposed additions. The [full result index](evidence/RESULT-INDEX.md) supplies statements, attribution, proofs and exact Lean-certificate scope; the [catalogue](CATALOG.md) retains supporting results. Catalogue keys identify claims in this package, rather than official database records or independent theorem counts.

The [certificate index](evidence/CERTIFICATES.json) and [reproduction instructions](REPRODUCING.md) provide the saved checks and source files. Research certificates and the database’s own verification status are recorded separately.
