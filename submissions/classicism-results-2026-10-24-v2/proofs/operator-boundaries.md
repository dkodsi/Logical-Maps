# Universal-operation observers and BF

For a finite type list $`\Delta`$, let $`S_\Delta`$ assert proposition separation by admitted observers preserving top and meet and satisfying the universal composite identities at each tested type. Over C,

```math
\Box\mathrm{BF}_\Delta\ \Longrightarrow\ S_\Delta\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathrm{BF}_\Delta
```

For the right implication, if every $`Fx=\top`$, the universal operation identity makes every qualified observer accept $`\forall x\, .\,Fx`$. Separation from top gives its necessity.

For the left implication, use $`P_a(p)=\Box(a\to p)`$. Its top and meet composite identities are unconditional C identities. Necessitate the unconditional C theorem that BF implies truth agreement for its universal-quantifier calculation; necessary BF and Intensionality then give the universal composite identity. The tests $`P_p`$ and $`P_q`$ distinguish $`p,q`$ whenever they differ: if both agreed on the pair in truth, they would give $`p\le q`$ and $`q\le p`$.

No equivalence of these two bounds is asserted. These observers need not preserve negation or bottom. For the Boolean qualification and the full typed principle, see [Logical Observers](logical-observers.md).

Source: Codex, ordinary mathematical argument, `misc`, unpaginated. The C modal identities are from Bacon–Dorr, *Classicism*, §§1.3–2.1.
