# Maximalism and logical observers

OpenAI Codex, 26 October 2026 (`misc`), §§1–4, unpaginated. The maximalization argument uses the [existing interpretation-root construction](../../integration-register/proofs/complete-henkin-interpretation-cover.md#8-arbitrary-c-targets-and-pure-maximalism); it is an ordinary mathematical proof.

## 1. Fixed reference theory

Work in the pure finitary relational language. For an H-theory T containing C, let Max(T) be the H-theory generated by T together with

```math
A\ne B\qquad(A=B\text{ closed and pure};\ T\nvdash A=B).
```

Max(C) therefore includes C. Its added axioms are not automatically closed under theorem necessitation. OS retains full relational separation and each finite list of whole-operation qualification laws.

## 2. Adding OS preserves the maximalization schema

The interpretation root in [§§8.1 and 8.3 of the cover theorem](../../integration-register/proofs/complete-henkin-interpretation-cover.md#81-the-isolated-interpretation-root) satisfies Max(C) + OS. Its components witness every C-consistent closed pure sentence. The root preserves those components and reaches each of them, so every such sentence is possible there. The full typed OS verification uses admitted observers whose transported operations are identity at the old objects; surjective interpretations supply quantifier preservation and separation at the root. The construction is in ZFC, with the action-model existence input stated in that proof.

Suppose C + OS proved a closed pure identity A = B that C did not prove. This root would satisfy A = B because it satisfies C + OS. It would also satisfy A ≠ B by Max(C). Consequently C + OS has exactly the same closed pure identity theorems as C. The two maximalization schemas are identical, and hence

```math
\mathrm{Max}(C+OS)=\mathrm{Max}(C)+OS.
```

OS can supply new truths, including BF, while leaving closed pure identities unchanged. The stronger [positive-equational conservation theorem](../../integration-register/proofs/observer-positive-conservativity.txt) also allows ∧, ∨, ∀ and ∃ outside the identities, arbitrary terms and signatures, and a background theory built in the same way from disequalities. It permits the entire addition OS + Strong Actuality + Zorn. It does not assert conservativity for unrestricted sentences.

## 3. Necessary separation is excluded

If a closed pure A is not a theorem of C, neither is the identity A = Top, since C proves that this identity implies A. Max(C) therefore supplies

```math
A\ne\top,\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad\neg\Box A.
```

Choose a closed pure BF instance B that is not a C theorem. The proved OS-to-BF implication uses finitely many OS instances. Necessitate its discharged C proof and apply the normal modal laws: ☐OS implies ☐B. This contradicts Max(C). No optional assumption has itself been necessitated.

The other exclusions follow from the proved observer connections:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Rev}&\longrightarrow\Box OS,\\
\mathrm{End}&\longrightarrow\mathrm{Rev},\\
\mathrm{BoxSep}&\longrightarrow\Box BF.
\end{aligned}
```

Every boxed stronger separation principle also implies ☐OS. See the [observer hierarchy](logical-observer-hierarchy.md), [necessity-preserving observers](h-observer-space/native-box-logical-observers.md), and the [map over C](../LOGICAL-OBSERVERS.md#map-over-c).

## 4. Compatible choice and Actuality variants

The [two interpretation-root constructions](../../integration-register/proofs/complete-henkin-interpretation-cover.md#83-the-exact-maximalism-corollaries) give models of

```math
\mathrm{Max}(C)+OS+\mathrm{Relational\ Choice}+\mathrm{Zorn}
```

with Actuality and with its negation. Relational Choice and Zorn have their all-type scope. These are simultaneous compatibility witnesses, not consequences of OS. The addition of Boolean Completeness at type t remains unresolved; all-type Boolean Completeness is already excluded by the database path above.

The original typed-name, landing and component-preservation construction is attributed to Cian Dorr, 19 October 2026, in the [coalesced finitely pinned interpretation-root write-up](../source/topics/classicism/writeups/coalesced-pinned-interpretations-root.md). The full OS and permutation-root extensions are attributed to OpenAI Codex in the cover theorem. The new deduction in §2 uses that existing model to establish closed pure identity conservativity and the maximalization equality.

For closed additional axioms Γ, Equivalence closure of C + Γ is exactly Cn_H(C + ☐Γ). Relative Max(C + ☐OS) is consistent, while Max(C) + ☐OS is inconsistent. Closing the finished maximalizations under Equivalence makes them inconsistent. [Proof and reference-theory distinction](../../integration-register/proofs/equivalence-and-maximalism.txt), §§1–5.
