# Ternary Cantor symmetry: separate connective–quantifier observers

By OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 24 October 2026. Direct source: `misc`, this note, §§1–9, unpaginated.

There is a model of C in which conjunction, disjunction and negation each have enough fully quantifier-preserving observers to separate every relational type, while Boolean observers do not even separate propositions. All these positive witnesses fix Top and Bottom as objects. Every Barcan and Functionality instance, and its boxed version, holds.

The three positive separation theories hold **together**, with different permitted witnesses. They do not give separation by observers satisfying the three connective qualifications together. In particular this supplies separation-theory countermodels to the unrestricted N+Q and V+Q reductions over C; it is not merely a non-Boolean example of one qualified observer.

## 1. Exact conclusion and qualification scope

Use M, V and N for the full composite identities preserving primitive conjunction, disjunction and negation. Q_Δ and P_Γ require the full universal and existential composite identities at the finite external type lists Δ and Γ. Put

```math
\mathrm{Fix}_{01}(E):=(E\top=\top)\land(E\bot=\bot).
```

Write S_τ[K] for separation at relational output type τ by material agreement after all observers satisfying K, as in [Typed separation and Classicism](typed-separation-and-classicism.md), §1. At the model's root, for each K₀ in the **separate list** M, V, N, every relational τ and every finite Δ,Γ,

```math
S_\tau[\mathrm{Fix}_{01}\land K_0\land Q_\Delta\land P_\Gamma]
\tag{1}
```

holds. Every separately boxed instance of (1) holds as well. Nevertheless,

```math
\neg S_t[\mathrm{Bool}],
\tag{2}
```

where Bool requires the three full Boolean operation identities. Indeed Boolean separation fails even for its larger class with no quantifier or endpoint qualification, and therefore fails after adding any finite Q/P qualification. The failure is also a failure of full ordinary logical separation OS.

The model additionally satisfies the full schemas BF, □BF, Functionality and □Functionality. No C5 or modal .2 premise is asserted; both fail at the root. The positive qualifications in (1) contain no additional equality-preservation clauses. Over C the translated equality clauses for fully Boolean observers are already redundant, so (2) refutes the usual complete logical qualification too.

All laws below are full operation identities, not only their current material or pointwise versions. The type lists are finite and external throughout. A semantic witness may meet every quantifier instance without placing an infinite conjunction in an object-language qualification.

## 2. The clopen space and its symmetry group

Let W={0,1,2}^ℕ with its product topology and uniform product probability measure μ. Let B be its Boolean algebra of clopen subsets. Every clopen set is a finite union of basic cylinders of a common length, so its measure is an integer divided by a power of three. In particular a basic cylinder c of length n has measure 3^(-n), and no clopen subset of c has measure μ(c)/2.

Let G be the group of all measure-preserving homeomorphisms of W. A finite family of clopens determines its pointwise set stabilizer

```math
G_{(\mathcal P)}:=\{g\in G: gP=P\text{ for every }P\in\mathcal P\}.
```

Equivalently one can use the finite Boolean partition generated by the family. These are stabilizers of clopen **sets**, not stabilizers of individual points of W. This distinction is essential: refining a finite partition never isolates a point.

We use G only to restrict admitted root truth tables. It is an external symmetry group for the construction, not a type of admitted individual maps and not a collection of additional accessibility arrows.

The frame has one root r and one terminal leaf for each w∈W. Its arrows are the identity arrows and a single arrow r→w for each leaf. There are no arrows between distinct leaves or from a leaf to the root.

## 3. All typed domains and application

At each leaf use the same full classical finite hierarchy L_σ: L_e is a singleton, L_t={0,1}, and L_(στ) is the set of all functions L_σ→L_τ. Only the fixed relational types are formed: τ≠e in an arrow type. Every L_σ is finite and nonempty.

Define root domains D_σ recursively on type complexity. Put D_e=L_e. If ρ is the relational type σ₁…σₙt, a root ρ-object is a pair

```math
a=(a_0,a_1),\qquad
a_0:\prod_{i=1}^nD_{\sigma_i}\longrightarrow\{0,1\},
\qquad a_1:W\longrightarrow L_\rho,
\tag{3}
```

subject to two requirements:

1. a₁ is continuous, where the finite set L_ρ is discrete.
2. The current truth table a₀ is invariant under the simultaneous action of G_(𝒫) on its arguments, for some finite clopen family 𝒫.

The action on the lower argument domains is already defined by the recursion. On the pair in (3), define

```math
(g\cdot a)_0(\bar x)=a_0(g^{-1}\cdot\bar x),
\qquad
(g\cdot a)_1(w)=a_1(g^{-1}w).
\tag{4}
```

It is the identity on D_e. This action preserves the domain: it transports a finite supporting family to another finite clopen family, and preserves continuity. A continuous finite-valued profile a₁ is itself fixed by the stabilizer of its finite fiber partition. Thus every pair has a single finite clopen family supporting both components, after taking the union of two families.

When n=0, a₀ is just a truth bit and a₁ is a clopen characteristic function. Consequently

```math
D_t=\{0,1\}\times B.
\tag{5}
```

The actual truth of a proposition at the root is its first coordinate. Its truth at the leaf w is membership of w in its second coordinate. The action on its first coordinate is trivial.

Application takes a current truth-table section and applies leaf values pointwise. Explicitly, for a of type σρ and x∈D_σ,

```math
(ax)_0(\bar y)=a_0(x,\bar y),
\qquad
(ax)_1(w)=a_1(w)(x_1(w)).
\tag{6}
```

For type e the second-coordinate notation means the unique leaf individual. A supporting family for a and x supports the first component in (6); the second is continuous between finite discrete values. Application is therefore admitted and equivariant.

The transport along r→w is evaluation of the profile:

```math
\pi_{w,\sigma}(a)=a_1(w),
\tag{7}
```

with the singleton map at e. These transports respect application. They are onto at every type. More strongly, **every continuous profile** s:W→L_σ lifts to a root σ-object: for a relational σ choose s as the profile and choose the constant-false current truth table. For e there is only the unique profile. This continuous-profile lifting fact is used in the quantifier proof below.

These are genuine Henkin domains. We do not admit every current truth function. The finite-support condition on the first component is a substantive restriction, and will exclude the Boolean truth functions that could otherwise separate the clopen coordinate.

## 4. Logical constants, lambda closure, and C

Interpret each logical constant by its ordinary current truth operation on the root domains and by its classical operation in the leaf hierarchy. Boolean operations act coordinatewise on (5). In particular Top=(1,W) and Bottom=(0,∅), including for the fixed H abbreviations.

Equality at each type has current truth equal to **actual equality of the admitted objects**, including both components of (3). At a leaf it is actual equality in L_σ. Thus the equality proposition of root objects a,b has the current equality bit and the profile w↦[a₁(w)=b₁(w)]. The equality logical constant is admitted: actual equality is invariant under the action (4), and its leaf logical-operation profile is constant. Equality of individual objects is handled by the singleton domain.

For F∈D_(σt), quantifiers are interpreted by

```math
\begin{aligned}
(\forall x^\sigma Fx)_0&=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall x\in D_\sigma\ F_0(x)=1,\\
(\forall x^\sigma Fx)_1(w)&=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall z\in L_\sigma\ F_1(w)(z)=1,
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
```

and the analogous existential clauses. The quantifier constant's current table is invariant under G because G permutes each admitted domain and acts equivariantly on application. Its profile is the constant classical leaf quantifier. Thus both quantifier constants are admitted at every type. The resulting profiles in (8) are continuous since F₁ is finite-valued and continuous.

Lambda abstraction has two independently specified components. The current table of λx.A is obtained by evaluating A under the actual root argument and root assignment. Its profile at w is the classical lambda abstraction evaluated under the leaf images of the free root parameters. These definitions satisfy (6), so beta holds exactly.

To check admission, take the union of finite clopen supports of the finitely many free parameters. Logical constants are equivariant. Induction on the term makes its current interpretation equivariant in all inputs, so the abstraction's current table is fixed by the stabilizer of that union. The leaf interpretation depends on only finitely many free parameter profiles, each continuous into a finite set. It is consequently continuous into the finite leaf function domain. This proves lambda closure, including arbitrary relational binders. Eta holds because the current tables and leaf functions are actual functions. Contextual beta and eta identities therefore hold as object identities.

These clauses verify H. Propositional truth is classical at each node; quantifier truth ranges over that node's admitted nonempty domain; reflexivity and Leibniz substitution use actual equality; and lambda conversion is exact. The usual side conditions on the quantifier inference rules are respected. No rule deriving abstraction identity from an optional current coextension is presumed.

They also verify C's **unconditional H Logical Equivalence** schema. Suppose H proves A↔B. Its truth equivalence holds at the root for every admitted assignment, so the root truth tables of any abstractions of A and B agree on every root argument tuple. It holds in each classical leaf under all assignments as well, so the leaf abstractions have equal truth tables and hence are the same full classical functions. The two components of the root abstractions are therefore equal. This proves exactly the required abstraction identity. It does not convert an equivalence under an additional premise into a C identity.

No arbitrary theorem-to-abstraction rule is needed beyond this verified schema. If a signature expansion is desired, assign each relational constant the top relation and each individual constant the unique individual; the argument remains valid.

## 5. Functionality, Barcan, and their boxed forms

The model has Functionality at every node. At a leaf it is ordinary full-function extensionality. At the root, suppose Xx=Yx for every root x of type σ. Their current tables then agree on every full root tuple. For a leaf w and arbitrary z∈L_σ, choose a root x with x₁(w)=z using (7). Equality of Xx and Yx gives equality of X₁(w)(z) and Y₁(w)(z). Full function extensionality in L therefore gives X₁(w)=Y₁(w). This holds at every w, so both components of X,Y agree. The same argument covers every relational codomain.

For a root proposition p=(ε,b), the formula □p, defined by p=Top, is true at the root exactly when ε=1 and b=W. At a leaf its truth is just that of p. Thus it has the expected reading “true at this node and every accessible leaf.” In particular, a closed formula true at the root and all leaves is the proposition Top and has a true box at the root.

Fix any type σ. If ∀x^σ□Fx is true at the root, then every root value Fx is Top. The root bit of ∀x Fx is therefore 1. At each leaf w, the transport (7) is onto, so the leaf predicate F₁(w) is true of every element of L_σ. Formula (8) gives profile W. Hence ∀x Fx=Top, proving BF_σ at the root. At the leaves BF is classical and immediate. Therefore the universally closed BF_σ formula is true at every node, and □BF_σ also holds at the root. This works at every type and proves the full BF and □BF schemas.

Likewise every universally closed Functionality instance is true at every node, so its boxed instance holds. No inference necessitating an optional assumption is used: these are direct semantic verifications at all nodes.

## 6. Monotone tests preserve every quantifier

Let h:B→{0,1} be monotone for inclusion and invariant under G_(𝒫) for some finite clopen family 𝒫. Define the root observer E_h by

```math
(E_h)_0(\varepsilon,b)=h(b),
\qquad
(E_h)_1(w)=\mathrm{id}_{\{0,1\}}.
\tag{9}
```

It is admitted by its stated support and its constant leaf profile. Its action on root propositions is

```math
E_h(\varepsilon,b)=(h(b),b).
\tag{10}
```

It ignores the input's current bit, but retains its entire clopen profile.

**Quantifier lemma.** Every E_h in (9) satisfies the full Q_σ and P_σ identities for every type σ.

Fix an admitted F∈D_(σt). Its leaf profile is a continuous map into the finite set of predicates L_σ→{0,1}. Choose a fixed enumeration of the finite nonempty set L_σ. On each fiber of F₁, choose the first argument minimizing that predicate's truth value. This gives a continuous finite-valued profile s_min:W→L_σ: the chosen argument is constant on each clopen fiber of F₁. By the lifting fact after (7), there is an admitted root argument x_min with this profile. Its current table may be chosen constant false when σ is relational; no requirement is imposed on the root truth F₀(x_min).

Put $`b_*=\{w:\forall z\in L_\sigma\ F_1(w)(z)=1\}`$, the profile of ∀x Fx. For every root x let b_x be the profile of Fx. Then

```math
b_*\subseteq b_x\quad\text{for every }x,
\qquad b_{x_{\min}}=b_*.
\tag{11}
```

Monotonicity and the attained minimum give

```math
h(b_*)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall x\in D_\sigma\ h(b_x)=1.
\tag{12}
```

This is the equality of the root truth coordinates of E_h(∀x Fx) and ∀x E_h(Fx). Their leaf profiles agree as objects because E_h acts as identity at every leaf. Thus those two root propositions are equal, for every admitted F.

More directly, the two whole-operation lambda terms have identical current truth tables by (12), and both have the same classical universal-quantifier profile at each leaf. They are therefore equal as objects in D_((σt)t). This proves full Q_σ, without promoting a current material law through Logical Equivalence.

For P_σ choose a continuous maximizing argument profile instead. It attains $`b^*=\{w:\exists z\ F_1(w)(z)=1\}`$, contains every b_x, and yields h(b^*)=1 iff some h(b_x)=1. The same two-component argument proves the full existential-operation identity. The singleton individual type is included: its minimizing and maximizing argument is unique.

The proof uses a finite classical leaf domain for each fixed σ, not a uniform finite bound over types. It does not select a root witness for an arbitrary root existential relation and does not assert Plenitude at the root.

## 7. Three different separating observer families

For any nonempty basic cylinder c, define three tests on B:

```math
\begin{aligned}
h_c^M(b)&=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad c\subseteq b,\\
h_c^V(b)&=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad c\cap b\ne\varnothing,\\
h_c^N(b)&=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mu(c\cap b)>\tfrac12\mu(c).
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
```

All three are monotone, fixed by the clopen-set stabilizer G_({c}), send ∅ to 0 and W to 1. Their observers are admitted, fix both endpoint objects by (10), and satisfy every Q/P identity by §6.

The first test preserves binary intersection and the second preserves binary union. Their observers consequently satisfy full M and full V respectively: the current tables give those identities, and their leaf components are identity. The third test is selfdual:

```math
h_c^N(W\setminus b)=1-h_c^N(b).
\tag{14}
```

Here the fact that c is a **basic cylinder** matters. Its measure is 3^(-n), whereas μ(c∩b) has denominator a power of three, so equality with μ(c)/2 is impossible. The two complementary strict-majority cases exhaust the possibilities. Equation (14) gives the full N identity by the same current-table and leaf-profile check. No majority claim is made for an arbitrary clopen c whose measure could permit a tie.

Each family separates the clopen part of propositions. If b≠b′, one of their differences contains a nonempty basic cylinder c. On c one profile is everywhere 1 and the other everywhere 0. Each corresponding test in (13) therefore distinguishes them. If two propositions have the same clopen profile but different current bits, the identity observer distinguishes them. Identity satisfies all the qualifications being considered.

We have proved propositional separation for each separate M, V and N family, with endpoint fixing and all finite Q/P lists. Functionality from §5 lifts each to every relational output type by the typed factorization theorem. Equivalently, different current relation tables provide a root argument tuple with different output bits, while different leaf profiles provide a leaf argument tuple that can be lifted to root arguments; either way the proposition-level separator applies.

At every terminal classical leaf, identity alone separates every relational type and satisfies all these laws. Thus each universally closed instance (1) is true at the root and every leaf. Its boxed instance is consequently true as well. The witnesses for different connective families need not coincide.

## 8. Why no Boolean observer can separate the clopen coordinate

Let E be any root observer satisfying all three Boolean composite identities. Its current truth table

```math
f_E:\{0,1\}\times B\longrightarrow\{0,1\}
```

preserves the Boolean operations. It therefore preserves the Boolean bounds, even if endpoint fixing was not separately included. Its leaf profile must be identity: the only Boolean homomorphism from the two-element Boolean algebra to itself is identity.

Let a=(1,∅), the root atom of the product Boolean algebra. If f_E(a)=1, every proposition (ε,b) has f_E-value ε. Indeed a lies below exactly the propositions whose first coordinate is 1; the complement of a has f_E-value 0, and the product decomposition determines every other value. Thus f_E is the root truth reader.

If f_E(a)=0, it factors through a Boolean homomorphism u:B→{0,1}. The set $`U=\{b:u(b)=1\}`$ is an ultrafilter. Admission of E gives a finite supporting clopen family for f_E, hence for U. We now show that no such supported ultrafilter exists.

Refine the support family to its finite partition into nonempty clopen cells. Exactly one cell C belongs to U. Express C as a union of cylinders of one length n. In each of these cylinders, split according to the next ternary digit. Let C₀,C₁,C₂ be the unions of the corresponding digit-0, digit-1 and digit-2 children. They form a partition of C into three nonempty clopens of equal measure.

There is an explicit measure-preserving homeomorphism g which cyclically changes that next digit inside each length-n cylinder belonging to C, and is identity outside C. It cyclically permutes C₀,C₁,C₂ and fixes every original support cell setwise. Invariance of U under g makes the three membership assertions C_i∈U have the same truth value. But an ultrafilter containing their union C contains exactly one of the three disjoint pieces. This is a contradiction.

Therefore every admitted Boolean observer has the root truth-reader table and identity leaf profile. It is the identity observer as a root object. In particular, all Boolean observers agree in truth on any two propositions with the same first coordinate. For a nonempty clopen b, the distinct propositions (0,b) and Bottom=(0,∅) are such a pair. This proves (2).

The identity observer is present and satisfies every logical qualification, so the failure is not an absence of qualified observers. Adding quantifier laws only restricts the already nonseparating Boolean class. Consequently every finite propositional Bool+Q/P separation instance fails on the same pair.

## 9. Scope, comparison, and sources

The model establishes genuine theory nonimplications over C, even after adjoining all boxed BF and Functionality instances:

```math
\begin{gathered}
\text{full endpoint-fixing }N+Q/P\text{ separation}
\ \not\Longrightarrow\ \text{Boolean separation},\\
\text{full endpoint-fixing }V+Q/P\text{ separation}
\ \not\Longrightarrow\ \text{Boolean separation}.
\end{gathered}
```

Full endpoint-fixing M+Q/P separation also holds, simultaneously with both displayed premises. Hence their conjunction still does not give separation by the common Boolean qualification. The conclusions refer to conjunctions of **separation theories**, not to adding N and V to the same individual observer; a single observer preserving both negation and disjunction is Boolean over C.

This does not conflict with the positive reductions under modal .2 in [Modal selectors and quantifier reduction](../modal-selectors-and-quantifier-reduction.md). The present frame has incompatible leaves. For a proper nonempty clopen b, a proposition with profile b is possibly necessary at the root but is not necessarily possible, since it is false at every point of the complementary leaves. Thus .2 fails. C5 fails as well: distinct root propositions can have the same image at a leaf. Necessary BF does not repair this obstruction.

The construction proves only the stated failures and satisfied schemas. It does not classify all mixed observer qualifications, prove a converse to any additional admission condition, or assert that the three positive individual observer classes coincide.
