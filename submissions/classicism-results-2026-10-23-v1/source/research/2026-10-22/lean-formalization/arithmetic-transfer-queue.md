   unions, functional relational images, finite-fibre unions and finite
   separating families. This supplies actual constructions needed by rows 1–2
   and the profile/cardinality results; it is not the completed classification.
2. **Arithmetic noncollapse:** bottom-cardinality criterion, successor collision,
   possible realization, finite numerical domain versus arithmetic Infinity,
   and actual-realization equivalence. Keep quantified Infinity distinct from
   the external finite-distinctness schema and from possible realization.
3. **Cross-base graphs:** synchronized successor graph, unfolding, totality,
   functionality under source noncollapse, surjectivity, bijection under both
   noncollapse hypotheses, and arithmetic-graph preservation. Do not select an
   operation. The Max author owns the predecessor/operation obstruction.
4. **All finite-sort generated algebras:** implement a uniform finite-signature
   proof construction with arbitrary relational source/target sorts, arbitrary
   finite lists of generators and constructor arities, simultaneous induction,
   persistent carriers, rigid images, onto maps and the inverse laws. The prior
   one-sort/unary/binary example is not this theorem. Distinguish a kernel-checked
   instance generator from a universally certified deep-syntax metatheorem.
5. **Concrete typed/profile consequences:** use the finite calculus to prove
   profile separation/counting and genuine type propagation, then connect the
   world lane's actual/Gallin domains. Do not replace a recursive interpretation
   or model construction with assumptions asserting its conclusion.
