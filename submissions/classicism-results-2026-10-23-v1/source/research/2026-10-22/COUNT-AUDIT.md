An equivalence counts once, rather than once per direction. Closely related type instances, corollaries and construction variants are grouped. Repeated proof versions, audits, summaries and copies are not additional results. A model classification and its examples can form one theorem family; a construction receiving several new verdicts can form one model family. The numbered lists identify the actual counting units and proof routes.

The arithmetic/profile/complexity row includes seven SP-relative or iteration-conditional families; the further SP row includes two SP-relative consequence families. Other results retain their explicit consistency, standard-model, metatheoretic and coding assumptions. The world/algebra row includes one paired BPI-factor separation whose reverse direction uses a published ZFA model as an input to the typed transfer. Neither direction is presented as an independently reconstructed published construction.

These are written ordinary mathematical arguments and model constructions. The count does not assert human review, Lean verification, or novelty in the literature. It excludes unresolved conjectures, failed speculative constructions, and unchanged published baseline results. In particular, it includes no proof that Maximalist Classicism is inconsistent and no proof of the consistency or inconsistency of Strong Possibility. Essentiality is a sufficient SP construction criterion; its necessity remains unproved.

The earlier **36-entry catalog** groups the work more broadly; it was not an exhaustive theorem count. The **136 archived notes** count files, including audits and overlapping versions. Neither number is comparable to the 134 deduplicated result families. The main database has not been updated with these research results.
