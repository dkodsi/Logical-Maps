## RC + BC ⇒ Actuality: novelty and audit

The proposed theorem is the local, unboxed

    C + RC_(t,t) + BC_(tt) ⇒ Actuality.

It appears new relative to the map and the sources found, but priority is unconfirmed. The targeted literature search found no prior proof. Classicism §2.3, p. 32, provides contextual evidence that this interaction was not known there; the exact source and retrieval limits are recorded in the [novelty and proof audit](choice-completeness-actuality-audit.md). That audit found no gap in the canonical-chain lemma, represented increment family, or spanning argument. It makes explicit why an interval supremum is also an ambient Boolean supremum.

The original proposed proof is [here](choice-completeness-actuality.md). It has not had an independent review.

## RC alone ⇒ TC: progress, still unresolved

[Exact history limits](choice-compatible-history-limits.md) proves that partial selector graphs can reach exactly the union of compatible earlier terminal partial transversals, eliminating fallback-driven extra progress. Sections 7–8 strengthen this to a represented chain-complete preorder of partial history graphs, with actual union as the least upper bound, and an RC-derived strict-unless-full progressive operation.

The missing condition is successor congruence for distinct codes of the **same actual entire history graph**. Different histories with merely the same terminal partial transversal may remain incomparable. A finite-ambiguity condition at the history-code level would supply the missing congruence and close the proof; it is not derived from RC. An explicit full two-world example shows why arbitrary RC witnesses need not have finite ambiguity.

## Concrete checks and countermodel fragments

[Propositional symmetry obstruction](choice-propositional-symmetry-obstruction.md) directly refutes RC_(t,t) in the existing symmetric full-BC surjection model. It uses the pure serial relation W(b,x) selecting an actual falsehood not below b. Support compression yields a greatest false proposition in each stabilizer-fixed Boolean subalgebra, making an equivariant selector impossible. This is a different check of the proposed Actuality theorem's prediction; it does not depend on that theorem or on the canonical-chain lemma.

[Paired-atoms fragment model](choice-paired-atoms-type-fragment.md) gives a concrete full Henkin tower of C in ZFC satisfying choice from every finite tuple of individuals to every output type, and choice from arbitrary inputs to purely propositional output types. TC_e fails for the partition into pairs. RC_(et,e) fails explicitly, so this is **not** a separator for full RC⇒TC. The arbitrary-output claim uses finite input-orbit analysis and induction on the number of unanchored input pairs.
