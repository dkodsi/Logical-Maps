# Consolidated result catalog

22 October 2026. This is a research index, not a set of database records. The linked files contain the arguments and their audit qualifications. Statements are ordinary mathematical results or model separations; no literature-novelty, human-referee, or Lean-verification claim is made. Some entries combine several closely related results rather than count them separately.

Throughout, C is the fixed relational-type framework, B = C + □Plenitude, and F = C + □Functional Choice. “Internally” means with the represented objects, families and functions of the model. Bare RC is avoided below because the notes use it for both Relational Choice and Rigid Comprehension.

The principal unresolved questions remain consistency of pure/signature Strong Possibility, Max + Atomicity, Relational Choice alone ⇒ Transversal Choice, and full TR + RP ⇒ WRP. Original finitary Max is consistent; the session did not prove it equal to falsity. Positive-model constructions establish relative consistency and useful mathematical structure, not logical validity of all their axioms.
