# Model interpretation counting audit

22 October 2026. Five additional result families from the independent model audit, after comparison with the other five counting lists. These are ordinary mathematical arguments, with the hypotheses and coding restrictions in their proof files. They are not five claims of literature novelty.

1. **C5 transfer of generated algebras and natural cardinalities.** The finite-sorted impredicative-generation transfer theorem, with admitted commuting maps and its explicit inverse conditions, includes the original/core Frege-natural isomorphism at every counting type. Group the general theorem and this application once. No Actuality or Choice is required. Proofs: [generated algebras](../notes/c5-generated-algebra-transfer-20261022.md), §§1–6; [natural-cardinality transfer](../notes/audit-infinity-transfer-20261022.md), §§3–7. This concerns the rigid C5 core and is distinct from the Actuality/GC slice transfer and the arithmetic-infinity classification counted elsewhere.

2. **Exact finite-support nominal classification.** For the specified Sym(A) hierarchy with trivial-action individuals E=N, classify all-type Intensional Choice, propositional Dedekind Infinity, Functional Choice and Relational Choice by world orbits and stabilizers. Include the four-way separating examples in this one classification family. Proof: [nominal classification](../notes/nominal-choice-classification-20261022.md), §§1–8. It is not a classification of all positive models.

3. **Larger support bounds and world geometry.** Countable supports give boxed Intensional Choice and propositional Dedekind Infinity without Functional or Relational Choice. Group this construction with its regular/singular support-bound and world-stabilizer classification. Proofs: [countable supports](../notes/countable-support-choice-countermodel-20261022.md), §§8–9; [general bounds](../notes/support-bounds-world-geometry-20261022.md), §§1–5. These use ZFC metatheory. They do not subsume the pair-individual construction with a nontrivial individual action in the world/algebra count.

4. **Effective Gallin representation of every positive model.** Original positive models and the specified relational Henkin presentations have explicit effective reconstructions, with exact degree preservation for the stated canonical type/operation coding. The treatment of truth and arbitrary signature constants retains its qualifications. Count the coding refinements and biinterpretation once. Proofs: [representation audit](../notes/effective-gallin-representation-audit-20261022.md); [biinterpretation](../notes/gallin-biinterpretation-20261022.md). The computational content is additional to the semantic world-indexed representation counted in the algebra list.

5. **Exact degree realization preserving a supplied complete theory.** Every complete consistent pure theory extending C has a uniformly constructed adequate model of exactly its degree and with that exact pure theory. Group the resulting exact degree spectra and the stronger oracle needed to parametrize the entire completion space in this one family. Proofs: [exact spectra](../notes/exact-completion-model-spectra-20261022.md), §§3–6; [parametrization boundary](../notes/completion-space-parametrization-audit-20261022.md), §§1–4. This strengthens, and is not a recount of, the oracle-capacity results. Conditional SP applications retain their consistency hypotheses.

The independent [audit handoff](../notes/audit-report-20261022.md) summarizes these results. Its bottom-cut, finite-cell, signature, standard-number and complexity findings are counted in other lists and add nothing here. No model of Strong Possibility is counted.
