1. THEOREMS

Let K be any pointed set-sized general model of C in the fixed relational
type grammar. There is a pointed general C model M and an admitted
whole-idempotent observer E preserving the Boolean operations and every
typed quantifier as WHOLE operation identities such that:

  Core(M,E) is isomorphic to K as a pointed typed logical model;
  Im(E) is proper;
  D_e^M=D_e^K and B_M is isomorphic to B_K x B_K.

The core is the canonical all-sort fixed-test quotient. The isomorphism
preserves any specified nonlogical signature, interpreted in M as below.
All higher M domains use objects ALREADY ADMITTED at explicit K types;
no full external function spaces replace the original Henkin domains.

The construction preserves full current Boolean Completeness, and also
full necessary Boolean Completeness. In particular:

  EVERY K satisfying C + Box BC_all is a proper observer core of
  another M satisfying C + Box BC_all.                    (1.1)

Together with observer-completeness-descent.txt this answers both
directions: necessary completeness descends to qualified cores, and every
necessarily complete general model has a proper realization within that
same class. No BF, C5, Atomicity, Choice, Infinity or standardness premise
is added. Properness concerns the observer image; for an infinite K no
claim is made that K and M must be abstractly non-isomorphic.

The exact instance bound for the construction is

  K satisfies BC_(T tau)     => M satisfies BC_tau;
  K satisfies Box BC_(T tau) => M satisfies Box BC_tau,    (1.2)

where the type translation T is defined in Section 2. Thus (1.1) uses
full completeness only to supply the corresponding coded-type instances.

2. PURE PAIR CODES AND WHOLE RETRACTIONS

All notation in this section denotes original K terms. Write 1 and 0
for its proposition endpoints. Define

  pair(p,q) := lambda z^t. ((z=1) and p) or ((z!=1) and q),
  pi_0(g):=g(1),              pi_1(g):=g(0).

C proves the exact identities pi_0(pair(p,q))=p and
pi_1(pair(p,q))=q. Identity of 1 with itself has value 1, and identity
of 0 with 1 has value 0, by the unconditional C theorems and their
necessitations. No assertion that z=1 is crisp for arbitrary z is used.

For any Boolean coefficient c, the expression (c and p) or (not c and q)
preserves Boolean operations in the pair (p,q). Ordinary Boolean
tautologies therefore prove that pair is a Boolean homomorphism into
the OLD pointwise Boolean algebra at tt. These are whole term identities
in C: universally close the tautologies, necessitate and apply
Intensionality when abstraction is required.

Translate new types into old types by

  T e=e,             T t=tt,
  T(sigma tau)=(T sigma)(T tau).

All types remain within the permitted grammar. Define old admitted
retractions at the coded types:

  d_e=id externally;
  d_t(g)=pair(pi_0(g),pi_1(g));
  d_(sigma tau)(F)=lambda x. d_tau(F(d_sigma(x))).          (2.1)

At individual arguments d_e means substitution of x, not a forbidden ee
operation. Each relational d is an admitted term. C proves WHOLE
idempotence d_sigma d_sigma=d_sigma. The arrow calculation is equational
composition of the lower-type identities, using beta and eta. It does
not infer function identity from agreement on current arguments.

At each relational sigma, d_sigma is a Boolean homomorphism of the
ORIGINAL coded-type Boolean algebra B_(T sigma). This follows from the
pair calculation, recursively by postcomposition and precomposition.
Set

  D_sigma^M := Fix(d_sigma) in D_(T sigma)^K.              (2.2)

These domains are nonempty, and their relational members are Boolean
subalgebras of the old coded-type algebras. A fixed arrow F has the whole
identities d_tau F=F and F d_sigma=F. Application therefore closes on
the fixed domains. We do not identify an arrow with its current table.

3. LOGICAL CONSTANTS, ABSTRACTION AND THE TWO TRUTH EVALUATIONS

The new proposition domain consists exactly of pair codes, hence is
B_K x B_K. Give it coordinatewise Boolean operations. If F is fixed
at the NEW predicate type sigma t, define

  Q_sigma^M(F)=pair(
    Q_(T sigma)^K(lambda x. pi_0(F(x))),
    Q_(T sigma)^K(lambda x. pi_1(F(x))) ).                 (3.1)

On arbitrary old coded inputs use their d-normalizations. This supplies
admitted total old extensions for these new constants. Because F d_sigma
=F, ranging x over the original carrier is equivalent, for this formula,
to ranging over Fix(d_sigma). Existential quantification is dual.

Translate an arbitrary new term A to an old coded term A^d: normalize
variables by d, use the coded logical constants, preserve application,
and use normalized abstraction. At an arrow the latter can be written

  lambda x^(T sigma). d_tau(body(d_sigma(x))).            (3.2)

A structural induction gives fixed outputs, input invariance, and
substitution. Beta and eta use the corresponding whole equations. All
inductive identities are unconditional C theorems about the displayed
pure coding terms; they may therefore be universally closed,
necessitated and abstracted by source Intensionality. This is the same
stable-premise abstraction method as general-core.txt, with no optional
premise here. It is not an appeal to Functionality.

For each i=0,1 put T_i(pair(p_0,p_1)) iff p_i is true in pointed K.
Boolean truth clauses hold coordinatewise. Equation (3.1), the original
K truth clause, and input invariance give

  T_i(Q_sigma^M F) iff every a in D_sigma^M satisfies T_i(F(a)).

Choose T_0 as M's designated truth. Both T_i will be useful in checking
the construction. No set of old world atoms or Kripke presentation is
introduced, and no quantifier is replaced by an external Boolean meet.

4. EXACT NATIVE IDENTITY: WHY NO EXTRA QUOTIENT IS NEEDED

Let delta_K at T sigma denote the original Leibniz identity proposition.
Let Delta_M(a,b) be the new Leibniz proposition obtained by quantifying
over ALL admitted new predicates. For d-fixed a,b the exact identity is

  Delta_M(a,b)=pair(delta_K(a,b),delta_K(a,b)).             (4.1)

Here is an internal proof of each coordinate. For any old P:(T sigma)t,
the term

  L_P(x)=pair(P(d_sigma x),P(d_sigma x))                  (4.2)

is a fixed new predicate. Thus equality under all new predicates entails
agreement under every old P. Conversely, for any old coded candidate F,
the function x |-> pi_i((d_(sigma t) F)(d_sigma x)) is an old admitted
predicate. Old Leibniz identity therefore entails agreement at that
coordinate under every new predicate. Use a=d_sigma(u), b=d_sigma(v)
throughout: both quantified implications are unconditional C theorems
in arbitrary old u,v. Necessitate them and apply source propositional
Intensionality to obtain literal coordinate identities, hence (4.1).
The argument does not identify quantifiers merely because their current
ranges agree, or abstract under unboxed fixedness assumptions.

At either T_i, (4.1) is true precisely when a=b literally in the original
K carrier. Thus the new prestructure already has literal current
identity at EVERY type. No quotient is needed to obtain M itself.
Identity may remain noncrisp as a proposition; C5 is not inferred.

At every relational type the new Boolean operations equal the OLD
coded-type operations restricted to Fix(d). At t this is the mux
calculation in Section 2; at arrows use it recursively, beta/eta and
input invariance. Consequently the new native algebraic order is

  (a <=_tau^M b)
     =pair(a <=_(T tau)^K b, a <=_(T tau)^K b)             (4.3)

for fixed a,b. This exact native-order comparison is essential below.

5. THE FIXED C LOGICAL-EQUIVALENCE GATE

Current classical truth clauses alone do not establish the C gate.
Translate a finite H proof into each coordinate of the coded formulas.
The propositional and quantifier axioms reduce to their original K
counterparts by Section 3. The substitution calculation gives beta/eta;
Section 4 supplies identity and congruence. Modus ponens and
generalization are preserved. Thus, if H proves A iff B, C proves

  pi_i(A^d) iff pi_i(B^d),     i=0,1,                     (5.1)

uniformly over all original coded arguments, whose occurrences normalize
by d. These are unconditional C theorems, not H equivalences under
additional axioms or optional hypotheses.

Universally close all arguments. For an extra original tag z, the mux
definition combines the two coordinate equivalences into coextension
of the paired outputs at z. Necessitate that universally closed C
theorem and apply SOURCE Intensionality through every coded argument
and the final tag z. The resulting identity is literal equality in K
of the coded lambda abstractions corresponding to A and B. Therefore
their interpretations in M are identical, as C's fixed H-equivalence
rule requires. The same argument covers arbitrary finite tuples of
abstracted variables and formula parameters.

H truth and rules, literal current identity and this exact gate establish
that M is a general pointed model of C. Neither an actual-function
representation nor Barcan was used.

6. BOOLEAN COMPLETENESS SURVIVES DOUBLING

Fix a relational tau and a coordinate i. Let U be a fixed new family
at tau t. In K form the admitted old family on T tau

  F_i(w):=(d_tau(w)=w) and pi_i(U(d_tau(w))).              (6.1)

Assume old BC_(T tau). It supplies an old greatest lower bound y of F_i.
Set g=d_tau(y), an admitted fixed new tau-object. For each selected fixed
w, ambient lower-boundedness gives y<=w. Monotonicity of d_tau gives
g<=d_tau(w)=w. If fixed z is another lower bound of all the selected
fixed objects, it is a lower bound of F_i on ALL old w: the guard d_tau(w)
=w in (6.1) permits identity substitution. Thus z<=y and
z=d_tau(z)<=d_tau(y)=g. Conversely z<=g implies lower-boundedness by
transitivity. By (4.3) these are exactly the new GLB inequalities and
their biconditional at coordinate i.

Every old family variable V codes U=d_(tau t)(V); original y is the
witness whose new occurrences normalize to g. Therefore this proof is
a uniform INTERNAL derivation

  C proves BC_(T tau) -> pi_i((BC_tau)^d).                (6.2)

It is not just an external assertion about current true extensions.
Both directions of the native GLB biconditional are proved above, and
all quantifiers on the right range over original coded variables with
d-normalized occurrences. Only the indicated old BC instance is used.

Current BC_(T tau) gives current BC_tau in M immediately (indeed at
both T_i). Necessitate the unconditional theorem (6.2). If K satisfies
Box BC_(T tau), modal K gives

  K satisfies Box pi_i((BC_tau)^d),
  hence pi_i((BC_tau)^d)=Top_K literally, for i=0,1.

The coded BC proposition is therefore pair(Top_K,Top_K)=Top_M. Native
M necessity is equality with Top_M, so M satisfies its OWN Box BC_tau.
No optional completeness premise was necessitated, no core/ambient
modal operators were identified, and no family of witnesses was chosen
uniformly across evaluations. This proves (1.2) and hence (1.1).

7. A PROPER WHOLE-IDEMPOTENT LOGICAL OBSERVER

Define, on paired propositions,

  E(pair(p,q))=pair(p,p).                                 (7.1)

Its extension to all old codes first applies d_t, so it is an admitted
new tt-object. It preserves Boolean operations and is idempotent by
the pure pair/projection equations. Equation (3.1) computes both
E(Q_sigma^M F) and Q_sigma^M(E composed F) as the repeated pair whose
coordinate is Q_(T sigma)^K(lambda x.pi_0(F(x))). Thus every typed
quantifier is preserved.

All these equalities are WHOLE old coded-term identities, obtained by
the same unconditional C equational calculations and source abstraction
used above. By (4.1) they are precisely the required whole-operation
identities in M. This avoids an inference from current tables to
operation identity. The one specified E satisfies every typed law; no
object-language quantification over types is introduced.

The element pair(1,0) is distinct from E(pair(1,0))=pair(1,1), since
their second projections differ. Hence the image is proper, including
when K has only two propositions and one individual.

8. EXACT RECOVERY OF K THROUGH ALL TYPES

Define embeddings and decoders as original K terms:

  i_e=j_e=id externally;
  i_t(p)=pair(p,p),                 j_t(g)=pi_0(g);
  i_(sigma tau)(f)=lambda x. i_tau(f(j_sigma(x)));
  j_(sigma tau)(F)=lambda x. j_tau(F(i_sigma(x))).          (8.1)

Simultaneous equational induction, using beta/eta and abstraction of
unconditional C theorems, gives WHOLE identities

  j_sigma i_sigma=id,
  d_sigma i_sigma=i_sigma,
  j_sigma d_sigma=j_sigma.                               (8.2)

For example the first arrow composite reduces to
j_tau i_tau(f(j_sigma i_sigma(x)))=f(x). These are equations of original
intensions, not merely agreements on current inputs. Thus every i_sigma
embeds the EXACT old carrier into the new fixed carrier.

Put n_sigma=i_sigma j_sigma on new objects. At t this is E. At an arrow,
direct beta reduction gives the whole identity

  n_(sigma tau)(F)=lambda x. n_tau(F(n_sigma(x))).         (8.3)

The normalized new abstraction includes d, but (8.2) and new fixedness
remove those extra d occurrences. Hence n is exactly the recursive
normalizer induced by E in the canonical general core construction.
Its fixed carrier is exactly

  Fix(n_sigma) = i_sigma[D_sigma^K],                      (8.4)

with inverse j_sigma: one direction follows from ji=id, and the other
from a=na=i(ja). No extra Henkin objects survive.

The bijections preserve application by (8.1), Boolean operations by
diagonal coding, and abstraction by the whole equations. For a core
predicate i_(sigma t)(P), its value at a coded argument x is
pair(P(j_sigma x),P(j_sigma x)). Since j_sigma i_sigma=id and
j_sigma d_sigma=j_sigma, its original quantified formula is equivalent
to quantification over ALL of D_sigma^K. Both implications are uniform
unconditional C derivations using instantiation at i_sigma(a); after
necessitation, source Intensionality supplies literal equality of the
quantifier values. Thus this is exact term transport, not just agreement
at the designated truth point.

Old predicates i_(sigma t)(P) separate i_sigma(a),i_sigma(b) whenever
a!=b in K: choose the original current haecceity of a. It need not be
crisp. Consequently the core's fixed-test quotient introduces no further
identifications. Current truth of i_t(p) at M is exactly old truth of p.
This establishes the claimed pointed typed isomorphism. Interpret each
specified nonlogical constant c in M as i(c); normalization fixes it,
and j recovers it. All signature interpretations are thereby retained
