FULL BOOLEAN COMPLETENESS CAN FAIL IN A GENERAL OBSERVER CORE

26 October 2026. Ordinary mathematical research by OpenAI Codex.
Direct source: misc, this note, sections 1-8, unpaginated.
The completed proof received an independent ordinary AI audit. Its one

1. The counterexample

In ordinary ZFC there is a C model M satisfying

  BC_all + boxed BF_all + boxed OS,

and an admitted whole-idempotent Boolean observer E preserving every
typed quantifier, such that its general all-sort quotient core K satisfies

  C5 + OS + not BC_t.

Thus ambient CURRENT Boolean Completeness does not descend through the
general observer-core quotient, even if supplied at EVERY relational
type, even with necessary all-type Barcan and necessary full typed OS,
and even when the resulting core satisfies C5. A fortiori neither the
single-instance BC_t transfer nor the full-schema BC transfer holds.

The proposition quotient in this example is explicitly nontrivial.
The result therefore does not conflict with the earlier positive transfer
under necessity reflection / triviality of all relational quotients.

The model modifies the earlier infinite-colour two-object countermodel
by allowing all source surjections instead of just source permutations.
This simple change supplies Dorr's finite-amalgamation hypothesis and
upgrades current BC_t to full BC. A new family of source baselines retains
full observer separation despite the added noninjective self-arrows.

2. Semantic framework and exact construction

Use the symmetric ideally-full finite-pinning construction recorded in
topics/classicism/groups/symmetric-ideally-full.yaml. Its underlying
source is Cian Dorr, Boolean Completeness does not imply Rigid
Comprehension, draft of 30 July 2026, Definitions 15-18 p.8, Lemma 21
p.10 and Proposition 22 p.11. The new two-object category below is not
attributed to that source.

Let Y=N and X=N x N. Put C_y={y} x N and beta(y,n)=y. The arrows are

  X -> X: all surjections of X onto X;
  X -> Y: all maps with every fibre infinite;
  Y -> Y: all permutations of Y;
  Y -> X: none.

This is a category under ordinary composition. The only point needing
checking is b h:X->Y, with b having infinite fibres and h:X->X onto.
The preimage under h of the infinite set b^{-1}{y} is infinite, since
h maps it onto that set. Thus b h again has infinite fibres. The other
compositions plainly retain their arrow classes.

Use finite pinning at both objects, with designated symmetry groups

  G_X = product over y of Sym(C_y),     G_Y={id_Y}.

Individuals are the underlying sets. For a relational type tau=sigmabar t,
an admitted entity A at W is a symmetric finitely pinned bit profile

  A(h,zbar),   h:W->V, zbar in the appropriate domains at V.

A finite P subset W pins A when same-target arrows agreeing on P have
the same row on EVERY target tuple. Symmetry is

  A(mu h, rho_mu zbar)=A(h,zbar),    mu in G_V.

Transport is precomposition: (rho_f A)(h,zbar)=A(hf,zbar).
Application evaluates the profile on transported arguments. All such
profiles are admitted, recursively through the fixed relational types.
The finite-pinning/symmetry framework gives closure under the language
and a model of C with its original H-equivalence gate. The target's old
domains are unaffected by adding a source with no arrows returning from Y.

3. All typed transports are onto; hence necessary BF

Every underlying arrow is onto. The general finite-section lifting
argument gives onto action at EVERY type, not merely at e.

Let i:W->V and let A at V have finite pin T. Choose a finite section
s:T->W with i s=id_T. For j:W->U define a source lift B by

  B(j,zbar)=A(k,zbar) if some k:V->U has k|T=j s;
  B(j,zbar)=0 if there is no such k.

The chosen value is independent of k because T pins A. B is pinned by
s[T]. For mu in G_U, k is a matching arrow exactly when mu k matches
mu j; applying the inverse symmetry proves the reverse implication.
Symmetry of A therefore makes B symmetric. Thus B is admitted.
At j=k i the old arrow k itself matches, and

  (rho_i B)(k,zbar)=B(k i,zbar)=A(k,zbar).

Hence rho_i B=A as complete profiles. This proof applies to self
surjections, including noninjective ones, and to arrows X->Y. Individuals
are covered by ordinary surjectivity. It is the all-type proof recorded
under 'barcan-surjective' in the group file, supplied here to expose its
exact hypotheses.

Surjectivity at every type makes BF hold at both objects: a witness
available after an arrow lifts to the present. Every object is one of
these two, so each separately boxed BF instance holds at X.

4. Full current Boolean Completeness at X

Apply Dorr's hull theorem, Theorem 36 p.18 and Corollary 37 p.19, in the
multiple-object form recorded as 'hull-conditions' and
'boolean-completeness' in symmetric-ideally-full.yaml. This theorem
gives BC at every relational type, not merely BC_t. Here are all its
construction-specific checks, with distinguished finite set M0 empty.

(B1) Let h,h':X->V agree on finite M, and let finite P,Q satisfy
P intersection Q subset M. The prescription h' on P and h on Q is
consistent on its overlap. If V=X, any finite map X->X extends to a
surjection: map the countably infinite remainder onto every missing
target point, assigning remaining inputs arbitrarily. If V=Y, extend
the finite map to an infinite-fibre surjection by dividing the remaining
source into countably many infinite sets, one for each y. Thus the
required amalgam exists for ALL h, including those covered by the hull
theorem's separation proviso. No injectivity is demanded of this amalgam.

(B2) Given finite N,P,Q, choose within each infinite colour class a
permutation fixing N and moving the finitely many points of P outside N
away from Q. The union of these finitely many prescriptions extends to
an element of G_X. This is exactly the required symmetry displacement.

Both hull hypotheses therefore hold at X. The theorem constructs, for
each admitted current family of relational entities, the least upper
bound of that family using symmetric finitely pinned hulls. By Boolean
duality it also gives the greatest lower bound. Hence BC_all holds at X.

This step uses the all-type hull theorem, not an illicit extension of
the simpler propositional orbit-envelope proof. That simpler proof does
not by itself control moving higher-type argument tuples. Allowing source
surjections is precisely what makes the stated all-type theorem apply.

5. A specified idempotent observer and its exact core

Define E:tt by

  E(h,q)=q(beta) for every h:X->X;
  E(l,q)=q(id_Y) for every l:X->Y.

This is empty-pinned. It is symmetric at X because beta mu=beta for
mu in G_X, and at Y the designated symmetry is trivial. Thus E is
admitted. Its transport along every self-arrow is E, and its transport
along every X->Y arrow is the WHOLE identity observer of Y.

At every self row its Boolean laws are evaluation laws. For any type
sigma and arbitrary current predicate F,

  E(forall x F x) is true at that row
  iff forall y in D_sigma(Y), (rho_beta F)(y) is true
  iff forall x in D_sigma(X), (rho_beta F)(rho_beta x) is true
  iff forall x E(F x) is true at that row.

The middle equivalence uses section 3's actual all-type onto transport.
The existential law is dual. At Y rows E is the whole identity. Since
these checks use arbitrary future inputs at arbitrary rows, they prove
the required WHOLE operation identities. Thus E is Good at all types
simultaneously. Moreover E(Eq) at a self row has value (Eq)(beta)=q(beta),
and at Y it is plainly Eq. This proves the whole identity E composed E=E.

Let r_sigma be the recursive normalizers and S_sigma their fixed domains.
Write q_sigma=rho_beta at each type. Since q(E) is the identity at Y,
induction through the type grammar gives

  q_sigma(r_sigma a)=q_sigma(a).                            (5.1)

Consequently q restricted to S is onto every target domain: lift a target
object first by section 3 and then normalize its lift. For a fixed
proposition p, its actual truth at X is exactly its truth at beta,
because Ep=p. Thus q both preserves and reflects current truth on S.
Action transport preserves applications, abstractions and logical
operations; (5.1) removes every normalization in their images. Hence
q is a surjective truth-exact logical homomorphism from the fixed
prestructure S onto the original model at Y.

Its kernel is precisely the all-sort fixed-test equivalence. One
direction is immediate by transporting each fixed predicate. Conversely,
every target predicate has a fixed lift by surjectivity. If fixed a,b
agree in truth under all fixed predicates, their images agree in truth
under all target predicates. The target has literal actual Leibniz
identity, so q(a)=q(b). Therefore

  S_sigma / ~_sigma  is canonically isomorphic to D_sigma(Y)

at every type, compatibly with every term and truth. This identifies the
GENERAL observer core, including its proposition quotient, with Y.

6. The core is C5 but fails BC_t

At Y the future category has just all permutations of N, with trivial
designated symmetry and finite pinning. This is the recorded finite-
support permutation model. Every transport is invertible, so distinctness
is persistent at every type, giving C5.

Here is a direct failure of propositional completeness. For m>=1 let A_m
be the proposition that a permutation exchanges 0 and m:

  A_m(h) iff h(0)=m and h(m)=0.

It is pinned by {0,m}. The external family A={A_m:m>=1} is the current
extension of an admitted tt predicate: give that predicate the SAME row
A at every permutation. It is empty-pinned and there is no designated
symmetry restriction at Y.

Suppose u were its least admitted upper bound. Let finite S pin u,
enlarged to contain 0, and choose distinct m,n outside S. The cycle
alpha=(0 m n) agrees on S with the transposition (0 m), so alpha is in u.
But

  v(h) iff h(0)!=m or h(m)=0

is a finitely pinned upper bound of every A_j and excludes alpha. This
contradicts u<=v. Thus the displayed admitted family has no least upper
bound, and BC_t fails. The isomorphism in section 5 transfers this exact
failure to the observer core.

The proposition quotient is genuinely nontrivial. Choose distinct x,x' in one
colour class and first let p0 have zero self rows and old row

  p0(l) iff l(x)!=l(x').

Put p=E(p0). It is fixed by whole E-idempotence. Its old rows are unchanged,
so it is nonzero; its self row at h is beta(h(x))!=beta(h(x')), which
need not be zero. Nevertheless p0(k beta)=0 for every permutation k of
Y, so rho_beta p=rho_beta p0=Bottom. Hence [p]=[Bottom] in the core
although p!=Bottom in the original Boolean image. This locates the exact
extra identification that defeats the earlier trivial-quotient
completeness transfer. Normalizing p0 is essential: application at a
self row h transports its argument, so (E p0)(h)=p0(beta h), rather
than the fixed bit p0(beta).

7. Full OS persists despite the noninjective source arrows

Fix a bijection j:N->X and two baselines

  b_X(y,n)=j(y),        b_Y(y,n)=y.

Each has infinite fibres. A finite modification of either baseline,
matching ANY prescribed same-target arrow on a finite P, remains an
allowed onto arrow. Call these arrows selected. If b differs from its
baseline only on P, then b g=b for every g in G_X fixing P pointwise.

For such b define a reader O_b. Its source-row chart consists of arrows
h:X->X whose restriction to P is injective and preserves the original
colour of each point. On that chart choose g_h in G_X extending h|P,
and set

  O_b(h,q)=q(b g_h^{-1}) on the chart;
  O_b(h,q)=q(beta) off the chart;
  O_b(l,q)=q(id_Y) at every Y-target arrow l.

Different g_h choices differ on the right by a colour symmetry fixing
P, which b absorbs; hence the definition is independent of that choice.
It is P-pinned. The chart is invariant under left multiplication by
mu in G_X. Taking g_(mu h)=mu g_h gives the symmetry equation on-chart;
beta mu=beta gives it off-chart. Y has trivial symmetry. Thus O_b is
an admitted observer.

Every row reads an arrow with onto transport at every type: b g_h^{-1},
beta, or the identity of Y. Exactly the arbitrary-future-input argument
of section 5 proves all whole Boolean and quantifier identities. At the
actual identity arrow the chart holds and the reader's current truth
profile is evaluation at b.

If relations A,B differ at an arbitrary arrow h on a target tuple, take
the union P of their finite pins and select b agreeing with h on P.
Their rows still differ on that tuple at b. Lift the tuple through rho_b
using section 3. Then O_b separates the resulting current applications.
This works for ALL relational output types and qualification lists.

At Y, the constant-row readers O_b(h,q)=q(b), for permutations b, are
admitted and fully qualified, and separate relations by the same lifting
argument. OS therefore holds at both objects, and boxed OS holds at X.

The extra injection requirement on the chart is essential. We did not
try to extend an arbitrary colliding finite self-map to a permutation.
Such arrows are instead approximated by the newly selected infinite-fibre
source surjections. This is why the old reader proof survives the changed
source monoid only after adding these baselines.
