The comparison is made separately for the pure language and for exactly
Σ={c:e→t}, with no original individual names. In each case write C_L for

Write H=hat(Box) and D=dual(H), as in

## 1. The constructor fragment works in more than one model

Let K be M together with every individually M-consistent sentence

    U = forall a:e, x₁,…,x_k,
          (I₁(x₁;a) and … and I_k(x_k;a)) -> θ(a,x₁,…,x_k),

where the I_i are components of the finite mutual constructor systems in
[inductive-witnesses.md](inductive-witnesses.md), §§2–4, and θ is a finite
Boolean combination of whole typed identities. The terms in θ and in the
constructors remain unrestricted. Constructor guards must have the stated
C_L(a)-proofs of persistence. The marker a is bound in U, not added to M.
An empty tuple of relational variables is allowed.

**Model lemma.** Any genuine M-model N in which every external subset of
each actual type domain is the actual extension of an admitted predicate
satisfies K.

Fix an actual a and one constructor system. Its externally finite closure
under the actual applicable rules is a least closed family of subsets.
The assumed predicate extensions admit that family. The impredicative
definition of I therefore makes its actual members exactly those finite
outputs. Every such output is the value of an L(a)-term. A true guard at
one of these ground tuples is C_L(a)-provable: its C-provable persistence
makes its value top, and the common marked identity diagram reflects that
ground identity to C_L(a). Thus these are exactly the valid constructor
trees used in the earlier theorem.

Now choose an M+U-model. Every valid ground tree has its I-membership
proved by C_L(a), so the model satisfies the corresponding ground matrix
θ. The common marked identity diagram makes that same Boolean matrix
true in every M-model, uniformly at every a. The preceding exhaustion
therefore proves U in N. This works for every U simultaneously. QED.

The hypothesis is about actual predicate extensions. It is not the
intensional Extensionality principle, and it is not asserted of every
M-model. It holds at the relevant full-current roots and retract roots
below. It is also only sufficient for K, not an asserted consequence of K.

Every U is C_L-equivalent to a universal identity sentence, by persistence
of the I-membership guards. Universal identity reflection gives DU→U.
Consequently full SP entails K. The earlier density fragment W is included
in K: each W_I(P) has exactly this guarded universal identity form and the
singleton profile model witnesses its M-consistency. This supplies an
actual inclusion, not just a comparison of their displayed models.

## 2. Arithmetic already separates K from M

K has the following one-step consequence rule. For a fixed L-formula P(n)
of the Frege number type ν_e, suppose that, for every external standard k,

    C_L proves I_e -> P(numeral k).

Then K proves

    U_P := forall n (N_e(n)=top -> (I_e and P(n))=I_e),

and hence, under its M theorem I_e,

    forall n in N_e P(n),    forall n in N_e H(P(n)).

Indeed N_e is the constructor domain generated by zero and successor.
The singleton profile root has exactly the external numeral orbit as its
actual N_e-domain. Each stipulated C_L theorem equationalizes to the
displayed identity, so that root satisfies U_P. Thus U_P is one of K's
eligible guarded universal axioms. Its consequences follow from necessary
numberhood, reflexivity, and I_e=diamond I_e. This is the earlier SP
one-step rule with its proof localized to the consistent fragment K.
The same argument handles a fixed finite tuple of numerical variables.

In particular K proves every standard-true Π⁰₂ arithmetic sentence:
write it as forall n P(n) with P Σ⁰₁, and use the separate finite PA proof
of each true standard instance. It also proves every true Σ⁰₃ sentence
by selecting a standard witness to its Π⁰₂ matrix. This uses the previously
recorded effective PA interpretation under I_e, not an added choice or
necessity principle. The standard profile model shows K is arithmetically
sound.

For each exact language, M theoremhood is Σ⁰₂: a finite Max proof has a
Π⁰₁ correctness condition. Its standard-arithmetic profile model makes M
sound. The complexity-preserving diagonal lemma therefore supplies a true
Π⁰₂ arithmetic sentence Γ_L with

    PA proves Γ_L iff not Pr_M(code(Γ_L's Frege translation)).

If M proved its translation, standard soundness and this equivalence
would contradict that proof's existence. The equivalence consequently
makes Γ_L true, and soundness rules out a proof of its negation. K proves
Γ_L while M does not. Thus M is a proper subtheory of K, independently
for the two languages. No claim that their two diagonal sentences coincide
is required.

There is an exact effectiveness check. K's constructor descriptions and
persistence certificates can be effectively enumerated; M-consistency of
each displayed U is Π⁰₂. K theoremhood is therefore Σ⁰₃. Conversely, the
effective Frege translation reduces true Σ⁰₃ arithmetic to K theoremhood:
true sentences are proved, and false ones are excluded by the standard
model. Thus K theoremhood is Σ⁰₃-complete. In particular K cannot prove
all true Π⁰₃ sentences: otherwise parallel proof searches using 0'' would
decide Σ⁰₃ truth using 0'', contrary to the jump theorem. These effectiveness
claims concern external proof sets; they supply no internal truth predicate.

## 3. A larger simultaneous fragment, retaining both signatures

Put E₁=forall x,y:e (x=y), and A₁=Fregean and E₁. Define the finite family
of singleton cells B_L as follows:

* Pure language: B_L={A₁}.
* Unary signature: B_L={A₁ and c=bottom_(et), A₁ and c=top_(et)}.

Let A_L be the conjunction of Atom_t(B) for B in B_L. The original
singleton profile constructions prove M+A_L consistent. Each atomhood
assertion is universal in identities, so SP entails A_L.

Let R_L be the schema consisting of DP for every finite conjunction P of
any of the following closed assertions: not E₁, any specified all-variable
Barcan instance for ordinary Box at a type, Atomlessness at a relational type, Relational
Choice or Transversal Choice at specified types. All type choices remain
external; each P is a finitary sentence. Define

    K* = K + E₁ + A_L + R_L.

**Theorem.** K* is consistent and is entailed by full SP. It does not entail
full SP.

For the pure case use the two-new-object hybrid from
[retract grafts](../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/source/research/2026-10-22/notes/reflection-retract-fragments-20261022.md),
§§4, 6 and 8. Its root R has one distinguished individual and no free
relational generators. Its target W has that distinguished individual,
countably many free individuals, and countably many free generators at
every relational type. Arrows are the specified type-preserving
substitutions; a:R→W and every default return b:W→R satisfy ba=id_R.
Old C-complete components remain unchanged. The new domains use finite
support and original-language term profiles at all old-target arrows.

The source proves genuine C validity and Max, arbitrary actual predicate
extensions at R, the singleton cell's atomhood at R, and all the listed
target assertions at W. Hence the model lemma supplies *all* of K at R,
not merely finitely many numerical instances. The return identity gives
DP at R whenever P holds at W: for every true root q, its image at W is
possible because the return makes it true again. Thus
diamond(P and diamond q) holds at R for every such q, exactly the raw
definition of DP. One and the same R satisfies K*.

Here is the required signature check rather than an appeal to pure/signature
conservativity. Use C(Σ)-complete old components with their given c
interpretations, and L(Σ)-terms for the profiles. At both new objects
interpret c by its own profile and the empty actual extension at every
new-target coordinate. Every new substitution fixes this distinguished c;
it is not one of the fresh generators. Old arrows preserve it as a
signature constant. Substitution and term closure consequently have the
same proof. Fresh-generator splitting and Barcan lifting still use
generators outside the finite support, and never change c. Arbitrary
actual predicate extensions remain available by the same zero-old-profile
construction. Actual choice graphs remain admitted for the same reason.
Thus W still satisfies every listed target assertion, and both new
objects satisfy the original MΣ. At R, each B in B_L has zero new-object
coordinates, while every L(Σ,a)-profile is constant on its complete
singleton extensional c-diagram. Therefore each such B is an atom.
This verifies all ingredients of K* independently in the signature case.

Since W is an M-model, every DP just used has the original M-consistency
side condition. The E₁ and A_L requirements are likewise SP consequences.
This proves both consistency and the claimed entailment.

For a strict separation, these same hybrids refute D(StrongActuality), where

    StrongActuality := exists w:t (w and Box forall p:t
                                      (w <= p or w <= not p)).

The old proof in retract grafts §12 applies also with c fixed. The true
root identity-coordinate proposition q has zero old profile. Where its
possibility can persist, the world retracts into the atomless W and has
no strong world. Thus no accessible point satisfies both StrongActuality
and diamond q, refuting the raw D-instance. On the other hand the
one-layer profile root has a true strong identity-coordinate atom, so
StrongActuality is M-consistent in each language. Its D-instance is
therefore required by full SP. This is a separating countermodel for K*,
not a claim that every K*-model refutes that instance.

## 4. All steps below SP can be made strict

In terms of deductive closure, the following is a strict chain:

    M < K < K+E₁ < K+E₁+A_L < K* < SP.

Every theory displayed before SP is consistent. The final strictness
means SP entails K* and K* does not entail SP; it does not presume SP's
consistency. Here are witnesses for the other steps, in addition to §2:

* K does not entail E₁. The arbitrary-individual-set profile construction
  with two root individuals is a genuine M-model with all actual predicate
  extensions, hence satisfies K by §1. In the signature case retain c's
  term profile and let old assignment arrows preserve its interpretation.
* K+E₁ does not entail A_L. Use an ordinary full-current singleton
  coalesced Max root, with two distinct copies of a component satisfying
  a chosen B in B_L. Its arbitrary component coordinates split B into
  two nonzero parts. Its arbitrary actual predicate extensions give K.
* K+E₁+A_L does not entail K*. The original one-layer profile root
  satisfies these three requirements and H(E₁), so refutes D(not E₁),
  whereas R_L includes that instance.

The second witness uses full coalescence, not the restricted term-profile
root used for the third. Keeping those constructions distinct is essential.
Adding duplicated components to the term-profile root would not create
independent coordinates and would not prove that separation.

The earlier upper bound remains: replacing D throughout SP by the dual
D₂ of hat(H), while retaining M in its side condition, yields an
inconsistent theory. Only D₂E₁ and D₂(not E₁) are needed. See
[neighborhood-comparison.md](neighborhood-comparison.md), §2. No strict
inclusion between SP and that inconsistent upper theory is asserted,
because that would presuppose the unresolved consistency of SP.

## 5. What this systematic comparison establishes

The consistent lower bound now includes the entire constructor-guarded
universal theory, all true Π⁰₂ arithmetic and its one-step reflection rule,
the SP-required singleton and singleton-cell atoms, and a simultaneous
family of genuinely nontrivial D-possibilities. These requirements have
one common model for each exact language. Individual consistency was not
silently substituted for joint consistency.

The next concrete omitted instance in these witnesses is
D(StrongActuality). The arithmetic test Γ_L+D(not Γ_L) also remains
unsettled: §2 proves Γ_L at K* but §3 supplies no D(not Γ_L). Both are
licensed full-SP requirements. Adding further constructor operations or
merely recovering the known arithmetic truth levels cannot by itself
settle either missing conjunction.

The ladder is a strengthened lower-bound result and a comparison of exact
theories. It is not a full SP model, an inconsistency proof, or a claim
that the remaining distance is small.

## 6. Strong Actuality and atomlessness can be strongly possible together

There is a further consistent comparison theory. Let R_L^- be R_L with
the ordinary Barcan assertions removed from its permitted conjunctions,
and put

    K† = K + E₁ + A_L + R_L^- + D(StrongActuality).

**Theorem.** K† is consistent for each assigned language. K† and K* are
incomparable, although both are consequences of full SP. In particular,
the previously missing Strong Actuality instance is jointly consistent
with the constructor fragment, the singleton-cell atoms, and strong
possibility of atomlessness. The construction below is new; it does not
claim the old Barcan verification survives this modification.

### The three-object category

Keep R and W from §3 and add a new singleton-individual object S. Give S
one auxiliary propositional generator z, constrained to denote bottom at
every old component. This is semantic bookkeeping, not an original
signature constant. At R there are no free generators; at W there are
the old unrestricted countable supplies. All new arrows fix the common
distinguished individual a, and fix c in the unary-signature case.

Retain the old R/W substitution arrows. The additional arrows are:

* R→S: the unique marker inclusion j.
* S→R: the unique substitution b sending z to bottom.
* S→W: the unique substitution sending z to bottom.
* S→S: id_S and e, with e(z)=bottom.
* W→S: send every free individual to a and every free relational generator
  to logical bottom or top of its type. In particular no generator is
  sent to z.

Composition is substitution. Thus bj=id_R and jb=e, with e≠id_S.
Every f:W→S factors through R and satisfies ef=f. Every nonidentity
arrow out of S sends z to bottom. These prescriptions are closed under
composition, including the mixed R/W/S paths. Into each old C_L component
use all source-generator assignments, imposing z=bottom for S and
preserving the original signature constants. There are no old→new arrows.
The zero constraint is preserved under old transports and every new
substitution, so old-arrow composition is well-defined.

At S interpret z as the proposition true only at id_S and false at every
other outgoing arrow. Its old profile is bottom. Its transport along e,
b, S→W, or an old arrow is consequently bottom, as required. At W use
the usual generator intensions: logical defaults have their logical
values, free target generators may all have empty actual extensions, and
old coordinates read the assignment. In the unary-signature case c has
its own old profile and empty extension at every new target, as in §3.

### Domains and C verification

Induct on relational types using full extension families at outgoing
arrows, with two restrictions: finite support in the source generators,
and a finite source-term profile at all old arrows. R profiles are L(a)
terms; W profiles may use its finitely many supported free generators;
S profiles may be taken to be L(a) terms, since its z is bottom at every
old arrow. S supports may include z, so id_S and e are distinguished even
though their old profiles agree. An extension family is determined by
its complete intension, as in the earlier constructions.

Transport substitutes the support and the old term profile. At S, a
nonidentity outgoing arrow deletes the supported z by replacing it with
bottom. Incoming W→S arrows introduce no z. Thus transport stays in the
stated domains. Arrows to the same target agreeing on a parameter's
finite support give equal complete transported parameters: after any
further arrow their composites still agree on that support. Therefore a
term in finitely many parameters has the union of their supports and
the substituted old term profile. This argument includes quantified and
identity-containing terms; their evaluation occurs over the same whole
target domains with identical transported parameters.

Lambda abstraction consequently has an admitted extension family, beta
and eta hold, and the classical local quantifier and literal-identity
clauses verify H. For H-equivalent formulas the abstractions agree at
every new coordinate and are identical in every old C_L component.
They are therefore identical intensions, verifying Logical Equivalence
with its original theoremhood side condition. These checks give a genuine
C_L model. Old completeness makes every new object an M-model.

Every actual predicate extension is still admitted at each new object:
prescribe a chosen extension separately for each new target object,
independent of the arrow to it, and take bottom old profile. This family
has finite support (indeed no free-generator dependence). The model lemma
therefore gives K at R. R has E₁, and its unchanged L(a)-profile argument
gives all singleton-cell atoms A_L. The same actual-extension observation
gives the stated Relational and Transversal Choice instances at W.

### The two different targets

At S, z is a true strong world. For every proposition p, z∧p is either z
or bottom: z is supported at the single identity coordinate. At every
nonidentity successor the image of z is bottom and hence settles every
proposition. Thus S satisfies StrongActuality. Since bj=id_R, the return
argument proves D(StrongActuality) at R for every true root neighborhood.

At W the original fresh-generator proof still gives atomlessness at
every relational type. Given a nonzero X, choose a proposition generator
d outside its finite support. The W-endomorphisms sending d to bottom
or top while fixing that support leave X unchanged and split it at any
witnessing arrow and tuple. This works also when the witnessing arrow
lands at S: precomposition still yields an allowed W→S substitution.
W has infinitely many actual individuals, so satisfies not E₁. Together
with the actual choice graphs, W satisfies every permitted conjunction
in R_L^-. The unchanged retraction R→W→R proves all its D-instances at R.
This establishes the joint model for K† in both languages.

### The exact comparison with K*

The K* model in §3 refutes D(StrongActuality), so K* does not entail K†.
Conversely the present K† model refutes D(BF_e and BF_t), an instance in
K*. Here BF means ordinary Barcan, in its converse-domain form.

At R and S, BF_e fails: the sole actual a has no distinct actual witness,
whereas a component with another individual witnesses
diamond exists x (x≠a). At W take a predicate F:t→t with bottom old
profile, empty new-target extension except at S, and extension {z} at
every arrow W→S. This is an admitted finite-support family. It has a
possible existential witness z at S. But for any actual W-proposition x
and arrow f:W→S, ef=f makes f(x) fixed under e, whereas e(z)=bottom≠z.
Consequently f(x)≠z. At all other targets F is empty. Hence

    diamond exists p F(p),    not exists p diamond F(p)

hold at W, refuting BF_t. Finally the root proposition q true at every
new-object coordinate and bottom at every old coordinate is admitted,
true and possibility-fixed. Every new object refutes BF_e and BF_t's
conjunction, so q witnesses H(not(BF_e and BF_t)). This gives the claimed
failure of D(BF_e and BF_t) at R. The old §3 target satisfies both Barcan
instances, verifying that this is a licensed SP requirement in each
original reference language.

Thus neither consistent lower bound contains the other. Their union is
equivalent to K*+D(StrongActuality), whose consistency has not been
established. This is a more focused next comparison than adding arbitrary
further constructors. It also shows why a model's failure at Strong
Actuality alone cannot be read as evidence that Strong Actuality and
atomlessness are incompatible under strong possibility.

## 7. A masked lift combines the two lower bounds

Continuation, 26 October 2026. The union left open at the end of §6 is
consistent. Define

    K+ = K* + D(StrongActuality).

**Theorem.** K+ has a genuine model, separately for each exact language.
Thus the two previously incomparable fragments have a common consistent
extension entailed by full SP. This does not establish full SP: the
specific models below still omit its arithmetic instance D(not Γ_L).

### Change only the incoming substitutions to S

Use the three-object construction of §6, with the following modification:
an unrestricted proposition generator of W may now be sent to z by an
arrow W→S, as well as to bottom or top. Generators at other relational
types still go to their logical defaults, and free individuals still go
to a. Every outgoing nonidentity arrow from S still sends z to bottom.
In particular S→R is b, R→S is j, bj=id_R, and jb=e. The old W→R
returns and R→W inclusion i also remain, with every return left-inverse
to i. Every f:W→S satisfies fi=j, although ef=f need no longer hold.

These enlarged substitution sets are closed under composition. A W→S
assignment using z followed by a nonidentity S-arrow replaces that z by
bottom, producing an allowed substitution. Precomposition by a W
endomorphism merely renames the relevant proposition generator or
replaces it by a logical default. Old exits from S also replace z by
bottom, giving legitimate old assignments of W's unrestricted generators.
The domain, finite-support, term-closure and C-validity proofs of §6 apply
with this enlarged arrow set. Interpret each W generator by its assigned
target entity; in particular its image may now be z at S. Original c is
still fixed by every new arrow and interpreted by its own old profile.

StrongActuality at S is unchanged: z remains supported at id_S alone,
and every other outgoing S-arrow annihilates it. Arbitrary actual
extensions at R, S and W remain available. The root retains K, E₁ and
A_L. The fresh-generator splitting proof still makes W atomless at
every relational type; its actual individual domain is infinite and
its actual choice graphs remain admitted.

### The lift that restores Barcan at W

Fix an admitted relational type σ, an entity y∈D_σ(S), and a proposition
generator d of W. Put v=b(y)∈D_σ(R), and let x₀=i(v)∈D_σ(W). Define a
new W-entity L_d(y) by its extension coordinates:

* At an arrow g:W→S with g(d)=z, use y's actual extension at S.
* At every other new-target arrow, use x₀'s extension at that arrow.
* At old targets, use x₀'s old term profile.

This definition works also at σ=t, where an extension is a truth bit.
It is an admitted entity. The old profile of v is an L(a)-term, and
x₀, being transported from R, has no free W-generator dependence.
The displayed family therefore has support contained in {a,d} and
the old profile of v. No new arrow assigning d an arbitrary higher-type
entity is postulated; all information about y occurs in this one
admitted σ-entity.

For every f:W→S with f(d)=z,

    f(L_d(y)) = y.                                             (8)

At id_S the two sides have y's actual extension by definition. At a
nonidentity arrow h out of S, h(z)=bottom, so the exceptional coordinate
is no longer used. The resulting extension is that of h(f(x₀)). Since
fi=j, this is the extension of h(j(b(y)))=h(e(y)). But he=h for every
nonidentity S-arrow: explicitly this holds for e, b, S→W and every old
exit, because each already annihilates z and S has no other free
generators. Thus h(e(y))=h(y). The complete intensions agree at every
outgoing arrow, proving (8). At the individual type no such extension
construction is needed: S has only a, already lifted by W's a.

Now let F:σ→t at W and an arrow f:W→S witness
diamond exists x F(x), with a particular S-witness y. Choose a proposition
generator d outside a finite support of F, and change only f(d) to z,
obtaining another allowed arrow f'. Finite support gives f'(F)=f(F).
Equation (8) gives f'(L_d(y))=y, so the actual W-entity L_d(y) witnesses
diamond F(x). This proves the previously missing S-target case of
ordinary Barcan at σ. It does not require an injective S→W map.

The other target cases remain the earlier verified ones. For an old
target, change a fresh generator of type σ to the old witness while
fixing the predicate support. For target R, lift the witness by i and
use the return identity. For target W, rename the finite support of the
witness into fresh generators disjoint from the predicate support and
modify the forward substitution to undo those renamings. The usual
support equalities remain equalities of complete intensions with the
enlarged W→S arrow set. At σ=e, the S-target case uses a directly and
the other three cases use the same individual-generator arguments.
Thus W satisfies ordinary Barcan at every admitted type.

### Joint consistency and the remaining separation

W now satisfies every finite target conjunction permitted by R_L,
including ordinary Barcan, atomlessness, not E₁ and the actual choice
instances. Its return to R gives all their D-instances at R. S satisfies
StrongActuality, and its return gives D(StrongActuality) at R. Together
with the root requirements already checked, this is a model of K+.
The argument is unchanged for the exact unary signature because all
new substitutions fix c and the masked entity's old profile is an
original L(Σ,a)-term. No pure/signature conservativity is assumed.

This construction strictly extends each previous lower bound: the §3
model separates K* from its added StrongActuality instance, while the
§6 model separates K† from the restored D(BF_e and BF_t) instance.

It still refutes D(not Γ_L), with Γ_L the true Π⁰₂ sentence of §2
independent of the appropriate M. All three new objects admit every
actual predicate extension, so their Frege numbers are exactly the
external numeral orbit and their arithmetic is standard. They all
satisfy Γ_L. The root proposition q true at every new-object coordinate
and bottom at every old coordinate is admitted, true and
possibility-fixed; it is below Γ_L. Hence H(Γ_L) holds at R. Since
M+not Γ_L is consistent, its opposite D-instance is required by SP.
This supplies a strict separation from full SP, not a verdict on the
consistency of the abstract theory K+ with that extra instance.

The completed comparison now has a common consistent upper extension
of the two previous lower bounds. The full arithmetic and arbitrary
Henkin-witness requirements remain. The masked lift closes the specific
StrongActuality/Barcan comparison problem; it is not claimed to lift
arbitrary M-models or to construct a full SP model.

## 8. Strong Actuality and Barcan can hold at the same retract target

The lift can also preserve all the Barcan instances at S itself. This
gives more than their separate strong possibilities at R. Give S, in
addition to its special z, a countable supply of unrestricted generators
at every type, in bijection with W's supplies. Both W and S have the
distinguished a and countably many other actual individuals. R still has
only a and no free generators.

Use the following substitution category. New arrows fix a and the
original signature. Between W and S, unrestricted generators may go to
same-typed unrestricted target generators or logical defaults; free
individuals may go to target free individuals or a. Into S, proposition
generators may additionally go to z. Every arrow with source S sends
z to bottom, except id_S, which fixes everything. Thus the S-endomorphisms
are id_S and all these z-annihilating substitutions. Into R use the
logical defaults and a. From R the inclusions remain unique. Old exits
interpret the unrestricted generators arbitrarily and z as bottom.

This is closed under composition: a path containing a nonidentity
S-arrow annihilates z, while any newly introduced occurrence of target z
is an allowed image of an unrestricted proposition generator. No such
path becomes id_S. The same finite-support domains and old term-profile
condition give a genuine M-model, by the preceding transport and C
checks. At S, old profiles may now use its unrestricted generators but
erase z. The special z is still true precisely at id_S, so S still
satisfies StrongActuality. Every actual extension remains admitted.

There are arrows p:S→W and j:W→S matching the two unrestricted supplies
and with p(z)=bottom. They satisfy pj=id_W. Put e=jp:S→S; it fixes every
unrestricted generator and annihilates z. Consequently

    he=h for every nonidentity arrow h with source S.          (9)

The root R retracts into both W and S by the usual default returns.
The original root profiles and new-object falsity of the Fregean axiom
still give E₁, A_L and, by actual-extension fullness, all of K at R.

Here is the additional Barcan check, including the finite-support
alignment absent from the simpler §7 lift. Let X be W or S, let
F:σ→t be an X-entity, and suppose f:X→S has a witness y for its
transported predicate. If X=S and f=id_S, the witness is already actual.
Otherwise f annihilates source z when there is one.

Choose a finite support for y at S. Apart from a, the fixed original
signature and possibly z, it uses only finitely many unrestricted S
generators A. Rename A into a fresh family B of matching unrestricted
X-generators, disjoint from a support of F. This defines an arrow
g:S→X with g(z)=bottom. Choose another fresh unrestricted proposition
generator d of X. Modify f on B so that it sends each renamed generator
back to its original member of A, and set f'(d)=z. Leave the support of
F unchanged and leave source z annihilated. The resulting f' is allowed,
and finite support gives

    f'(F)=f(F),       f'(g(y))=e(y).                           (10)

For relational σ put x₀=g(y). Define an X-entity L by taking x₀'s
coordinates except at arrows k:X→S with k(d)=z, where its actual
extension is y's actual extension. Its old profile is x₀'s profile and
its support is contained in B∪{a,d}, so L is admitted. At f', its current
S-extension is y's. After any nonidentity S-arrow h, the exception is
disabled, while (9)–(10) give h(f'(x₀))=h(e(y))=h(y). Thus f'(L)=y as
a whole entity. Hence L witnesses diamond F at X. If σ=e, no mask is
needed: e fixes individuals and (10) directly makes g(y) a witness.

This proves the S-target case of Barcan both at W and at S. For target
W the standard fresh renaming and inverse assignment work from either
source; target R uses its inclusion and return; old targets use a fresh
same-typed generator assigned directly to the witness. The special z
remains bottom in every modified old assignment and every nonidentity
arrow from S. These are all target cases, so **both W and S satisfy
ordinary Barcan at every type**.

W is still atomless at every relational type, by its unrestricted fresh
proposition generators. Both W and S have infinitely many individuals
and every actual predicate extension, giving the same Relational and
Transversal Choice instances. Consequently a single root R satisfies:

* K, E₁ and A_L;
* DP for every finite conjunction P of ordinary Barcan instances,
  atomlessness instances, not E₁, and the actual choice instances;
* DQ for every finite conjunction Q of StrongActuality, ordinary Barcan
  instances, not E₁, and the actual choice instances.

The first D-family is witnessed at W; the second is witnessed at S.
Their side conditions are verified by those same M-targets, separately
for the original pure and unary-signature M. All constructions and the
alignment argument fix c in the latter case.

This is a strict further strengthening of the §7 lower bound. Its model
there refutes D(StrongActuality and BF_e): R and S are singleton and
refute BF_e, while the atomless W refutes StrongActuality. The same
new-object indicator witnesses the failure of that D-instance. The new
S here verifies its M-consistency and the new R realizes it. The §8
model still has standard arithmetic throughout its new objects and
therefore retains the separation at D(not Γ_L) proved at the end of §7.
No consistency verdict for full SP follows from this stronger fragment.

## 9. Many targets and arbitrary local finite-order structures

The §8 construction extends to a set of target pairs, whose individual
domains may have different infinite cardinalities. It also supports more
than the local second-order fragment used in the earlier retract note.
The extension below gives a new strict consistent lower bound. It makes
no unrestricted claim about intensional identity or arithmetic disagreement.

### The precise local translation

Consider ordinary extensional higher-order logic in the same relational
type grammar, with full standard domains over a nonempty set E. In the
unary case its sole nonlogical constant is c:e→t, interpreted by a subset
of E. Each sentence uses only finitely many types. Write V_e=E, V_t={0,1},
and V_(τ₁…τ_k t)=P(V_τ₁×…×V_τ_k). Curried application has its usual meaning.

There is a finitary translation φ↦φ° into the original C_L language. The
translation restricts higher-type quantifiers as well as replacing equality;
replacing equality alone would not suffice. Define admissibility A_σ and
local equivalence ≈_σ recursively on types:

    A_e(x) := top;       x≈_e y := x=y;
    A_t(p) := top;       p≈_t q := (p↔q).

For a non-base relational type σ=τ₁…τ_k t, put

    A_σ(F) := forall ū,v̄,
      [all A_τi(u_i), A_τi(v_i), and u_i≈_τi v_i]
          -> (F[ū]↔F[v̄]);

    F≈_σ G := forall ū,
      [all A_τi(u_i)] -> (F[ū]↔G[ū]).

Translate typed equality by ≈, and ∀x:σ by ∀x:σ(A_σ(x)→…). Existential
quantifiers are restricted dually. Application, lambda abstraction and
truth-functional connectives are translated recursively. These definitions
contain no modal operator. The original c is admissible because its only
argument type is e.

**Local interpretation lemma.** At any new object whose actual predicate
extensions are unrestricted, its admissible entities modulo ≈ form the
full extensional hierarchy V over its actual individuals. Consequently
it satisfies φ° exactly when that full hierarchy satisfies φ, with the
same actual extension of c in the unary case.

Proof: proceed by type induction. The assertion is immediate at e and t.
At a relation type, admissibility makes the actual extension depend only
on the lower-type equivalence classes of admissible arguments. It thus
defines a subset of their product; ≈ identifies exactly the entities
defining the same subset. Conversely, pull back any subset of that product
along the lower-type quotient maps, taking the extension empty outside
admissible tuples. The unrestricted actual-extension property admits this
pullback as an entity. This proves surjectivity onto the full powerset.
Application respects the equivalences. Translated lambda bodies are
invariant on admissible arguments, by formula induction; their values on
inadmissible arguments do not enter the quotient. Quantification ranges
over every quotient class. Induction on terms and formulas proves the
truth assertion. This is an interpretation of local extensional logic,
not an identification of ≈ with C's typed identity. QED.

### The enlarged theory

Let F_L consist of those ordinary extensional higher-order L-sentences φ
which have a full standard model with an infinite individual domain.
This is an external semantic eligibility class, not an effective test.
For the pure and unary languages define it separately.

Let Q_S range over finite conjunctions of StrongActuality, ordinary Barcan
instances, Relational Choice, Transversal Choice, and not E₁. Let Q_W
range over finite conjunctions of the same list with StrongActuality
replaced by relational Atomlessness. Empty conjunctions are allowed.
Define T_loc,L by adding to K+E₁+A_L all sentences

    D(φ° and Q_S),       D(φ° and Q_W),       φ∈F_L.           (11)

**Theorem.** T_loc,L is consistent in each exact language, is entailed
by the corresponding full SP, and strictly strengthens the §8 lower
bound K+B_L. It still does not entail full SP.

Here B_L denotes the combined structural fragment of
[barcan-arithmetic-conservativity.md, §7](barcan-arithmetic-conservativity.md#7-the-combined-structural-sp-fragment-is-also-arithmetically-conservative).
The theorem below concerns consistency and strict inclusion; it does not
assert arithmetic conservativity for this enlarged fragment.

### Construction and all cross-target witness cases

For each φ∈F_L choose a full standard model with infinite individual set
E_φ, and in the unary case a chosen subset C_φ interpreting c. Since the
sentences form a set, ordinary set-theoretic collection and choice suffice
to choose these witnesses. Add a pair W_φ,S_φ with individual sets of
cardinality |E_φ|. Give each pair corresponding unrestricted individual
generators of that cardinality, a distinguished marker a, and matching
countably infinite supplies of unrestricted generators at every relational
type. An infinite set and that set with one distinguished point added have
the same cardinality. S_φ additionally has its special proposition z_φ.
R has only a and no free generators.

Use all the §8 substitutions between every pair of new objects. They fix
a and the original signature; free individuals go to target individuals;
free relational generators go to matching-type free generators or logical
defaults. Into S_ψ, free proposition generators may additionally go to
z_ψ. Every nonidentity arrow with source S_φ kills z_φ. Old exits interpret
all unrestricted generators arbitrarily and every special z_φ as bottom.
No old object points to a new object. These arrows are closed under
composition: a special proposition killed along a path stays bottom,
while any target special proposition introduced by a later substitution
is an allowed image of an unrestricted proposition generator.

Use the same finitely supported domains with old original-language term
profiles as in §§7–8. There is no additional finite-support restriction
on the set of target objects: support concerns source generators. Thus
prescribing an actual extension separately at every new target, constantly
over the arrows to that target, is allowed. In particular every actual
predicate extension remains admitted. In the unary case prescribe c's
actual extension to be C_φ at W_φ and S_φ, after identifying their
individual sets with E_φ; its old profile is c_M. A substitution fixes the
original constant c, so these different current extensions are compatible
with its transport. It does not have to preserve their current truth.

The term-closure and C checks of §8 are unchanged: the source support of
a composite term is finite, substitution sends finite supports to finite
supports, and old coordinates are evaluations of the same original-language
term on the substituted generator values. The old complete components
still witness every C_L-consistent original sentence, making every new
object an M_L-model. This is the same construction independently for the
two languages, with no conservativity assumption between their Max theories.

For each pair there are matching arrows p_φ:S_φ→W_φ and j_φ:W_φ→S_φ.
They satisfy p_φj_φ=id_Wφ. Put e_φ=j_φp_φ. It fixes all unrestricted
generators of S_φ and kills z_φ. Hence

    h e_φ=h for every nonidentity h with source S_φ.          (12)

This holds also for cross-target arrows to W_ψ and S_ψ, and for old exits.
The proposition z_φ is true exactly at id_Sφ and false at every other
coordinate, so S_φ satisfies StrongActuality. W_φ is atomless at every
relational type by the fresh proposition-generator splitting argument;
cross-arrows add no restriction on the fresh endomorphisms used there.

Here is the only new check needed for Barcan. Let X be any W_φ or S_φ,
let F:σ→t have finite support at X, and let f:X→Y carry it to a predicate
with a true witness y. Both X and Y have infinitely many individual
generators and fresh generators of every relational type. Therefore the
finite-support renaming proof does not require their total individual
cardinalities to agree.

If Y=W_ψ, rename y's finitely many parameters into fresh matching-type
generators at X, outside F's support. Modify f on those fresh generators
to undo the renaming. The predicate transport is unchanged, and the
transported renamed witness is y as a whole entity.

If Y=S_ψ and f is not its identity, do the same for the unrestricted
parameters of y, sending z_ψ to bottom in the reverse renaming g:S_ψ→X.
Use a further fresh proposition generator d at X and modify f to f' with
f'(d)=z_ψ. As in (10),

    f'(F)=f(F),        f'(g(y))=e_ψ(y).

For relational σ, mask g(y) by y's actual S_ψ-extension precisely at
coordinates k:X→S_ψ where k(d)=z_ψ, keeping g(y) elsewhere and retaining
its old term profile. This has finite support. At f' it has y's actual
extension; after every nonidentity S_ψ-arrow it agrees with y by (12),
because that arrow kills the mask. Hence its entire f'-transport is y.
At σ=e, e_ψ fixes individuals, so the unmasked g(y) suffices. The identity
case has an already actual witness. Targets R use the default return and
its section. Old targets use a fresh generator assigned to the old witness
while fixing the support of F. These exhaust all cases. Thus every W_φ
and S_φ satisfies ordinary Barcan at every type.

Arbitrary actual extensions give Relational and Transversal Choice as
before. By the local interpretation lemma, W_φ and S_φ both satisfy φ°.
Their ordinary individual domains are infinite, so they also satisfy not
E₁. Each inclusion R→X has a default return X→R whose composite is id_R.
The return argument therefore proves every instance of (11) at R. The
same targets verify all their original M_L-consistency side conditions.

At R, arbitrary actual extensions give K by §1, and E₁ is immediate.
The canonical singleton-cell atom proofs also survive: all new objects
are Max and falsify the Fregean axiom, and R's old profiles remain L(a)-terms,
constant on each complete singleton cell. This proves A_L. We have one
model of the entire T_loc,L, not merely separately consistent instances.
Full SP entails it because every instance (11) has its licensed side
condition and the other axioms were already proved SP consequences.

### Strictness and what the comparison isolates

Let U be the local second-order sentence saying that the individual
domain is Dedekind infinite but admits no full second-order Peano structure
on the whole domain. Both assertions are expressible using binary relations
in place of individual-valued functions, so the relational type grammar
is preserved. In full standard semantics with ordinary ambient choice,
U holds exactly when the domain is uncountable. It is false on finite and
countably infinite domains. It belongs to F_L, using an arbitrary c in
the unary case.

The §8 model has one individual at R and countably many at W and S;
therefore U° is false at every new object. Its admitted proposition q,
true at every new coordinate and bottom at every old coordinate, satisfies
q=diamond q and q≤not U°. Since q is true at R, it witnesses H(not U°).
That model refutes D U° and hence refutes D(U° and StrongActuality).
The enlarged theory proves both, so the inclusion from K+B_L is strict.

Conversely, all new objects of the present set-indexed construction still
have standard Frege arithmetic: the exact external numeral orbit is an
admitted actual predicate, and distinct numerals are separated by the old
finite extensional components. Thus the fixed true Π⁰₂ sentence Γ_L holds
at every new object. The same new-object indicator q gives HΓ_L at R.
It refutes the licensed D(not Γ_L), so T_loc,L still does not entail SP.
The resulting comparison is

    K+B_L  <  T_loc,L  <  SP_L,

with both displayed lower theories consistent. This does not presuppose
that SP_L is consistent.

At each S_φ the structural package also entails Persistent Comprehension
and Inextensible Comprehension separately: StrongActuality gives Actuality;
Actuality gives Persistent Comprehension; and Actuality with Barcan gives
Inextensible Comprehension by Christopher Sun's recorded argument. Requiring
one coextensive witness to be both persistent and inextensible would instead
give Rigid Comprehension, which Max excludes. These are existing theorem
applications, not new implications. They identify a concrete incompatible
strengthening of the consistent target package.

The lower-bound extension permits arbitrary local finite-order structure,
including quantification over relations on relations after the stated
admissibility restrictions. The unresolved extension allows the original
typed identities and unrestricted higher-type quantifiers. No result here
identifies those with their local extensional interpretation.
