1. The theorem

Let K be any pointed general/Henkin model of C + boxed Functional Choice
in the fixed relational type grammar. A fixed nonlogical signature may
be retained. There is a pointed general/Henkin model M of the SAME theory,
with an admitted observer E satisfying

  whole Boolean preservation;
  whole preservation of every typed universal/existential quantifier;
  E composed E = E;
  E != identity;

whose canonical normalized all-sort logical quotient core is isomorphic
to K as a pointed typed logical model, compatibly with its constants.

M has exactly K's individuals. Its proposition algebra is B_K x B_K.
All higher M domains consist of canonically normalized operations ALREADY
ADMITTED at explicitly specified K types. No external full function space
or standard Gallin completion replaces any Henkin domain.

The construction is a definable finite-coordinate interpretation inside
K. It gives a proper realization even when K has only one individual and
only two propositions. No Infinity premise is needed.

identified by this presentation. Identity propositions at every type are
crisp: Top if the objects coincide, Bottom otherwise. This follows from
NI and ND, including their necessary forms.

Let B=D_t^K and W the external set of its atoms. An atom a evaluates p
as true exactly when a<=p. Atoms separate B. The designated current truth
of K is evaluation at its unique true atom a0: if p is true, a0 and p
have nonzero intersection, so atomhood gives a0<=p; the converse follows
because a0 is true. No externally full powerset algebra is assumed.

The original universal Q_sigma is the infimum of the admitted range of
its input F. Universal elimination gives the lower-bound inequalities;
the converse uses BF to turn all current inequalities p<=F(x) into the
necessary universal implication p<=Q_sigma F. Consequently

  a<=Q_sigma F iff forall x in D_sigma^K, a<=F(x).

Boolean complementation and atomhood supply the existential truth clause.
Thus every a in W is a classical point for the entire original typed

In particular boxed FC in K gives every FC instance at every a. This

3. Finite codes and total typed retractions

Use old endpoints 1,0 in B as two distinct tags. Define the K-admitted
pair code

  pair(p,q)=lambda z^t. ((z=1) and p) or ((z!=1) and q).

Crisp old identity gives pair(p,q)(1)=p and pair(p,q)(0)=q.
Write pi_0(g)=g(1), pi_1(g)=g(0).

Translate the NEW type grammar into OLD K types by

  T_e=e,      T_t=tt,      T_(sigma tau)=T_sigma T_tau.

All these types are permitted in the fixed relational grammar. Define
total retractions d_sigma on the K carrier D_(T_sigma)^K:

  d_e=id;
  d_t(g)=pair(pi_0 g,pi_1 g);
  d_(sigma tau)(F)=lambda x. d_tau(F(d_sigma x)).            (3.1)

The symbol d_e is a metatheoretic identity substitution, not a term of
the forbidden type ee. Every other d_sigma is an admitted K term.
Induction gives d_sigma squared=d_sigma as an actual operation. Define

  D_sigma^M = Fix(d_sigma) inside D_(T_sigma)^K.             (3.2)

These carriers are nonempty. A fixed F:T_sigma T_tau satisfies
d_tau F=F and F d_sigma=F, so application stays in the fixed carriers.
It also has a unique extension from the fixed inputs to the whole coded
input carrier: its values outside Fix(d_sigma) are prescribed by d_sigma.
Thus the admitted coded F is faithfully its actual operation on D_sigma^M.

In particular D_e^M=D_e^K and D_t^M is exactly the admitted pair codes,
canonically B x B. All K pairs are admitted because pair is a K lambda
term. Conversely fixedness determines a pair by its two coordinates.

4. Logical constants, abstraction and truth in M

Give propositions coordinatewise Boolean operations. For fixed
F in D_(sigma t)^M define

  Q_sigma^M(F) = pair(
    Q_(T_sigma)^K(lambda x. pi_0(F(x))),
    Q_(T_sigma)^K(lambda x. pi_1(F(x))) ).                  (4.1)

The existential is dual. Every expression here is a K term. On arbitrary
coded inputs first apply the relevant d, so these constants have the
canonical extensions demanded by (3.2). Formula (4.1) quantifies over
the old coded carrier, but F d_sigma=F and d_sigma maps onto its fixed
carrier, so this is precisely quantification over D_sigma^M.

Interpret M at points (a,i) in W x {0,1}, taking pair(p,q) to be true
when a<=p for i=0 and a<=q for i=1. The designated point is (a0,0).
Equation (4.1) and section 2 give the usual quantifier truth clauses at
each point. Application is the original coded operation application.

Abstraction is closed without enlarging any K domain. Translate a body
using the K terms for the logical constants above and normalized input
variables. If its coded output type is T_tau, use

  lambda x^(T_sigma). d_tau(body(d_sigma x)).              (4.2)

The smaller-term induction makes its output fixed and its input
normalized, hence its denotation belongs to D_(sigma tau)^M. The same
induction proves substitution and beta. Eta follows because every fixed
F already satisfies F d_sigma=F. Arbitrary finite tuples and higher
parameter types cause no change to this syntactic interpretation.

Identity in M is literal and crisp. For any fixed a, the predicate

  x |-> pair(delta_K(d_sigma x,a),delta_K(d_sigma x,a))

is an admitted fixed predicate; old delta_K is crisp identity at T_sigma.
These diagonal haecceities distinguish different fixed objects at every

Thus H is sound at every (a,i). If H proves two formula bodies equivalent,

M is therefore a C model. Crisp identity gives C5. Its proposition algebra

5. Necessary Functional Choice is retained

Fix a point (a,i), a new input type sigma, a relational output type tau,
and an admitted M relation R serial there. Define an OLD admitted
K relation, on the coded carriers, by

  U_i(u,v) = pi_i(R(d_sigma u,d_tau v)).                    (5.1)

Currying in this display is left-nested. Seriality of R over the fixed
M carriers makes U_i serial over ALL of D_(T_sigma)^K at the old point a:
normalize u first, choose a fixed M output, and use it as v. K has FC at
a by its boxed FC. Since T_tau is relational, the permitted old FC
instance supplies an admitted f:T_sigma T_tau such that

  U_i(u,f(u)) is true at a for every coded u.

Put g=d_(sigma tau)(f), an admitted M operation. For fixed x,

  pi_i(R(x,g(x)))
    = pi_i(R(x,d_tau(f(x))))
    = U_i(x,f(x)).                                        (5.2)

Thus g selects R at (a,i). This works for each point and every allowed
type instance, proving boxed FC in M.

Different points may have different existential witnesses g. Nothing
requires assembling them into a single operation or mixing selected
output objects coordinatewise. Such mixing could invalidate an arbitrary
relation on whole objects; (5.1)-(5.2) never use it. Output normalization
is global and algebraic, so a witness chosen at a does not have merely
local membership in the new typed domain.

6. A proper fully qualified idempotent observer

Define E on paired propositions by

  E(pair(p,q))=pair(p,p).                                  (6.1)

Its K-coded extension is i_t followed by pi_0 and d_t, so it is an
admitted M operation and has canonical input/output normalization.
It is Boolean and whole-idempotent. For every M type sigma, (4.1)
computes both E(Q_sigma F) and Q_sigma(E composed F) as the pair whose
two coordinates are Q_(T_sigma)^K(lambda x.pi_0(F(x))). Hence E preserves
every typed quantifier. Existentials follow either identically or by
duality. These are equalities of actual operations; M's Functionality
therefore gives the demanded whole-operation identities.

It is proper: pair(1,0) and E(pair(1,0))=pair(1,1) are different. No
assumption about K having two individuals or infinitely many objects
enters this argument.

7. Exact recovery of every original Henkin domain

Define K-admitted embeddings i_sigma and decoders j_sigma recursively:

  i_e=j_e=id;
  i_t(p)=pair(p,p),            j_t(g)=pi_0(g);
  i_(sigma tau)(f)=lambda x. i_tau(f(j_sigma x));
  j_(sigma tau)(F)=lambda x. j_tau(F(i_sigma x)).            (7.1)

At e these again denote identity substitutions, not forbidden terms.
Their domains/codomains elsewhere are the old sigma and translated
T_sigma. Simultaneous induction gives whole operation identities

  j_sigma i_sigma=id,     d_sigma i_sigma=i_sigma,
  j_sigma d_sigma=j_sigma.                                (7.2)

For example, decoding the encoded f reduces pointwise to
j_tau i_tau(f(j_sigma i_sigma x))=f(x). For d i at an arrow,
use output d_tau i_tau=i_tau and input j_sigma d_sigma=j_sigma.
For j d use j_tau d_tau=j_tau and d_sigma i_sigma=i_sigma.
Old Functionality identifies the resulting operations. These equations
use only old admitted lambda terms, not arbitrary external decoders.

Therefore i_sigma maps every original K entity into D_sigma^M. On these
new domains define n_sigma=i_sigma j_sigma. At t this is exactly E;
at e it is the identity. At an arrow, direct substitution in (7.1) gives

  n_(sigma tau)(F)(x)=n_tau(F(n_sigma x)).                 (7.3)

Thus n is precisely the recursive normalization determined by E in the
general observer-core construction. Both sides in (7.3) are canonical
M functions, so equality on fixed new inputs establishes their literal
equality. Its fixed domain is EXACTLY

  Fix(n_sigma) = i_sigma[D_sigma^K],                     (7.4)

with inverse j_sigma. The inclusion from right to left follows from
ji=id; the reverse follows from F=nF=i(jF). This proves that no new
Henkin functions survive merely because the new source has more objects.

The bijections preserve application by (7.1). They preserve Boolean
operations by diagonal coding. For a fixed core predicate i_(sigma t)(f),
its value at a coded new argument x is pair(f(j_sigma x),f(j_sigma x)).
In the core quantifier every argument is normalized by n; its old coded

The fixed-test quotient introduces no further identifications: if i(a)

8. Constants and precise strength

For each nonlogical constant c:sigma of K, interpret its M counterpart
as i_sigma(c). The core normalization fixes it by (7.4), and j_sigma
recovers the exact original interpretation. This works simultaneously
for any fixed signature, including individual constants. No signature
reference theory or maximalization side condition is involved.
