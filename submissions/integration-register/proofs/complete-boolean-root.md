# Observer separation with propositional completeness and divergent possibilities

Ordinary mathematical research by OpenAI Codex, 25 October 2026. Direct source for the new arguments: `misc`, §§1–7 below, unpaginated. The framework is C in the fixed relational type system. OS means the full typed schema, with identities between whole Boolean and quantifier operations. BC_t is propositional Boolean Completeness only. The metatheory is ZFC.

**There is a model of C + OS + BC_t + Relational Choice + Actuality in which all of the following hold together:**

- Boolean Completeness at t can fail at an accessible evaluation whose proposition algebra is atomless, so Actuality fails there too.
- Actuality can fail, and can remain false necessarily at an accessible evaluation.
- Even separation by Boolean observers can fail necessarily at an accessible evaluation.
- BF_t can fail at an accessible evaluation.
- The arithmetical alternatives in the recorded two-arithmetic construction are both possible.

The root's proposition algebra is externally complete and has an atomless interval. The construction therefore separates ordinary observer separation with BC_t from Atomicity, necessary Actuality, necessary observer separation, necessary BC_t, necessary BF_t and Necessity of Arithmetic, simultaneously. It does **not** settle C + Max(C) + OS + BC_t: the construction supplies the stated possibilities, rather than every C-consistent pure possibility.

## 1 A complete Boolean algebra with finite supports

Choose a complete atomless Boolean algebra B such that every nonzero principal interval is isomorphic to B. For example, take the regular open algebra of Cantor space. A nonempty regular open set is a disjoint union of finitely or countably many basic clopen sets; its regular open algebra is the corresponding product of copies of B. A finite clopen partition, or the dense open union of the cylinders 0^n1, identifies each nonzero finite or countable such product with B.

Put G = Aut(B). A finite subset P of B supports an element when every automorphism fixing P pointwise fixes that element. Let H_P be this pointwise stabilizer. The finite Boolean algebra generated by P has finitely many nonzero cells c_1,...,c_m.

Two facts will be used repeatedly.

**Fixed elements.** The elements of B fixed by H_P are exactly the unions of the cells c_i. If b splits c_i, interval homogeneity supplies an automorphism of the interval below c_i exchanging b∧c_i and c_i−b, extended by the identity elsewhere. It fixes P and moves b.

**Finite attainment.** Every H_P-invariant subset S of B has a supremum attained by a finite subset of S, unless S is empty, in which case its supremum is 0. For each cell c_i meeting some member b of S nontrivially, either b already covers c_i, or an automorphism fixing P exchanges b∧c_i with c_i−b. In the latter case b and its image together cover c_i. At most two members per cell suffice. Their join is precisely the union of the cells met by S and is therefore its supremum. Complementation gives the corresponding finite attainment of infima. This is stronger than the mere existence of arbitrary joins in B.

## 2 A typed component whose only Boolean observer is identity

Define the typed domains recursively. Individuals form a singleton, propositions form B, and D_(σ→τ) consists of all functions D_σ→D_τ having finite B-support under the conjugation action


```math
(g\cdot f)(x)=g\cdot f(g^{-1}\cdot x).
```


Every admitted entity has finite support. Application is ordinary application. Boolean operations are those of B; equality is crisp, taking value 1 on identical entities and 0 otherwise. Quantifiers are the actual B-valued infimum and supremum of the range of an admitted predicate.

These operations and the lambda operations are admitted. The conjugation action preserves finite supports, and applying a supported function to a supported argument gives a supported result. A term with parameters of combined support P is equivariant under H_P, so its abstraction has support P. Quantifier operations have empty support because automorphisms preserve all infima and suprema.

For a supported predicate F:D_σ→B, its range is H_P-invariant for a support P of F. Section 1 therefore makes its infimum and supremum finite meets and joins of range values. Consequently **every ultrafilter U on B gives ordinary classical truth conditions for every admitted quantifier**:


```math
\bigwedge_xF(x)\in U\iff\forall x\,[F(x)\in U],
\qquad
\bigvee_xF(x)\in U\iff\exists x\,[F(x)\in U].
```


The empty range conventions cause no exception. Equality is also the defined Leibniz identity: the predicate x↦1[x=a] has the finite support of a and separates a from every unequal entity. Thus each ultrafilter evaluation satisfies H. Since all ultrafilters give these truth conditions, an H-theorem has B-value 1 at every assignment. In particular H-provable equivalence gives equality of proposition values, and H-provable coextension gives equality of functions. This validates C's theoremhood rule. Native Box is the crisp test b=1, so the component satisfies C5.

External completeness of B gives BC_t at every ultrafilter evaluation: whatever the actual extension of an admitted X is, it is a subset of B and has a least upper bound. The quantifier truth lemma just proved turns this external witness into the required object-language existential truth. Thus BC_t has value 1. Actuality has value 0: a true proposition below every true proposition would be an atom of B, and B is atomless.

**Every admitted Boolean endomorphism E:B→B is identity.** Let P support E and use its cells c_i. E commutes with H_P. Since E(c_i) is H_P-fixed, it is a union of cells. If a distinct cell c_j lies below E(c_i), then


```math
x\longmapsto c_j\wedge E(x),\qquad x\le c_i,
```


is a unital Boolean homomorphism into B below c_j that is unchanged by every automorphism inside c_i. Exchanging a nonzero proper x<c_i with its relative complement would make their images equal, although those images are complements in the nonzero algebra below c_j. This is impossible. Hence E(c_i)≤c_i; since the images of all cells join to 1, E(c_i)=c_i for every i.

Within a cell c, the stabilizer of x<c fixes exactly 0,x,c−x,c. Accordingly E(x) is one of those four elements. All nonzero proper x<c lie in a single automorphism orbit, so the same one of the four alternatives applies throughout that interval. The constant alternatives violate complementation. The complement alternative violates meets, using disjoint nonzero x,y with nonzero remainder in c. Only E(x)=x remains. This proves E=I.

The Boolean operation laws in the observer definition are whole-operation identities. Crisp equality and ordinary function application therefore make any admitted Boolean observer an endomorphism of exactly the kind just classified. At any ultrafilter evaluation there is a nonzero false proposition: split 1 into two nonzero parts and take the part outside the ultrafilter. Identity cannot distinguish it from Bottom. Hence Boolean separation at t, even with no quantifier qualification, has value 0. In this component both ¬Actuality and ¬BooleanSep have value 1 and are necessary.

## 3 An unrestricted predecessor with full logical separation

Adjoin a new root r to the algebraic component of Section 2. A root individual has its component individual. For a flattened relational type τ=σ_1...σ_n t, a root entity is a pair


```math
(R,a),\qquad
R\subseteq\prod_{j=1}^nD_{\sigma_j}(r),\quad a\in D_\tau.
```


The first coordinate is its arbitrary current extension and the second is its component entity. Application uses the current row and applies component functions to component arguments. Connectives, lambda terms and quantifiers are interpreted by their current truth clauses and their component interpretations. Thus D_t(r)=2×B, with current truth the first coordinate.

Projections to the component are onto at every type: lift a component relation using an empty current row. These surjections ensure that defined identities and the logical operations have the specified component coordinates. At the root, arbitrary singleton rows make defined identity actual equality of the pairs. Lambda closure follows from the arbitrary current rows and the component's lambda closure. The root validates H, and the component validates the algebraic H-equivalence identities, so C's theoremhood rule holds in the combined structure.

For every ultrafilter U on B define an admitted root operation


```math
E_U(\epsilon,b)=(\mathbf1_{b\in U},b).
```


Its component operation is identity. Its current row is unrestricted. It preserves the Boolean operations because U is an ultrafilter. For a root predicate F, Section 2's truth lemma and surjectivity of the argument projection give


```math
\mathbf1_{Q_\sigma(F_{\rm comp})\in U}
=\bigwedge_{x\in D_\sigma(r)}
  \mathbf1_{F_{\rm comp}(x_{\rm comp})\in U},
```


and the analogous existential equation. At the component, the observer is identity. These checks prove the required **whole-operation** Boolean and quantifier identities, for every type. The equality clauses are redundant over C, or follow directly from the crisp component identities.

Identity separates different root truth bits. Ultrafilters separate different elements of B, so the E_U separate different component values. At a higher relational type, unequal current rows are distinguished by identity at a tuple; unequal component functions differ at a component tuple, which lifts to a root tuple and is distinguished by some E_U. Hence the root satisfies full typed OS.

Its proposition algebra 2×B is externally complete, so it satisfies BC_t. The proposition j=(1,0) witnesses Actuality. Every nonzero (0,b) has no atom below it, so Atomicity_t fails. Arbitrary current relation rows, with empty component parts, represent the graphs of externally chosen selectors, giving the full Relational Choice schema in ZFC.

The component values calculated in Section 2 show that the root satisfies


```math
\boxed{
C+OS+\Box BC_t+\Box BF_{\rm all}
 +\mathrm{RelationalChoice}_{\rm all}+\mathrm{Actuality}
 +\neg\mathrm{Atomicity}_t
 +\Diamond\Box\neg\mathrm{Actuality}
 +\Diamond\Box\neg\mathrm{BooleanSep}(t,\varnothing).
}
```


Here boxed BF holds because root OS gives BF and the component's crisp C5 interpretation gives BF value 1. Boxed BC_t holds because it is true at the root and has component value 1. This is a new model with externally complete propositions, rather than a completion assertion about an arbitrary given model.

## 4 A cofinite component where completeness and BF can fail

For use in the combined model, take


```math
B_c=2\times\mathcal P(\mathbb N),\qquad
j(\epsilon,A)=
\left(\mathbf1_{(\epsilon,A)=\top},
\begin{cases}A&A\text{ cofinite},\\\varnothing&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}\right).
```


This is an S4 interior operator. Let O=Fix(j). Use the singleton individual domain, proposition equality δ_t(p,q)=j(p↔q), and, recursively, all nonexpansive function spaces for these O-valued equalities. At a flattened relational type put


```math
\delta(f,g)=j\!\left(\bigwedge_{\bar x}
 (f[\bar x]\leftrightarrow g[\bar x])\right).
```


Nonexpansive means δ(x,y)≤δ(fx,fy). Application is nonexpansive by transitivity of the equalities; sections and currying preserve this condition. Boolean operations and the quantifiers given by B_c-meets and joins are nonexpansive. For example δ(F,G) lies below the equivalence of their respective meets, and its openness lets it lie below j of that equivalence. The same argument applies to joins.

The admitted haecceity x↦δ(x,a) proves that the defined Leibniz identity is precisely δ: δ(x,y) is below each predicate equivalence by nonexpansivity, and the haecceity at x supplies the reverse bound. Boolean-valued soundness gives H-theorems value Top; H-equivalence therefore gives the identities required by C. At the current evaluation, the first coordinate, δ(x,y) is true exactly for actual equality. This evaluation is complete for meets and joins, so it has the required ordinary quantifier truth conditions.

The current proposition algebra is externally complete. The maps


```math
E_n(\epsilon,A)=(\mathbf1_{n\in A},A)
```


are nonexpansive Boolean endomorphisms preserving arbitrary meets and joins. They are therefore whole-operation logical observers at every type. Together with identity they separate proposition values, and ordinary function application lifts this to every relational type. Thus this component currently satisfies C+OS+BC_t.

At coordinate n, propositional equality identifies A and A' exactly when their symmetric difference is finite and their n-memberships agree. The local proposition algebra is


```math
2\times\bigl(\mathcal P(\mathbb N\setminus\{n\})/\mathrm{Fin}\bigr).
```


Partition N\{n} into infinitely many infinite blocks A_i. The family of their classes, with first local bit 0, has no least upper bound. From any upper bound remove one selected point in its intersection with each block; the result is still an upper bound and is strictly smaller modulo Fin.

This family is the extension of an admitted predicate, not just an external subset. For its set S of local classes put X_S(p)=(0,{n}) if the local class of p belongs to S and Bottom otherwise. At n its value respects local equality by construction. At every other coordinate its possible output values are locally identical, and at the current evaluation equal inputs have equal outputs. Hence X_S is nonexpansive. BC_t fails at n, giving current ◇¬BC_t.

An explicit BF_t counterexample at n is also available. Put


```math
F(\epsilon,A)=(1,f_n(A)),\qquad
f_n(A)=
\begin{cases}
\mathbb N\setminus(A\setminus\{n\}),&A\text{ finite},\\
\mathbb N,&A\text{ infinite}.
\end{cases}
```


If p and q are locally identical at m, their tail sets differ finitely and agree at m. Finiteness therefore agrees, and the output sets still differ finitely and agree at m; at n they always agree. Thus F is nonexpansive. Every Fp is locally identical to Top at n, but the meet of all Fp is (1,{n}), whose j-value is Bottom. Consequently ∀p Box(Fp) is true at n and Box(∀p Fp) is false there. This yields current ¬Box BF_t.

## 5 Incompleteness and failure of Actuality at the same evaluation

The preceding components can be combined more closely. Let A be the complete homogeneous atomless algebra of Section 1, and put


```math
B_*=2\times A\times\mathcal P(\mathbb N).
```


Define the S4 interior


```math
j(\epsilon,a,S)=
\left(
\mathbf1_{(\epsilon,a,S)=\top},
\begin{cases}1_A&a=1_A\text{ and }S\text{ cofinite},\\0_A&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}
\begin{cases}S&S\text{ cofinite},\\\varnothing&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
\right).
```


The automorphism group of A acts only on the middle coordinate. Use the nonexpansive typed hierarchy of Section 4, now admitting precisely those functions that also have finite A-support. The individual domain is a singleton. Proposition equality is j(p↔q); equality at every flattened relational type is j of the meet of pointwise equivalences. The interior is equivariant, so these equalities, application, sections, currying and lambda abstraction preserve both restrictions. The haecceity x↦δ(x,a) is supported by a and nonexpansive, proving that these equalities are exactly Leibniz identities. Quantifiers are the actual B_*-meets and joins of the admitted ranges.

There are three kinds of external truth evaluations: the first coordinate, each coordinate n of the last factor, and each ultrafilter U of A on the middle factor. All respect every admitted quantifier. This is immediate for the first and last coordinates. For the middle factor the range of a supported predicate is invariant under a finite-support stabilizer, so Section 1's finite-attainment lemma applies. The evaluations jointly separate B_* and respect the equality quotients. Consequently H-theorems have value Top and the hierarchy validates C's theoremhood rule, as before.

Fix a middle evaluation U. Two propositions are identical there exactly when their A-coordinates agree and their last coordinates differ by a finite set; their first coordinates are irrelevant. Its proposition algebra is therefore


```math
A\times\bigl(\mathcal P(\mathbb N)/\mathrm{Fin}\bigr).
```


Both factors are atomless, so the database's propositional Atomlessness principle holds and Actuality fails. To witness failure of BC_t with an admitted predicate, partition N into infinite sets A_i and let


```math
X(\epsilon,a,S)=
\begin{cases}
(0,1_A,\mathbb N)&[S]_{\mathrm{Fin}}\in\{[A_i]_{\mathrm{Fin}}:i\in\mathbb N\},\\
0&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
```


This predicate has empty A-support. If its outputs differ, the inputs' last coordinates differ by an infinite set, so their equality value is Bottom. It is therefore nonexpansive and admitted. Its extension at U consists of all A-coordinates paired with the selected partition classes. Any upper bound has A-coordinate 1, but its other coordinate cannot be a least upper bound: remove one point from its intersection with each block, exactly as in Section 4. Thus BC_t and Actuality fail at this same evaluation.

Adjoin an unrestricted predecessor as in Section 3. Its proposition algebra is 2×B_*, hence externally complete. For each of the external evaluations χ just described, the predecessor admits the observer whose current truth row is χ of the component proposition and whose component operation is identity. Quantifier correctness and the onto type projections give the whole-operation logical laws. The joint separation of B_* then gives full typed OS at the predecessor. These observers use the unrestricted current rows at the predecessor.

We have proved


```math
\mathrm{Con}\bigl(C+OS+BC_t+
\Diamond(\mathrm{Atomlessness}\land\neg BC_t)\bigr).
```


The predecessor also has Actuality and full Relational Choice. This component can be included in the common root of the next section, so the same-evaluation failure is compatible with all the other possibilities listed at the beginning.

## 6 A criterion for predecessor separation and a combination theorem

The observer construction has an exact characterization. Let M be a typed C component with actual proposition algebra B, and let R be its unrestricted predecessor, with proposition algebra 2×B and onto component projections at every type. For a finite type list Δ, call an external Boolean homomorphism χ:B→2 a Δ-logical character when, for every admitted F and every σ∈Δ,


```math
\chi(\forall x^\sigma Fx)=\bigwedge_{a\in D_\sigma(M)}\chi(Fa),
\qquad
\chi(\exists x^\sigma Fx)=\bigvee_{a\in D_\sigma(M)}\chi(Fa).
```


Then R satisfies propositional OS for Δ if and only if the Δ-logical characters separate B. For sufficiency, use the current row χ composed with the component projection and the identity component operation, as in Section 3; identity also separates propositions with different current truth values. Conversely, for nonzero b∈B, a qualified observer separating (0,b) from Bottom has a current Boolean character that accepts (0,1). It therefore factors through the B-coordinate. Lift each admitted component predicate F with an empty current row. The observer's quantifier laws and the onto projections prove exactly the two character equations above. Separating each nonzero b separates arbitrary distinct elements by their Boolean symmetric difference.

R has full typed OS if and only if this character separation holds for every finite Δ. Here is the additional step for higher types. Character separation for lists containing σ makes each component universal quantifier the actual Boolean meet of its admitted range: any lower bound is a lower bound after every character, hence lies below the quantified value by separation. Thus pointwise identical component relations have universal coextension equal to Top, and C's Intensionality identifies the relations. Distinct component relations therefore differ on some argument tuple, where a character detects the difference; lift that tuple to R. This supplies separation at every relational type. The characters remain external to M and are admitted as current rows at R.

If B is externally complete, R also satisfies BC_t. Thus the criterion isolates the additional quantifier requirement for the complete-algebra predecessor constructions above.

There is a useful closure principle. Let (M_i) be any set-indexed family of pointed typed C models, each with full OS and an externally complete current proposition algebra B_i. Form an unrestricted coalesced root with


```math
D_e(r)=\prod_iD_e(M_i),\qquad
D_{\bar\sigma t}(r)=
\mathcal P\!\left(\prod_jD_{\sigma_j}(r)\right)
\times\prod_iD_{\bar\sigma t}(M_i).
```


Nonempty component domains and metatheoretic Choice give the onto projections at every type. The current truth clauses and the component interpretations give lambda closure and C just as in Section 3. The root proposition algebra is 2×∏_iB_i, hence externally complete.

For a Good observer E in component i, take a root observer with current truth row p↦truth_i(E(p_i)) and identity operation in every component. Its current Boolean and quantifier laws follow from E's laws and the onto projections; its component laws hold because its component operations are identity. Thus it is Good for the same finite type list. These lifted observers, together with identity, give full typed OS at the root. Arbitrary current rows again give Relational Choice and the root-only atom gives Actuality.

Apply this closure to the predecessors in Sections 3 and 5, the cofinite model in Section 4, and one-world extensional arithmetic components from the recorded [two-arithmetic construction](../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/source/topics/classicism/models/coalesced-two-extensional-arithmetics-root.yaml). The extensional components have two-element proposition algebras and identity gives full OS. Their arithmetic disagreement survives as possibilities at the new root. The other components supply all the failures listed at the beginning. The atomless interval in the first component survives in the product proposition algebra.

More generally, include one extensional Henkin witness for each closed pure sentence consistent with C+Extensionality. There are only set-many sentences. The resulting root makes every one of those sentences possible while retaining the nonextensional possibilities above. This assertion concerns possible sentences consistent with C+Extensionality; it does not assert Extensionality at the root or replace the fixed reference theory C in Max(C).
