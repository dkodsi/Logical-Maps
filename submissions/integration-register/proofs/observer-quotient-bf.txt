GENERAL OBSERVER CORES BY LOGICAL QUOTIENT

Original mathematical argument, OpenAI Codex, 26 October 2026.
Direct source: misc; the numbered proof sections below are unpaginated.

1. THE TARGET AND THE POSITIVE RESULT

Work in the fixed relational language of C. Assume all-type BF in the
ambient pointed model M. Let E:tt be an admitted observer satisfying the
WHOLE Boolean identities, every WHOLE typed quantifier identity, and the
WHOLE identity E composed with E = E. Qualification at every type refers
to one metatheoretically specified observer, not an object-language type
quantifier. No identity-fixing assumption is imposed on E.

The normalized hierarchy always has a canonical logical quotient which
is a general model of C. All types, including the proposition type, must
be eligible for quotienting. Its proposition algebra is a quotient of
Im(E), not in general Im(E) itself. Observers with the same image give
canonically isomorphic pointed quotient cores.

Necessary BF descends to necessary BF. Current ambient BF is a different hypothesis from necessary BF.

2. NORMALIZED PRESTRUCTURE AND ITS ACTUAL TRUTH CLAUSES

BF gives Functionality. Use the ambient actual-function presentation:
D_t is the Boolean algebra B, current truth is an ultrafilter U, and
admitted quantified predicates have Boolean infima together with the
ordinary current quantifier truth clauses. Identity need not be crisp.
These are the same ambient inputs used in the preceding C+BF theorem.

Define r_e=id externally, r_t=E, and

  r_(sigma tau)(f) = lambda x. r_tau(f(r_sigma(x))).

No ee object is introduced. All relational normalizers are admitted.
Whole idempotence gives whole r_sigma squared = r_sigma by induction.
Set S_e=D_e and S_sigma=Fix(r_sigma) at relational types. A fixed arrow
f obeys r_tau f=f and f r_sigma=f. Thus it maps fixed inputs to fixed
outputs and is determined, as an AMBIENT object, on fixed inputs.

All S_sigma are nonempty. For relational sigma they are Boolean
subalgebras. The fixed proposition algebra is B_E=Im(E). A fixed
predicate F:sigma t satisfies E F=F and F r_sigma=F. Consequently

  E(Q_sigma F)=Q_sigma(E F)=Q_sigma F,

and the value range of F on D_sigma is exactly its range on S_sigma.
Thus the normalized quantifier has value Q_sigma F and computes its
Boolean infimum over S_sigma. Moreover

  Q_sigma F belongs to U iff F(a) belongs to U for every a in S_sigma.

The corresponding existential assertion holds as well. These are
current truth clauses in addition to Boolean infimum identities.

Boolean operations restrict to B_E. Interpret logical constants by
their recursive normalizations. For lambda closure, translate a core
term to M by normalizing variables, using these normalized constants,
preserving application, and abstracting the translated body. Induction
gives fixed outputs and invariance under normalizing each free input.
The abstraction is therefore fixed and admitted. Beta and eta are exact.

Call this fixed typed prestructure S. It has actual-function operations
but its internally defined Leibniz identity need not coincide with
external equality. Therefore S is not yet asserted to be a native
identity-separated C model.

3. OBSERVED IDENTITY IS EXACTLY CORE LEIBNIZ IDENTITY

For a,b in S_sigma define the Boolean value

  Delta_sigma(a,b) = forall P in S_(sigma t). (P(a) iff P(b)).

This is evaluation of a core formula, hence an admitted element of B_E.
For fixed a,b, normalization of an arbitrary original P gives

  (r_(sigma t)P)(a)=E(P(a)),
  (r_(sigma t)P)(b)=E(P(b)).

Every fixed predicate is obtained in this way. Equality of the value
ranges, Boolean preservation, and Q_(sigma t) preservation give

  Delta_sigma(a,b) = E(delta_sigma^M(a,b)).                 (3.1)

Here delta^M is native ambient Leibniz identity. Equation (3.1) is an
identity of proposition VALUES, not an identification of objects.

Define

  a ~_sigma b iff Delta_sigma(a,b) belongs to U.

The quantifier truth clause gives the equivalent external criterion

  a ~_sigma b iff every P in S_(sigma t) gives P(a),P(b)
                       the same current truth value.      (3.2)

This is an equivalence relation. At t, current truth is constant on
equivalence classes: the fixed tt object E restricts to identity on B_E,
so (3.2) includes the test p |-> p. Top is not equivalent to Bottom.

4. CONGRUENCE AT EVERY TYPE, INCLUDING LAMBDA PARAMETERS

Suppose f~g in S_(sigma tau) and x~y in S_sigma. For any fixed test
P in S_(tau t), the normalized translation of lambda h.P(h(x)) is a
fixed test on S_(sigma tau), by lambda closure. Concretely its ambient
body is P((r_(sigma tau)h)(x)). Hence P(f(x)) and P(g(x)) agree in
truth. The fixed test lambda z.P(g(r_sigma(z))) now gives agreement
of P(g(x)) and P(g(y)). Therefore f(x)~g(y).

The same argument works for every admitted term context T(z): compose
any output test P with the normalized translation T(r_sigma(z)) and
abstract z. In particular it applies when
T contains lambda abstractions, quantifiers, and other fixed parameters.
Changing equivalent parameters cannot change the equivalence class of
the resulting lambda object. This proves congruence for the entire
typed term interpretation, not just for application at the last type.

Form K_sigma=S_sigma/~_sigma at EVERY type. Its application and term
operations are induced from S. Its truth predicate is [p] true iff
p belongs to U. The proposition algebra K_t is the Boolean quotient
B_E/~_t. The Boolean congruence follows from the term-context argument;
its equivalence class of Top is a proper Boolean filter contained in U.

Quantification ranges over the quotient sorts. A quotient predicate
[F] has [F]([a])=[F(a)], independent of representatives. Consequently
the S truth clause gives exactly

  [Q F] true iff [F]([a]) true for every [a] in K_sigma.

Existential quantification follows similarly. No external selection
function for quotient representatives is needed.

Native derived identity in K has value [Delta_sigma(a,b)], since its
predicate quantifier ranges over all classes of fixed predicates.
Its current truth is equivalent to a~b, hence to [a]=[b]. Thus K has
literal actual identity, as required by the native model presentation.

5. THE H SOUNDNESS AND CLASSICISM GATE

First consider Boolean values in S, before quotienting. The Boolean
tautologies have value Top. Quantifiers are the admitted infima/suprema
over S, with lambda closure, so the ordinary quantifier axiom and rule
checks apply. Beta and eta hold as literal objects. Leibniz identity
has the value Delta defined above; for every fixed test P,

  Delta(a,b) <= (P(a) iff P(b)).

Tests obtained by abstracting arbitrary term/formula contexts give the
substitution instances. Reflexivity has value Top. These verify the
usual Boolean-valued soundness induction for H: every H theorem has
value Top under every S assignment. This check uses algebraic values,
not just the current truth of a theorem, and it does not assume that
Delta is crisp or that S is identity-separated.

If H proves A iff B, their values in S are therefore literally equal
under every assignment. For any tuple abstracted in a Logical Equivalence
instance, their admitted S abstractions agree in proposition VALUE on
all fixed input tuples. Input invariance extends that agreement to all
ambient tuples. Ambient Functionality then identifies the two lambda
objects literally in M and S. Their identity value in S is Top.

This verifies precisely C's H-theoremhood side condition. No equivalence
derived from optional observer assumptions is treated as an H theorem.
Closure under the H rules now proves that S validates C; the quotient
preserves the truth of every S formula by induction on terms and
formulas. Hence K, with its literal actual identity, is a general
pointed C model.

K is not claimed to have actual-function arrow domains. Quotienting can
make distinct output objects equivalent, so ambient Functionality by
itself cannot simply be reused after the quotient.

6. CANONICITY AND THE UNIVERSAL CHARACTERIZATION

The family (~_sigma) is the largest typed term congruence on S compatible
with its truth predicate. Indeed, if a congruence identifies a,b, it
identifies P(a),P(b) for every fixed test P. Truth compatibility then
gives a~b by (3.2). Conversely Section 4 verifies that ~ itself has all
these properties.

More precisely, let h be a sortwise surjective homomorphism of the
fixed typed logical structure into an identity-separated pointed model,
preserving and reflecting current truth. Then ker(h)=~. One direction
uses composition with tests. For the other, every target predicate is
the image of some fixed predicate, by surjectivity at predicate types;
indistinguishability by all those predicates means literal target
identity. Thus any such logical reduction is uniquely isomorphic to K.

For idempotent observers E,F with the SAME Boolean image, ambient BF
and Functionality give EF=F and FE=E as whole identities. The recursive
normalizers obey the mutual triple identities. The maps

  Phi_sigma(x)=r_sigma^F(x)  (and Phi_e=id)

give inverse bijections between their fixed domains, preserve
application, and are identity on the common B_E. They preserve the
quantifier values because they preserve the entire value ranges.
Consequently they preserve all fixed tests and hence the relations ~.
They descend to canonical pointed C-model isomorphisms of the quotient
cores. The isomorphisms compose coherently as in the earlier fixed-core
construction. The individual equivalence relation is also common to the
two observers.

Arbitrary named relational constants require the usual compatibility
condition between their projected interpretations. This canonicity
claim is for pure logical cores (and compatible expansions), not for
all arbitrary expansions at once.

7. NECESSARY BARCAN DESCENDS

Assume additionally all-type necessary BF in M. The native unconditional
equivalence of BF and Functionality gives necessary Functionality. For
any original f,g at sigma tau, the following Boolean inequality holds:

  (forall x in D_sigma. delta_tau(f(x),g(x)))
                                      <= delta_(sigma tau)(f,g). (7.1)

This follows by universal instantiation of the necessary Functionality
formula. Now restrict f,g to S_(sigma tau) and apply E. Whole Q_sigma
preservation, f r_sigma=f and g r_sigma=g, together with (3.1), give

  (forall x in S_sigma. Delta_tau(f(x),g(x)))
                                      <= Delta_(sigma tau)(f,g). (7.2)

Thus every Functionality instance has value Top in S, including its
universal closure over fixed f,g: the quantifier is the Boolean infimum
of a constant-Top family. In K its value is [Top]. Necessity of that
instance is true, because native identity is literal there. Necessitated
BF/Functionality equivalence inside the now established C-model K gives
all-type necessary BF in K.

There is also a weaker sufficient CURRENT hypothesis: E regards the
ambient Functionality instances as true. Expanding E through their
quantifiers and Boolean connectives, then restricting f,g to fixed
objects, gives the truth version of (7.2). Therefore K satisfies current
Functionality and BF. This does not silently replace that observed
hypothesis by current Functionality in M.

Whether current BF in M alone always guarantees current BF in K remains
open in this note. The fork example does not refute it: its quotient is
classical and satisfies BF.
