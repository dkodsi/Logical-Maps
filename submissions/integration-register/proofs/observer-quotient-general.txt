GENERAL LOGICAL CORES OVER BARE CLASSICISM

Original mathematical argument, OpenAI Codex, 26 October 2026.
Direct source: misc; the numbered proof sections below are unpaginated.

1. THEOREM AND SCOPE

Let M be an arbitrary general model of C in the fixed relational language,
in its usual presentation with literal actual identity. Let an admitted
E:tt satisfy whole Boolean preservation, whole preservation of Q_sigma
for every admitted type sigma, and whole idempotence E composed E=E.
One observer is specified metatheoretically; no object-language type
quantifier or infinite premise in a finite derivation is introduced.

Recursively normalize all types, then identify fixed objects that agree
in current truth under all fixed predicates. The resulting all-sort
logical quotient K is a general pointed model of C. Its proposition
algebra is a Boolean quotient of Im(E). Current Functional Choice descends
instance by instance, with the usual relational-output restriction.

No ambient BF, Functionality, ND, C5, endpoint-valuedness or external
Boolean completeness is assumed. The construction does not force BF in
the core: E=id recovers any original C model, including models failing BF.

Canonicity here means the canonical truth-compatible logical quotient for
the specified E. The stronger comparison of different same-image observers
is not silently imported from the earlier BF theorem; Section 8 records
the distinction.

2. NORMALIZATION IS EQUATIONAL, NOT EXTENSIONAL

Use r_e=id externally, r_t=E, and at an admitted arrow put

  r_(sigma tau)(f)=lambda x. r_tau(f(r_sigma(x))).           (2.1)

Omit r_e at an individual input. All relational r are admitted terms.
The whole identities r_sigma composed r_sigma=r_sigma follow by
induction from whole E-idempotence and C's beta/eta identities. At an
arrow, rewriting the composite produces r_tau r_tau f r_sigma r_sigma;
replace the two whole composites by their inductive equations. No use
is made of an inference from pointwise equality to function identity.

STABLE-PREMISE ABSTRACTION LEMMA. Let P be a finite conjunction of whole
identities, independent of a tuple zbar. Native C proves P -> Box P by
NI and finite conjunction. Suppose an unconditional C theorem has the
form P -> forall zbar.coext_tau(T,U), with tau relational. Necessitate
that theorem, apply K and P -> Box P, and use native C Intensionality.
The result is the C theorem

  P -> (lambda zbar.T)=(lambda zbar.U).                    (2.4)

The same conclusion follows from P -> forall zbar.(T=U), since identity
implies coextension. Here coext_tau quantifies the terminal arguments
of tau; all these quantifiers are INSIDE the boxed universal obtained
by necessitating the unconditional implication. No BF is used.
This is a derived rule for these stable premises, not an unrestricted
rule for abstracting pointwise equalities under optional assumptions.
It uses only native ambient C, before any core has been constructed.

Set S_e=D_e and S_sigma=Fix(r_sigma). The fixed domains are nonempty.
For fixed f at sigma tau, equational composition gives

  r_tau composed f=f,             f composed r_sigma=f.     (2.2)

These are whole identities. Thus application maps fixed objects to
fixed objects and is unchanged by normalizing an input. We do NOT assert
that a fixed arrow is determined by its values on current fixed inputs.
Without BF that assertion can fail even for E=id.

The fixed proposition algebra B_E is Im(E), with the restricted ambient
Boolean operations and current truth predicate. Every relational fixed
domain is closed under its defined Boolean operations, by whole Boolean
preservation and the recursive equations.

3. NORMALIZED TERMS, SUBSTITUTION AND THE TRUTH CLAUSES

Write T^r for the normalized translation of a core term into M:

  x^r=r_sigma(x), with x^r=x at e;
  c^r=r_type(c)(c) for a logical or named constant c;
  (T U)^r=T^r U^r;
  (lambda x.T)^r=lambda x.T^r.

Renaming bound variables avoids capture of E or other parameters.
For any fixed finite list of translated terms, the following statements
are C-derivable from a FINITE conjunction P(E) of the required whole
observer laws. They are uniform term identities, not metatheoretic
pointwise observations at one evaluation.

  (a) T^r has fixed output: r_tau(T^r)=T^r.
  (b) Replacing a free input x by r_sigma(x) leaves T^r unchanged.
  (c) (T[U/x])^r = T^r[U^r/x].                            (3.1)

These follow together by structural induction. In the variable case of
(c), the right side is r_sigma(U^r), which reduces to U^r by (a).
Constants and application use congruence and (2.2). At an abstraction,
alpha-rename its binder. Direct whole-composite identities can be rewritten
under the lambda; wherever an inductive equality is instead stated with
the bound variable free, first generalize that variable under P(E) and
apply the stable-premise abstraction lemma (2.4). P(E) contains no such
bound variable. The induction is well-founded on smaller terms, and
(2.4) uses only ambient C, so this is not a circular appeal to core
soundness. Beta/eta and whole r^2=r then remove repeated normalizations
of bound inputs. This proves lambda closure of S as an intensional
applicative structure without inserting ambient Functionality.

Normalized Boolean constants act on fixed inputs as the original Boolean
operations. If F in S_(sigma t), then E composed F=F and F composed
r_sigma=F. Whole Q preservation therefore gives the exact identity

  E(Q_sigma F)=Q_sigma(E composed F)=Q_sigma F.             (3.2)

The normalized quantifier is r_((sigma t)t)(Q_sigma); on fixed F it
therefore returns Q_sigma F itself. Its current truth clause is

  Q_sigma F true iff F(x) true for every x in S_sigma,      (3.3)

because the ambient truth clause ranges over D_sigma, and the values
on D_sigma and S_sigma have the same range by input normalization.
Existentials follow dually. This is a current TRUTH statement. Q_sigma F
need not be the external Boolean infimum of the current family F(x).

For formulas, (3.2) and Boolean preservation mean that translating a
logical connective or quantifier agrees literally with applying the
original connective or quantifier to the translated body. For example

  (forall x.A)^r = forall x.A^r.                           (3.4)

The bound x on the right still ranges over D_sigma, but all occurrences
in the translated body are normalized. Equation (3.4) follows by
applying the normalized Q to the fixed translated abstraction. It is
not a replacement of the original quantifier by a set-theoretic meet.

4. INTERNAL H-PROOF TRANSLATION

For each finite H proof of a formula A, there is a finite P(E), consisting
only of whole observer identities, such that

  C proves P(E) -> A^r.                                   (4.1)

This is an INTERNAL syntactic proof transformation. Merely observing
externally that S is currently H-sound would not suffice for Section 5.
Here are its checks.

Propositional axioms: by Section 3 the translated connectives are the
original connectives on translated bodies. Each propositional axiom is
therefore a substitution instance of the same classical tautology.

Quantifier axioms: translated universal elimination reduces by (3.4)
and (3.1) to the original elimination theorem, instantiated at U^r.
The apparent extra normalization r_sigma(U^r) disappears by fixedness.
Existential introduction is analogous. Distribution and the usual
free-variable side conditions are preserved. Domains stay nonempty
because they are images of nonempty domains under idempotent operations.

Beta/eta: beta uses the substitution identity (3.1); eta uses the whole
input-invariance identity of the translated arrow and original eta.
These are equations, not applications of the forbidden Functionality
inference. Equational congruence and renaming are preserved.

Derived identity: the translated Leibniz formula quantifies over fixed
predicates, as described explicitly in Section 6. Reflexivity is the
universal closure of P(a) iff P(a). For substitution into a formula
context A(z), abstract its normalized translation as a fixed predicate
of z. The translated Leibniz hypothesis applies to that predicate and
gives the required biconditional. For a term context followed by an
identity conclusion, compose each fixed output test with the translated
term context and repeat. This handles equality elimination through
application and lambda contexts without using Functionality.

Rules: modus ponens is preserved by Boolean compatibility. Generalization
is preserved by (3.4); P(E) is independent of the newly bound variable.
Capture-free substitution is (3.1). Taking the finite union of the
observer laws used in a finite proof gives a single finite P(E).

Equivalently the usual inductive H soundness proof has been performed
inside C under P(E). Original identity has not been substituted for the
translated fixed-test identity. No C Logical Equivalence inference is
being assumed as a rule of H.

5. THE NECESSITY OF IDENTITY REMOVES THE BARCAN OBSTRUCTION

Each conjunct of P(E) is a WHOLE object identity. C's Necessity of
Identity, followed by finite conjunction, proves

  P(E) -> Box P(E).                                       (5.1)

Let H prove A iff B. Apply Section 4, and universally close the variables
to be abstracted, to obtain the unconditional C theorem

  P(E) -> forall zbar.(A^r iff B^r).                       (5.2)

Any remaining free parameters can first be universally closed and then
instantiated using necessitated universal elimination. No BF direction
is used. Necessitate the UNCONDITIONAL C theorem (5.2). Modal K and
(5.1) give the C consequence

  P(E) -> Box forall zbar.(A^r iff B^r).                   (5.3)

In a model satisfying the stated whole observer laws, P(E) holds. C's
Intensionality now yields the literal original-object identity

  (lambda zbar.A^r)=(lambda zbar.B^r).                     (5.4)

Those are exactly the admitted normalized abstractions in S. This proves
C's Logical Equivalence gate for the fixed prestructure, before taking
its identity quotient.

The crucial move is to establish (5.2) by a finite proof transformation,
and then use the stability of its identity premises. We never infer
necessity from arbitrary current pointwise truth, never necessitate an
optional unboxed premise, and never regard equivalence under observer
assumptions as an H theorem. The H theorem is the original A iff B;
the transformed conditional (5.2) is an unconditional C theorem.

Sections 3-4 supply current H truth and rules in S; (5.4) supplies all C
Logical Equivalence instances. Hence S validates C as an intensional
typed logical prestructure, although its native actual identity may
identify different members of a fixed domain. The next step repairs
precisely that issue.

6. THE ALL-SORT LOGICAL QUOTIENT

For a,b in S_sigma define the core Leibniz proposition Delta_sigma(a,b)
by quantifying P(a) iff P(b) over the fixed predicates S_(sigma t), using
the normalized quantifier. Section 3 makes this an admitted fixed value.
By the whole Boolean and predicate-quantifier observer laws,

  Delta_sigma(a,b)=E(delta_sigma^M(a,b)).                  (6.1)

To verify this without a Boolean-infimum argument, expand the ambient
Leibniz definition and commute E through its Q and biconditional.
Its predicate body is E(P(a)) iff E(P(b)). On fixed a,b this is exactly
the value of the fixed predicate r_(sigma t)(P). Its quantification over
all original P is the translated fixed-predicate quantification from
(3.4). This is a term calculation, not an inference from equality of
the current ranges of unrelated quantifier operations.

Define a~_sigma b iff Delta_sigma(a,b) is currently true. By (3.3),

  a~b iff every fixed predicate gives a,b the same truth.  (6.2)

This is an equivalence relation at each type. At t, the normalized
identity test E distinguishes true from false fixed propositions, so
truth is constant on ~ classes and Top is not equivalent to Bottom.

For every normalized term context T(z) and every fixed output test P,
the context lambda z.P(T(r_sigma(z))) is an admitted fixed predicate,
by Section 3. Therefore a~b implies T(a)~T(b). This proves congruence
for applications, logical constants, quantifiers and lambda parameters.

Set K_sigma=S_sigma/~_sigma at EVERY type. Induce term interpretation
and Boolean operations from S, and let [p] be true iff p is currently
true. The truth clauses for quantifiers follow directly from (3.3):
quantifying over all fixed representatives is equivalent to quantifying
over all classes, because predicate values are congruent. No selection
of a representative from every class is needed.

The quotient's native Leibniz proposition at [a],[b] is [Delta(a,b)].
It is true iff a~b, hence iff [a]=[b]. Thus K has literal actual identity.
Induction on formulas preserves current truth from S to K. Since S
validates H and the C gate, K is an identity-separated pointed C model.

At t this is the Boolean quotient Im(E)/~_t. Its Top class is a proper
Boolean filter contained in the ambient current ultrafilter. The
quotient can be nontrivial on propositions as well as individuals.

7. CURRENT FUNCTIONAL CHOICE DESCENDS WITHOUT BF

Assume M satisfies FC_(sigma,tau), where tau is relational. Let [R] be
a serial core relation; choose its representative R in S_(sigma tau t).
For each original input u, normalize to r_sigma(u). Core seriality gives
some fixed y with R(r_sigma(u),y) true. Exact input invariance makes
R(u,y) the same proposition. Therefore R is originally currently serial.

Ambient FC supplies an admitted f:sigma tau with R(u,f(u)) true for
every original u. Put g=r_(sigma tau)(f). For fixed x,

  g(x)=r_tau(f(x)),
  R(x,g(x))=R(x,r_tau(f(x)))=R(x,f(x)).                     (7.1)

The last equality is the whole target-input invariance of fixed R.
Consequently [g] selects [R] in K. Well-definedness on classes is already
provided by quotient congruence. The argument applies an observer to no
merely true graph entry and chooses no class-representative function.

This strengthens the earlier arbitrary-image transfer result: neither
ambient BF nor triviality of the proposition quotient is required for
current FC inheritance. No analogous unrestricted current BC transfer
is claimed, because quotienting can change the order of relational objects.

8. CANONICITY AND LIMITS

For fixed E, ~ is the largest typed term congruence respecting current
truth: any such congruence must identify objects only when every fixed
test agrees in truth, and Section 6 proves that this test relation is
itself a congruence. Any sortwise-surjective, truth-preserving and
truth-reflecting logical homomorphism from S onto an identity-separated
model has this same kernel, because all target tests lift. Hence its
target is canonically isomorphic to K.

For E=id, normalization is literally the identity at every type, and
ambient identity separation makes ~ literal equality. Thus K=M. This
explains why the construction cannot generally prove BF or external
actual-function representation for its result.

The old C+BF same-image comparison used Functionality to upgrade
pointwise EF=F and FE=E to whole identities. We do not use that step
here or assert the unrestricted same-image comparison. Whole mutual
triple identities, if independently available, still permit the earlier
equational comparison. The bare-C endpoint comparison remains proved
by its separate endpoint-extensionality argument.

Necessary BF descends under necessary BF. The same-image comparison without BF is refuted by observer-same-image-countermodel.txt; the positive BF comparison is in observer-quotient-bf.txt, Section 6.
