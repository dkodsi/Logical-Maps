1. RESULT

Work in ZFC and in the database's fixed relational type system. Given any
nonempty set-sized family of pointed C action models (K_i,V_i), there is
one pointed C action model R with

  OS + BF_all + Strong Actuality + RC_all + TC_all + WO_all + Zorn_all

and, for each i, an admitted whole-idempotent Boolean/all-typed-quantifier
observer E_i whose canonical logical quotient core is isomorphic to K_i
at V_i as a pointed typed logical model.

One observer works at all types simultaneously. This is stronger than
satisfying a separate observer-existence instance for each finite list.
The isomorphism concerns domains, term interpretation and current truth;
it does not identify the surrounding world categories.

In particular CURRENT BF does not descend to general observer cores.
The failure can occur at both individual type e and proposition type t,
despite the strong ambient package displayed above.

The same universal realization holds with pure Max(C) additionally true
at the source: include a C-complete set of witness components. This uses
the ordinary action-model completeness input already employed in the
interpretation-root construction. It is not a C5 or boxed-BF construction.

2. A GENERAL READER-CORE LEMMA

Suppose R and K are pointed C action models and q_sigma:R_sigma->K_sigma
is onto at EVERY admitted type and preserves interpretation of every
typed term. Named constants, if present, have compatible interpretations.
Suppose an admitted observer E:tt has all the whole Boolean and typed-Q
laws, whole idempotence, and satisfies

  q_tt(E)=id_K,
  E(p) is currently true in R iff q_t(p) is currently true in K.   (2.1)

Use the normalization from observer-core-without-barcan/general-core.txt:

  r_e=id externally; r_t=E;
  r_(sigma tau)(f)=lambda x.r_tau(f(r_sigma x)).

The fixed domains S_sigma=Fix(r_sigma) form the normalized logical
prestructure. No Functionality or BF is needed for its construction.
Let ~ be equality of current truth under every fixed predicate, and let
Core(R,E)=S/~ at every type, including t.

LEMMA. The maps q restricted to S induce a canonical pointed typed
logical isomorphism Core(R,E) -> K.

Proof. First, transporting a normalizer gives identity at every type.
At t this is q(E)=id; at e it is the external identity. At an arrow type,
the induction hypothesis and preservation of lambda/application give
lambda x.f(x), which is f by C's eta identity. Hence

  q_sigma(r_sigma(x))=q_sigma(x).                            (2.2)

Since q_sigma is onto and r_sigma is idempotent, q_sigma restricted to
S_sigma is also onto: lift any k arbitrarily, then normalize its lift.

For fixed p, (2.1) gives

  p true in R iff E(p) true in R iff q_t(p) true in K.         (2.3)

Normalized constants transport to the original K constants by (2.2).
Application, lambda abstraction and quantifier terms are preserved by
the assumed full term-transport law. Thus q|S is a logical homomorphism
preserving and reflecting truth. This includes identity-valued terms:
K's identity remains its own defined Leibniz identity.

Here is the kernel calculation explicitly. For fixed a,b at type sigma,
if q(a)=q(b), every fixed predicate P gives P(a),P(b) the same truth,
by term preservation and (2.3); hence a~b. Conversely, if q(a) and q(b)
are distinct, some admitted K predicate distinguishes them in current
truth, because K's actual Leibniz identity is literal equality. For
example, use the haecceity of q(a). Lift this predicate through the ONTO
map S_(sigma t)->K_(sigma t). Its lift distinguishes a,b by (2.3).
Therefore

  a~_sigma b iff q_sigma(a)=q_sigma(b).                      (2.4)

Every quotient map induced by q is consequently bijective. Its logical
and truth properties give the required isomorphism. The proof uses no
choice of representatives for all equivalence classes simultaneously.

  forall y in K_sigma, (q_i F)(y),

For individuals the quotient kernel is exactly b_i(a)=b_i(a'). At every

4. THE AMBIENT PACKAGE

Full OS and current BF_all are established by the root's finite-pin
approximation-and-lifting argument. In detail, an old distinguishing row
or existential witness uses finitely many names. An onto interpretation
can agree with that row on those names, since finitely modifying an
infinite-fibre baseline preserves onto-ness. Lift its distinguishing
tuple or witness to the source. The qualified reader detects the former;
the latter gives the dual Barcan implication. A current distinguishing
row is detected by identity.

The proposition w={1_o} is admitted. It is true and decides each source
proposition in the Boolean order: w<=p or w<=not p according to the bit
of p at 1_o. At every old arrow w transports to Bottom, which lies below
every old proposition and its negation. Thus the deciding property holds
necessarily, even for newly available old propositions. This proves the
native Strong Actuality formula, not merely existence of a current atom.

Every external current relation on any finite tuple of source domains
is represented by its chosen current row and zero old rows. These rows
are empty-pinned and land. External ZFC choice therefore supplies and
represents current selector graphs, transversals and well-orders. These
are separate direct verifications, not an assumption that RC implies TC.
They give RC_all, TC_all and WO_all, including individual arguments.
The root also satisfies the native preorder form of Zorn: quotient a
current preorder by mutual comparison; each quotient chain has a set of
representatives forming an admitted antisymmetric chain; the internal
bound hypothesis and external Zorn yield a maximal equivalence class.
Any representative has the required preorder-maximality property.

None of these statements asserts an admitted operation representing
every actual graph, or necessitates one of these optional principles.

5. AN EXPLICIT BARCAN COUNTEREXAMPLE

For K use the native finite-support-identity-or-collapse action model:
one object N, with identity and the monotone maps h:N->N having h(0)=h(1),
and the finitely pinned hierarchy. Its source attribution is Bacon-Dorr,
Classicism, draft of 16 May 2023, Appendix D, Proposition D.5(4), pp.74,78.
The native record's barcan-positive argument witnesses failure at e.

For clarity the recorded sharper type-t witness can be checked directly.
Take the finite pin N0={0}, an arrow i with i(0)=2, and

  q={k : k(1)=1}.

Every arrow j agreeing with i on N0 has range contained in [2,infinity).
For any proposition p, membership of k in rho_j(p) depends only on k's
restriction to range(j). But q distinguishes two allowed arrows agreeing
on [2,infinity): let k(0)=k(1)=1 and k'(0)=k'(1)=0, and let both be the
identity from 2 onward. Thus q is outside every such transport image.

Define an admitted predicate X on propositions by

  X(j,r) iff (r!=q or j(0)!=2).

It is pinned by {0}. At every arrow j and every current p, X(j,rho_j p)
holds: when j(0)=2 the range argument excludes equality to q. Hence
forall p.Box(Xp) is currently true. At i, however, X(i,q) fails, so
Box(forall p.Xp) is false. This is precisely a failure of BF_t.

The root R built above satisfies OS+BF_all+Strong Actuality+RC_all+TC_all
+WO_all+Zorn_all, while its E_i core is this K and fails BF_e and BF_t.
This settles current BF inheritance negatively, without adding a premise
that already forces necessary BF. The observer is generally nonendpoint.

6. MAXIMALISM AND THE GENERAL INHERITANCE CONSEQUENCE

Adjoin to the component family one witness action model for every closed
pure C-consistent sentence. There are set-many such sentences; the usual
action completeness and Collection/Choice supply a set-sized family.
All their old truths remain true at reachable objects, so the source
satisfies pure Max(C), as proved in the existing Section 8.3 construction.
Keeping the designated K among these components leaves Sections 2-3
unchanged. Thus the BF counterexample also has ambient pure Max(C).

The general lesson has an exact form. If a pure sentence A fails in a
C action model, it fails in a designated observer core of a model of the
entire source package in Section 1 (even with Max(C)). Consequently no
new consequence of that ambient package can be universally inherited by
all its fully qualified idempotent observer cores. Such a universally
inherited consequence would already be a theorem of C, by action-model
completeness. This is a statement about all these cores, not about any
particular chosen one or about observers with further reflection laws.
