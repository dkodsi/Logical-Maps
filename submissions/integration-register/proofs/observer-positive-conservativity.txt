1. RESULT

Fix any set-sized signature Sigma of constants of admitted types, including
the empty signature. Work in C(Sigma), with the fixed relational type
grammar: e and t, and arrows whose result type is not e.

Let E+ consist of formulas built from typed identities s=t by finite
conjunction, finite disjunction, and either object quantifier. Let D+ be
the corresponding class starting from typed disequalities s!=t. Terms in
these atoms are unrestricted: they may contain lambda, quantifiers,
identity-valued terms, Boolean connectives, modal operators, and parameters
of arbitrary admitted types. Only the OUTER formula grammar is restricted.
In particular, all quantifier alternations and individual quantifiers are
allowed. E+ and D+ are the de Morgan duals of one another over C.

Write P for the following current package:

       full typed OS + Strong Actuality + all-type Zorn.              (1)

The constructed roots also satisfy all-type BF, Relational Choice,
Transversal Choice and Well-Ordering. These do not require extra premises:
OS implies BF, and the recorded Zorn -> Well-Ordering -> Transversal
Choice -> Relational Choice implications recover the other assertions.
Thus (1) is a simpler statement of the full package developed below.

THEOREM. For every set Gamma of D+ sentences and every E+ sentence A,

  C(Sigma)+Gamma+P proves A iff C(Sigma)+Gamma proves A.               (2)

Here theories are ordinary H-closures. Their additional axioms are not
silently necessitated. In particular the same theorem holds for the
requested smaller package OS + Actuality + Relational Choice + Zorn.

The semantic statement is stronger and parameter-sensitive. Every pointed
set-sized C action model K has a new pointed C action model R satisfying
(1), together with maps q_sigma:R_sigma -> K_sigma that are onto at EVERY
admitted type and commute with EVERY typed term. Under compatible
parameter assignments,

  R satisfies A implies K satisfies A                 for A in E+;
  K satisfies N implies R satisfies N                 for N in D+.   (3)

Thus one root preserves an entire D+ theory at once and reflects every
E+ failure of its target. The proof-theoretic theorem needs only ordinary
action-model completeness for a single C-consistent formula; the finite
proof argument below handles arbitrary infinite Gamma. There is no
completeness assertion about full standard higher-order models.

The novelty here is adding full OS to the earlier positive-equational
conservation theorem, with the stronger Strong Actuality package and an
explicit audit of all-type surjectivity, unrestricted terms, constants,
and parameters. The preservation induction itself is elementary once the
appropriate observer source exists.

2. THE INTERPRETATION ROOT, WITH AN ONTO BASELINE

Start with the underlying C action model of K, retaining its pointed
object V. Choose an infinite cardinal kappa large enough for all domains
at all old objects and for Sigma. At each type sigma take a set N_sigma
of auxiliary names, with sufficiently many spare names that every old
domain has an onto interpretation of N_sigma with infinite fibres.
These names are construction data, not additional constants in Sigma.

Adjoin a new object o with only its identity endomorphism. For every old
object W and every typed interpretation I of the auxiliary names at W,
add an arrow I:o->W. No other arrow enters o. Composition with an old
arrow k sends I to kI, interpreted name by name using old transport.
Allow ALL interpretations, not just the onto ones. This guarantees closure
under arbitrary old transports, which need not be onto or injective.

The root individuals are N_e. A root relational entity F is an intension
with an arbitrary identity row and old rows subject to two conditions:

  finite pinning: for some finite typed name set S, old rows into the same
  W agree whenever their interpretations agree on S;

  landing: the ENTIRE transported section along every I:o->W belongs to
  W's original domain of the same type.

Domains are defined recursively on type. No old domain, arrow, logical
operation or pure-language interpretation is changed.

For a relational name c define hat(c) by an empty identity row and the
current extension of I(c) at every old arrow I. For individual names put
hat(c)=c. Then, at every admitted type,

                       rho_I(hat(c)) = I(c).                       (4)

For relational names this is equality of WHOLE old intensions: at every
future k:W->U, the row of the left side is the current row of (kI)(c),
which is precisely the k-row of I(c). Thus hat(c) lands and is pinned by
{c}; it is not merely an entity matching I(c)'s current extension.

Choose a baseline interpretation b:o->V that is onto on names at every
type, with infinite fibres. For any y in K_sigma, choose a name c with
b(c)=y. Equation (4) gives rho_b(hat(c))=y. Hence

                 q_sigma := rho_b is onto at every type.           (5)

This includes e, all higher relational sorts, and domains not externally
full as function spaces. Only the auxiliary names are chosen freely;
the old Henkin domains remain exactly as they were.

3. TERM PRESERVATION: THE WHOLE-INTENSION CHECK

For every typed term u of type tau, every root valuation a, and every
old arrow I:o->W,

       rho_I(u^R(a)) = u^W(rho_I(a)).                              (6)

The right side uses the old model's own term interpretation, including
its own quantified domains and lambda interpretation. Root signature
constants are treated as additional parameters until section 5 fixes
their lifts. Here is the structural check, including the delicate cases.

Variables and individual names satisfy (6) by definition. Application
commutes with action transport. Boolean operations are interpreted row
by row and so commute as well.

For a lambda term lambda x.A, inspect an arbitrary future k of W and an
arbitrary tuple of arguments in its target U. The transported root
lambda has there the extension obtained by interpreting A in U with the
free parameters transported along kI and with that arbitrary local
argument tuple. This is exactly the k-row of W's own denotation of
lambda x.A. The arguments need not be images of root arguments. Checking
all such k and tuples proves equality of the whole intensions in (6),
not just equality on the actual image of rho_I.

For a quantified proposition term forall x^sigma A, its row at I is

       [for every x in D_sigma(W), A is true in W
        with free parameters rho_I(a)].

At a future k the same clause uses ALL of D_sigma(U). It is therefore
exactly W's old quantified term denotation, with its full future profile.
Existential quantification is the dual case. This is NOT obtained by
moving a quantifier across the current map rho_I, and it requires no
surjectivity of I. Surjectivity is used later only for the chosen baseline
and the outer preservation induction.

For an identity-valued subterm s=t, its row at I is the actual identity
test between s^W(rho_I(a)) and t^W(rho_I(a)); its rows at kI give the
corresponding old future tests. This is exactly W's identity-valued term.
An old transport can identify objects distinct at the root: the identity
proposition then changes truth along that arrow. Nothing here assumes
ND, crisp identity across worlds, or injectivity of transport. Actual
identity at each object is literal identity of that object's entities.

These same clauses verify root term closure. If the free parameters have
finite pins whose union is S, agreement on S identifies their whole
transports: after every k, the transported interpretations still agree on
S. Old term denotations therefore agree, and the new denotation is pinned
by S. Its old sections land by (6), because old terms are admitted. Its
root identity row is unrestricted and uses the ordinary truth clauses
over the recursively defined root domains.

For completeness about C's theorem gate: H is valid at each object under
all local assignments, with the displayed application, quantifier, identity,
and beta-eta clauses. If H proves A iff B, those truth values agree at
every old target and at the root, for arbitrary local arguments. The
corresponding abstractions have identical full profiles. Thus C's
H-theorem-gated Logical Equivalence holds. H-closure gives C. No equivalence
derived only from optional root assumptions is used for this step.

4. THE ROOT'S STRUCTURAL PRINCIPLES

Full OS. Every finite modification of an infinite-fibre onto baseline is
still onto at all types. Given any old interpretation I and any finite pin
set S, such an onto interpretation b' with the same target can be chosen
to agree with I on S.

For each selected onto b':o->W define E_b' as follows. Its identity row
reads the proposition at b'. Its row at an arbitrary old h:o->U is the
current truth test on the proposition input in U. Consequently the WHOLE
transport of E_b' along every old arrow is U's identity observer. The old
rows are independent of the name interpretation, so E_b' is empty-pinned
and lands; its arbitrary root row is allowed.

Boolean preservation at the root follows by reading a truth value. For a
typed predicate F, quantifier preservation follows by reindexing:

 E_b'(forall x F x) is true at o
   iff every y in D_sigma(W) satisfies (rho_b' F)(y)
   iff every x in D_sigma(o) satisfies (rho_b' F)(rho_b' x)
   iff forall x E_b'(F x) is true at o.

The middle equivalence uses (5). The existential case is dual. At every
old future E_b' is the whole identity, so the same preservation equations
hold there for all newly available inputs. Therefore these are identities
of the displayed OPERATIONS, not mere current pointwise equivalences.
The reader meets every finite typed qualification list simultaneously.

If X and Y differ on a current tuple, the identity observer separates
them. Otherwise they differ at an old interpretation I on a tuple in
its target. Match I on the union of their pins with a selected onto b',
and lift the distinguishing tuple along b'. The reader E_b' separates
the lifted applications. This proves full relational OS, not just its
propositional instance. In fact every reader is whole-idempotent: its old
transport is identity, so reading E_b'(p) at b' gives the same bit as
reading p there; all old rows are identity. Hence idempotent qualified
observers already suffice for all the separation witnesses in this model.

Strong Actuality. The proposition w with only the identity row true is
admitted and true. At o it entails each currently true proposition, and
entails the negation of each currently false proposition. Along every old
arrow its transport is Bottom, which entails every newly available
proposition and its negation. Hence

                 w and Box forall p (w<=p or w<=not p)

holds at o. Thus the root satisfies Strong Actuality, strengthening the
Actuality claim in the underlying cover construction.

Full current relational representation. For every external relation on
any finite tuple of root typed domains, take that relation as identity
row and take all old rows empty. This is empty-pinned, lands as Bottom,
and is an admitted relation. With ordinary external ZFC choice, every
current serial relation therefore has an admitted selecting subrelation;
every current equivalence relation has an admitted transversal; and each
typed domain has an admitted current well-order. These prove all-type
RC, TC and Well-Ordering, including individual outputs for RC.

For the map's exact preorder Zorn axiom, quotient a current preorder by
mutual comparability. Every quotient chain has a set of representatives,
an antisymmetric chain for the original preorder. Relational fullness
represents this set by a current predicate. The internal hypothesis gives
an upper bound for it, hence for the quotient chain. External Zorn gives
a maximal quotient class; any representative has the required preorder
maximality. Thus the precise native all-type Zorn schema holds.

Current BF follows independently by the same finite-pin approximation.
If Diamond exists x F x has a witness at an old arrow I, match I on F's
pin with an onto b', lift the witness, and obtain a current x satisfying
Diamond F x. An identity-row witness needs no approximation. This proves
the dual BF form at each type. No necessary BF claim follows: old failing
objects remain unchanged. Likewise none of the choice conclusions is
silently boxed, and no Boolean Completeness premise was introduced.

5. FIXED SIGNATURES AND PARAMETERS

For each official c in Sigma choose a root preimage of c^K under q at its
type. Give c this interpretation at o. Equation (6) at the chosen baseline
b now holds for every term of the SAME language L(Sigma):

                   q_tau(u^R(a)) = u^K(q(a)).                     (7)

If desired, choose distinct auxiliary names for the official symbols,
prescribe their baseline values to be c^K, and reserve a disjoint spare
name supply for surjectivity and finite modifications. This works for
arbitrary set-sized Sigma; no countability restriction is needed.

Only the designated baseline must match K's official constant
interpretations. Other interpretation arrows may give those parameters
different values. We do not claim to preserve K's fixed Sigma-valuation
along every incoming interpretation arrow. Along b and every continuation
kb it is exactly the original valuation and its old transport.

For free parameters choose compatible root preimages of their assigned
K-values. This is an external valuation choice, not an expansion of Sigma
or a change to the reference theory in a Maximalism schema. Repeated
occurrences use the same chosen preimage.

6. PRESERVATION AND THE CONSERVATIVITY PROOF

For E+, prove the first implication in (3) by induction. Actual typed
identity of denotations in R is preserved by the map q and equation (7).
Conjunction and disjunction use ordinary current truth clauses. An
existential witness maps to a K-witness. For a universal formula, take
any K-object, choose a root preimage using (5), and apply induction.
This argument covers arbitrary quantifier alternations and every sort.

The D+ implication is the dual induction. Unequal target denotations
cannot have equal root preimages. A target universal formula applies
to the image of every root object; a target existential witness lifts
using (5). Alternatively negate an E+ formula and put only its OUTER
connectives into de Morgan form. Internal terms stay unrestricted.

For a semantic countermodel K of Gamma and not A, (3) makes its root a
countermodel of Gamma+P and not A. Every D+ sentence in Gamma transfers
simultaneously, however large Gamma is.

For the exact syntactic statement (2), suppose an enlarged-theory proof
of A exists but C(Sigma)+Gamma does not prove A. The proof uses finitely
many assumptions Gamma_0 from Gamma. The single formula

                     (conjunction Gamma_0) and not A

is C(Sigma)-consistent, so ordinary action-model completeness supplies a
pointed action countermodel K. The root satisfies Gamma_0 and the entire
package P but falsifies A, contradicting soundness of that finite proof.
This needs only action completeness for one formula; it does not assume
that full standard models are complete, or that arbitrary C models are
identical to their action representations. The converse in
(2) is immediate from inclusion of theories.

The same proof applies to finite parameter tuples and deductions from
D+ assumptions under a valuation. For a sentence-level formulation, take
the appropriate universal closure of a conclusion or existentially close
the finite counterexample diagram. These outer quantifiers stay within
the same two classes. No stronger quantifier-free restriction is needed.

7. CONSEQUENCES AND EXACT SCOPE

Consistency is preserved: if C(Sigma)+Gamma is consistent, so is its
extension by P. Otherwise (2), applied to the false identity Top=Bottom,
would make the original theory inconsistent.

Every necessary consequence is preserved, since Box B is the identity
B=Top with arbitrary B. The theorem also covers such conclusions as

  forall x exists y (Box A(x,y) or (f(x,y)=g(x,y) and Box B(x,y))),

with admitted terms and types. The right side of the conservation claim
retains exactly those boxes and quantifiers; it is not a claim merely
that the original theory proves an unboxed version.

D+ premises include arbitrary positive quantified combinations of
possibility assertions, because Diamond B is B!=Bottom. Pure and fixed-
signature Maximalist DISTINCTNESS axioms are also D+ sentences. Therefore,
whenever such a Max theory is consistent, adjoining the full current
package P preserves its consistency and all its E+ consequences, without
altering the reference theory used in the Max schema.

This is stronger than closed-pure-equational conservativity: it allows
arbitrary signatures, parameters, positive Boolean combinations, mixed
quantifiers, and arbitrary negative-equational background theories. It
does not assert conservation of all formulas. In particular OS supplies
current BF and the package supplies current choice and strong actuality;
their equality-to-Top assertions are a separate matter. Conditional
equations are outside the preserved outer grammar unless independently
equivalent to an E+ formula. The theorem does not claim their conservation.

The semantic scope is arbitrary pointed C ACTION models, which suffice
for all the stated syntactic conclusions. No C5, Barcan, Boolean
Completeness, external completeness of the old proposition algebra,
fullness of old higher domains, or old-arrow surjectivity is required.
External ZFC choice is used for the size/name choices and the current
selection and maximality arguments.
