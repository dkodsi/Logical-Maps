Exact target and language

The upstream ID is necessity-of-analysis. The snapshot is
 a89a5058ebbd5ebe93aec07d47a1a32e3b93f8bb.
Its Background, Analysis, defines real structures at an admitted type sigma
using a carrier X, order, ternary sum and product graphs, 0, 1, and a predicate
N for the least inductive subproperty. Completeness is for NONEMPTY bounded
subproperties. First-order analytical formulas quantify only over elements
of X and use those relations and element identity. They do not quantify over
arbitrary relations on X. In particular N is included.

There is a parameter-free sentence A_L in exactly this first-order analytical
language such that, in a standard real field in any ZFC universe W,

  W |= A_L(R,+,times,<,0,1,N)
       iff W |= every subset of omega belongs to L.

Thus a real-adding forcing extension of a ground universe satisfying V=L
changes A_L from true to false. This is a first-order analytical witness;
it is not a CH witness and is not a sentence in the pure real-closed-field
language.

The descriptive-set-theoretic input is that the constructible subsets of
omega have a parameter-free Sigma^1_2 definition. See Boban Velickovic and
W. Hugh Woodin, Complexity of the reals of inner models of set theory,
arXiv:math/9501203v1, 2 January 1995, Introduction, p. 1. The paper explicitly
states the usual Sigma^1_2 definition of the constructible reals. The
parameter-free form comes from coding the constructible hierarchy without
an oracle parameter; no distinguished real is supplied to the sentence.

https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9501203

Fix such a second-order-arithmetic formula

  Constr(U) := exists V forall W theta(U,V,W),

where U,V,W range over subsets of omega and theta is arithmetical. Equally,
real function variables can first be coded as subsets of omega by coding
ordered pairs. The standard definition codes a countable well-founded
piece of the constructible hierarchy containing U: the well-foundedness
check is Pi^1_1 and the existential code gives Sigma^1_2. The cited standard
definability theorem is the input here; this note does not replace it by
an inadequately specified 'countable model of ZFC' assertion.

Consequently

  forall U Constr(U)

has a Pi^1_3 presentation, and its negation has a Sigma^1_3 presentation.
This names a sufficient projective presentation, not a claim of an
absolute minimal complexity in every universe.

Explicit real coding, with no digit-endpoint ambiguity

Here is a direct interpretation of second-order arithmetic in the required
first-order language. For a subset S of omega set

  c_S = sum over n>=0 of (1 + chi_S(n)) / 4^(n+1).

This is ordinary external mathematics explaining the interpretation, not
an infinitary expression added to the object language. Digits 1 and 2 in
base 4 avoid the ambiguity caused by terminating expansions. The tail
after the nth digit, multiplied by 4^(n+1), lies between 1/3 and 2/3.
Thus floor(4^(n+1) c_S) has final base-4 digit 1+chi_S(n).

Everything in the following definitions is first-order definable using
the field operations and N. Quantifiers displayed as over N are field
quantifiers restricted by N. Define Exp4(n,p) over N by the arithmetical
formula for a finite sequence beginning with 1 and repeatedly multiplying
by 4, ending at p after n steps. Finite sequences are coded by natural
numbers, for example with the standard Godel beta coding. This uses no
set quantifier, extra sort, exponentiation primitive, or infinite formula.

For d equal to the numeral 1 or 2, define

  Digit(c,n,d) iff
    exists p,k,q in N,
      Exp4(n+1,p) and k <= p*c < k+1 and k = 4*q+d.

Define

  Code(c) iff 0<c<1 and
    forall n in N, (Digit(c,n,1) or Digit(c,n,2));

  Bit(c,n) iff Digit(c,n,2).

Then S |-> c_S is a bijection between subsets of N and Code-reals, and
Bit(c_S,n) iff n belongs to S.

Proof of the converse, which matters for universal set quantifiers:
If Code(c), let k_n=floor(4^(n+1)c) and d_n=k_n mod 4. Since 0<c<1,
the initial integer part is zero. The floor recursion gives the base-4
prefix with digits d_0,...,d_n, each 1 or 2. The error between c and that
prefix is less than 4^(-n-1), so c is the real coded by the set of n with
d_n=2. If two sets first differ at n, their contribution at n has magnitude
4^(-n-1), while the total possible later difference is only
(1/3)*4^(-n-1); hence their codes differ. This proves surjectivity and
injectivity without making a choice of decimal expansions.

Syntactic translation

Translate number variables to field variables restricted by N. Translate
set variables U to field variables c_U restricted by Code. Replace
membership n in U by Bit(c_U,n). Replace equality of sets, if present,
by agreement of their bits for every n in N. Preserve Boolean connectives.
For the set quantifiers use

  (forall U phi)* = forall c_U (Code(c_U) -> phi*),
  (exists U phi)* = exists c_U (Code(c_U) and phi*).

Numerical addition, multiplication, order and finite-sequence coding use
N and the field operations. An induction on formulas proves that a
second-order-arithmetic sentence and its translation have the same truth
value in the full standard structures of each universe W. The bijection
just established proves both quantified steps, including the existence
of codes for ALL subsets of N in W, not just ground-model subsets.

A_L is the translation of forall U Constr(U). It is one finite formula.
After translation all bound variables are field elements. Its apparently
higher-order strength comes from coding subsets of N by real elements;
there are no quantifiers over higher-type predicates in A_L itself.

The database uses ternary graphs rather than operations. Eliminate every
sum/product term by the usual introduction of fresh field variables and
the graph atoms +(x,y,z) or times(x,y,z). Their totality and uniqueness on
a real structure justify this syntactic elimination. Numerals use 0, 1
and iterated addition; strict inequality abbreviates <= with inequality.
The result has exactly the native analytical grammar. No ambient
sigma-to-sigma operation, especially no e-valued function, is assumed.

Forcing calculation and a useful CH-preserving specialization

Let M satisfy ZFC+V=L and let G be generic for Add(omega,1). Work either
with a transitive-ground-model presentation or with the corresponding
Boolean-valued calculation internally to the ground metatheory. Forcing
preserves ordinals, so the constructible hierarchy of M[G] is the ground
constructible hierarchy. The generic subset of omega is not in M, hence
not in L of M[G]. Therefore

  M |= A_L,
  M[G] |= not A_L.

This needs a forcing which genuinely adds a real, not merely one which
changes a cardinality. More generally any real-adding forcing over M=L
has the same effect. Over a ground model already containing nonconstructible
reals, this particular sentence is false before forcing and cannot witness
a change; the V=L hypothesis must remain explicit.

One Cohen real is sufficient. It preserves CH over L: the forcing is
countable and ccc, preserves omega_1, and there are at most
(2^aleph_0)^(aleph_0)=aleph_1 nice names for reals in the CH ground model.
The old reals still give at least aleph_1 reals. Thus both M and M[G]
satisfy CH, while they disagree on A_L.

For the proposed B=2 x A construction one may therefore take
A=RO(Add(omega,1)). In the ground model |A|=2^aleph_0=aleph_1: a countable
base supplies the upper bound, and joins of arbitrary subsets of a
countably infinite antichain supply the lower bound. The ground carrier
B therefore has enough elements for a real field both in M and in M[G],
since CH and omega_1 are preserved. The parent model argument must still
supply the Boolean-valued real structure on this carrier and show that
its quantifier ranges represent all needed subsets internally. Once that
transfer is proved, this specialization separates analytical invariance
from CH invariance in the particularly strong way that CH remains true
in both coordinates.

Scope and remaining model obligation

This note settles the language issue and the changing truth value. It
requires no added infinitary conjunction and no parameters in A_L. It
uses the external ZFC/constructibility/forcing metatheory explicitly.
It does not claim that C5 itself has a canonical internal L predicate,
nor that the formula quantifies over sets in the Classicism language.

To finish the native non-entailment argument, separately verify that the
chosen full Boolean-valued typed construction validates the fixed C
framework, necessary-distinctness-necessary-r, functional-choice-r at the
specified evaluation point, and the exact completeness/quantifier
interpretation used for Real_sigma. In particular, a real of M[G] must
be represented by an element of a real structure there; presenting only
the old ground reals would fail completeness and would not suffice.
Do not infer boxed Functional Choice from its truth at the isolated atom.
