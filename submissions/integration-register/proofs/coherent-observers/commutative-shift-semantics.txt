1. THE REPRESENTATION THEOREMS

C5 + CoSep has a sound and strongly complete semantics using:

  a commutative monoid M;
  a Boolean algebra B of subsets of M;
  ordinary Henkin function domains;
  admitted shifts and an admitted predicate selecting their family.

Equality is crisp, quantifiers are literal intersections/unions, and native
Box is the test A=M. Every C5+CoSep Henkin model, after taking its current
identity quotients, has an ISOMORPHIC representation of this form. No
elementary extension is needed for this C5 theorem.

For the countable project language, strong completeness already holds with
one fixed index monoid: the finite-support sequences of natural numbers,
with coordinatewise addition. For CoIdemSep one may instead fix the finite
subsets of N, with union. These index sets are external semantic data, not
new logical sorts or internally admitted enumerations.

Over bare C, the same commutative shift idea supplies strong completeness
for the full CoSep schema, but identity may be noncrisp and an elementary
extension handles the finite qualification lists. Section 8 states the
separate argument and does not transfer the C5 simplifications to bare C.

The admitted-family requirement is essential to the definition. Merely
making each individual shift an admitted operation does not establish the
existence of an object-language predicate collecting them.

2. C5 MODEL DATA

Work in ordinary ZFC metatheory and the fixed relational type system.
Specify a commutative monoid (M,*,1), a nonempty D_e, and a Boolean
subalgebra B of P(M). Put D_t=B. At each allowed arrow type specify a
nonempty set of actual functions

  D_(sigma tau) subseteq (D_tau)^(D_sigma).

Application is evaluation. Impose the following ordinary Henkin closures:

(H) All permitted parameterized lambda interpretations and the Boolean
    operations belong to their indicated domains.

(Q) For each admitted F:D_sigma->B, its LITERAL intersection over D_sigma
    belongs to B; the quantifier map Q_sigma itself is admitted. Existential
    quantifiers are literal unions, obtained by Boolean duality.

(=) For every object a at every type sigma, its crisp singleton predicate
    x |-> M if x=a, empty otherwise, belongs to D_(sigma t).

(S) For every m in M, its shift

      R_m(A)={n in M:n*m belongs to A}

    belongs to D_(tt). This includes closure of B under that inverse image.

(K) There is K in D_((tt)t) whose truth fibre at 1 is exactly the shifts:

      1 in K(E) iff E=R_m for some m in M.                       (1)

No endpoint-valuedness of K is stipulated. Formula (1) is a condition on
the semantic data, not an internal quantifier over M. Distinct indices may
induce the same shift, as happens after the fixed-monoid reduction below.

Boolean operations are set operations. Leibniz equality is interpreted by

  delta_sigma(a,b)=intersection_(P in D_(sigma t)) (P(a) iff P(b)),

where iff denotes Boolean set equivalence. Q and lambda closure make this
an admitted proposition and operation. Condition (=) gives

  delta_sigma(a,b)=M if a=b, empty otherwise.                     (2)

Truth is membership of 1. Native necessity is defined as in C, so

  Box A=delta_t(A,M)=M if A=M, empty otherwise.                   (3)

It is NOT defined by the monoid's forward orbit from an index. Even when
M has an absorbing element, native necessity in these C5 models is the
global test (3). Observation routes and native modal accessibility are
different structures.

3. SOUNDNESS

The Boolean and quantifier clauses have their ordinary classical truth
conditions at every index. Actual functions supply beta, eta, application
congruence and Functionality. Crisp equality supplies H's identity rules.
Thus H theorems have value M under every assignment. An H-theorem
equivalence gives identical proposition sets, so its lambda abstractions
are literally identical actual functions. This verifies precisely C's
fixed H-theoremhood gate. Equation (2) gives all boxed ND instances: C5.

Every R_m is fully qualified. Inverse image preserves Boolean operations,
and for every admitted predicate F,

  R_m(intersection_a F(a))=intersection_a R_m(F(a)).              (4)

Lambda closure admits a |-> R_m(F(a)); hence (4) is exactly the typed
quantifier identity. These equalities hold for every law-input, so the
whole operations are literally identical. The existential law follows
dually. Commutativity gives R_m o R_n=R_n o R_m.

If propositions A and B differ, choose m in their symmetric difference.
Their observed values under R_m differ in truth at 1. By (1), R_m is a
current K-member. Distinct relational functions differ at an argument
tuple; the same propositional argument separates their resulting values.
Thus K witnesses every current CoSep instance, with all qualifications.

If M is idempotent, every R_m is idempotent and the same proof gives
CoIdemSep. Nothing in this soundness proof asserts separation at every
index. The isolated-point countermodel shows that assertion would be false.

4. DIRECT REPRESENTATION OF AN ARBITRARY C5+CoSep MODEL

Start with any general Henkin model A of C5+CoSep. Take current identity
quotients. C makes its proposition domain a Boolean algebra, and C5's
Functionality makes arrow objects faithful actual functions on current
domains. Current quantifiers retain the classical truth clauses.

By object-language-and-finite-basis.txt, Section 2, choose ONE admitted
predicate K whose current members separate propositions, commute, and
preserve Q_t and Boolean operations. C5 upgrades EACH SAME member to
qualification at every type. Replace K by its internal monoid closure
from Section 4 of that note. Separation, qualification and commutation
are preserved; I and composition are now included.

Let M be the external set of its current members, with multiplication
given by whole-operation composition. It is a commutative monoid of
admitted operations. For each proposition p define

  phi(p)={m in M : m(p) is actually true in A}.                  (5)

Every m is a Boolean endomorphism, so phi preserves all Boolean operations.
Separation makes phi injective. Set B=phi[D_t]. This is a Boolean subalgebra
of P(M). Current truth is preserved because I belongs to M:

  1 in phi(p) iff p is actually true.                           (6)

For every admitted F and m in M, whole quantifier preservation and current
quantifier truth give

  m(Q_sigma F) is true iff forall a in D_sigma, m(F(a)) is true.

Consequently

  phi(Q_sigma F)=intersection_(a in D_sigma) phi(F(a)).          (7)

Transport every higher domain recursively along phi at t and identity at e:
if j_sigma and j_tau have been defined, send f to

  j_tau o f o inverse(j_sigma).

These are typewise bijections onto the new ordinary-function domains.
All application, lambda and logical operations are transported, and (7)
gives the required literal quantifier intersections. This is an isomorphism
of the entire current typed structure, preserving all formulas, rather
than just an embedding of the Boolean algebra.

For any m in M its transported operation is exactly a right shift:

  phi(m(p))={n:n(m(p)) is true}=R_m(phi(p)).                     (8)

The transported K belongs to its transported higher domain. By (6), its
current truth fibre consists EXACTLY of the transported current members
of K, namely the R_m. This proves (K), not merely individual shift admission.

C5 makes every identity proposition in A an endpoint: NI treats true
identities and ND treats false ones. Therefore lambda x.(x=a) is its crisp
singleton predicate and transports to (=). All the data conditions hold.

If the original family was idempotent, use its idempotent monoid closure.
Then M is a commutative idempotent monoid. Thus the direct representation
also holds for C5+CoIdemSep.

5. STRONG COMPLETENESS AND THE FIXED COUNTABLE MONOIDS

Soundness and Section 4 give, for any set Gamma of sentences,

  Gamma |-_(C5+CoSep) A
    iff every C5 commutative shift model of Gamma satisfies A.   (9)

For the right-to-left direction, a nonderivation has a general Henkin
countermodel by the usual many-sorted Henkin construction for the fixed
language and C gate. Apply the direct representation. The C5+CoIdemSep
version restricts M to commutative idempotent monoids.

In the project's countable language, choose a countable Henkin countermodel.
Then M, a subset of its tt-domain, is countable. Enumerate M by m_0,m_1,...,
allowing repetitions. Let F=N^(N) be the finitely supported sequences of
natural numbers. The assignment of its k-th generator to m_k extends to
a surjective homomorphism pi:F->M. Replace each A subset M in B by pi^-1(A)
and transport all higher domains along this bijection onto the new B.

Surjectivity preserves distinctness; inverse image preserves every literal
intersection, union and Boolean operation. For each u in F,

  R_u(pi^-1(A))=pi^-1(R_(pi(u))(A)).                            (10)

Thus all new shifts are admitted. The transported K selects exactly their
set at 0, the monoid identity, since pi is onto. All satisfaction statements
are unchanged. This proves strong completeness with the ONE fixed monoid
(N^(N),+,0). The fixed-monoid class need not separate every distinct
external index by an admitted proposition.

For idempotent M, replace F by Fin(N), the finite subsets of N under union,
and let pi(S) be the product of the m_k for k in S. The same proof applies.
No internal map from an index TYPE onto the observer family is assumed.
This distinction matters: internal uniform idempotent indexing can imply
Atomicity, while the predicate-family principle has atomless models.

6. WHAT IS GAINED, AND WHAT IS RETAINED

OS already permits ordinary function domains, literal intersections and
admitted shifts of a monoid. CoSep permits the indexing monoid to be
COMMUTATIVE, and requires its shifts to have an admitted family predicate.
CoIdemSep permits an idempotent commutative monoid, equivalently a
join-semilattice with a least element (the identity).

The crucial C5 improvement is direct representation: one finite formula
provides a family whose members qualify at every type. There is no
compactness extension to produce uniformly qualified witnesses. Equality
and native necessity also have the simple explicit forms (2)-(3).

The higher domains are still Henkin domains, with genuine closure and
family-admission requirements. Replacing them by full function spaces
would change the theory. The theorem is not completeness for full standard
models, an algorithm for computing a representation, or a claim that
monoid multiplication alone determines the whole higher-order model.

7. WHY PROPAGATION NEED NOT HOLD IN THESE MODELS

At the identity, the shifts inspect every index of M. At another index n,
the same shifts inspect only nM. Commutativity does not imply nM=M, nor
that nM meets every nonempty admitted proposition. A separating family
at 1 can therefore fail to separate at n.

This explains a possible mechanism; by itself it does not exclude OTHER
admitted witnesses at n. isolated-point-nonpropagation.txt closes that
obligation. Its current rigid family has monoid

  (direct sum over J of C_2) with an absorbing zero adjoined.

The zero is the constant reader of the isolated point. From a binary world
the flip orbit detects every nonempty clopen of the binary component and
the zero detects the isolated atom. At zero, the whole monoid stays at zero.
The support/ultrafilter argument excludes every alternative Boolean reader
there. Thus that note gives an actual C5 countermodel to propagation, not
just a failure of one convenient witness family.

8. THE BARE-C VERSION, WITH ITS DIFFERENT COMPLETENESS CLAIM

For a commutative shift model of C+CoSep drop crisp-singleton condition (=).
Instead require admitted predicates to distinguish distinct individuals
in truth at 1. Retain H,Q,S,K. Define equality by the displayed Leibniz
intersection, and native Box by delta_t(A,M).

Shifts distinguish distinct propositions at 1; induction through actual
function domains distinguishes distinct relational objects there. Together
with individual separation this makes equality literal at 1, though it
may identify distinct raw objects at other indices. The substitution,
quantifier and H-gate argument of the earlier OS shift theorem still works.
Commutativity and K then give full current CoSep exactly as in Section 3.
No crisp-Box or C5 assertion follows in this version.

Conversely begin with any C+full CoSep Henkin model. OS follows, so BF and
Functionality supply its ordinary-function presentation. In its elementary
diagram add ONE new predicate constant K. Require propositional separation,
pairwise commutation, I membership, composition closure, Boolean qualification
of all members, and Q_sigma qualification for every external type sigma.
Every finite subset is satisfiable in the original model: choose one
CoSep_(t,Delta) instance containing the finitely requested types and apply
the INTERNAL monoid closure construction. Hence compactness gives an
elementary extension with such a K. This is one compactness step, not a
sequence choosing unrelated witnesses. All new propositions are covered
by the universal separation axiom for K in the extension.

Now use (5)-(8). Every current K-member qualifies at every type, so the
entire typed representation and admitted-family condition follow. For an
idempotent family include its idempotence in the diagram and use the
idempotent closure lemma. This proves strong completeness for the bare-C
full schemas using the corresponding commutative/semilattice shift models.
In a countable language the extension can be chosen countable, so the same
fixed-monoid reductions apply. Isomorphic representation is of the
ELEMENTARY EXTENSION in this paragraph, not necessarily the starting model.
