1. THE PRINCIPLE OVER THE FIXED BASE C

An observer is an object E:tt. A family is an admitted predicate K:(tt)t.
Write I=lambda p.p and E o F=lambda p.E(Fp). Application in the formulas
below is explicitly parenthesized when needed.

For a finite external list Delta of types, Logical_Delta(E) is the
conjunction of these WHOLE-OPERATION identities:

  (lambda p.E(not p)) = (lambda p.not(Ep));
  (lambda p,q.E(p and q)) = (lambda p,q.Ep and Eq);

and, for each sigma in Delta,

  (lambda F:sigma t.E(forall x:sigma.Fx))
    = (lambda F:sigma t.forall x:sigma.E(Fx)).

Over C these clauses are equivalent to the established Good_Delta:
disjunction and existential preservation follow from the unconditional
De Morgan identities and substitution into the displayed whole identities.
Boolean preservation supplies the endpoint and translated identity laws.
This reduction is not asserted over bare H.

For tau=sigma_1 ... sigma_n t define

  Separates_tau(K) :=
    forall X,Y:tau.
      [forall E:tt. KE -> forall x_1:sigma_1 ... x_n:sigma_n.
        (E(X[xbar]) iff E(Y[xbar]))] -> X=Y.

When tau=t the argument tuple is empty. The principle is

  CoSep_(tau,Delta) := exists K:(tt)t.
       [forall E:tt. KE -> Logical_Delta(E)]
   and [forall E,F:tt. KE and KF -> E o F = F o E]
   and Separates_tau(K).                                      (1)

The full CoSep theory consists of these finitary sentences for each
relational tau and each finite Delta. There is no object-language type
quantification and no conjunction over infinitely many types.

Plain English: an internally available family of logical operations
commute pairwise and collectively distinguish every distinct relation.
Separation is actual truth-value disagreement; commutation and logical
preservation are identities of operations. These roles must not be swapped.

Membership KE is current membership. The principle does not require K to
have endpoint values, or to be supplied as the image of an operation
i |-> E_i. Those are additional conditions and cannot silently be imposed.
CoIdemSep adds forall E.(KE -> E o E=E).

2. OVER C5, ONE FINITE SENTENCE SUFFICES

Theorem. Over C5, the full CoSep schema is equivalent to
CoSep_(t,{t}). The SAME K witnesses all relational output types and all
finite qualification lists. Likewise for CoIdemSep.

The forward implication is an instance. Conversely, the observer
quantifier-reduction theorem says that over C5 each Boolean Q_t observer
preserves every admitted typed quantifier. It changes neither the observer
nor its qualification at the original type. Consequently each current
K-member qualifies at every requested finite list, retaining commutation
and, if required, idempotence.

C5 supplies Functionality at all types. If X!=Y at a relational output
type, repeated use of its contrapositive supplies an argument tuple with
X[xbar]!=Y[xbar] as propositions. Propositional separation supplies a
current K-member distinguishing those values. This proves the full claim.

For clarity, the quantifier-reduction argument is not an assumption of
standard higher domains. For F:sigma t form its admitted range predicate
I_F(p):=exists x:sigma.Fx=p. Under C5, identity is endpoint-valued and BF
makes I_F(p) endpoint-valued too. The observer therefore fixes I_F(p).
Rewrite forall x.Fx as forall p.(I_F(p)->p); use Q_t and Boolean laws;
rewrite back to forall x.E(Fx). The persistent-premise/Intensionality
argument in the source upgrades this to the required whole Q_sigma
identity. This is why the SAME observer qualifies at all types.

Thus the object-language principle relevant to the C5 research is one
finite sentence using only t, tt, and (tt)t. Individual types and all
higher relational instances are recovered from the base and this sentence.

3. AN EVEN SHORTER QUALIFICATION OVER C5

Under C5, Logical_{t}(E) in (1) can be replaced by the TWO identities

  N(E): (lambda p.E(not p)) = (lambda p.not(Ep));
  Q(E): (lambda F:tt.E(forall p:t.Fp))
          = (lambda F:tt.forall p:t.E(Fp)).

Q_t already entails whole conjunction preservation over C+BF_t+.2,
and C5 supplies these premises. To see the essential calculation, C5
admits an endpoint selector, hence a proposition-indexed binary-range
family with designated values p and q. Its universal value is p and q;
the universal value of its E-image is Ep and Eq. Apply Q_t. NI and BF_t
upgrade the pointwise identities to the whole binary operation identity.
Negation supplies disjunction and endpoint laws by Boolean identities.

Accordingly a compact exact formulation over C5 is

  exists K:(tt)t.
       [forall E. KE -> N(E) and Q(E)]
   and [forall E,F. KE and KF -> E o F = F o E]
   and [forall p,q. (forall E. KE -> (Ep iff Eq)) -> p=q].    (2)

Over C5 Functionality also permits the displayed whole identities to be
written as universally quantified output IDENTITIES. Replacing '=' by
material equivalence would be a different condition.

4. INTERNAL MONOID FORM

One may additionally require K(I) and

  forall E,F. KE and KF -> K(E o F),

without changing CoSep. Define the monoid closure of a witness K by

  K*(E) := forall P:(tt)t.
    [PI and (forall F.KF -> PF)
        and (forall F,G.PF and PG -> P(F o G))] -> PE.

This admitted predicate contains I and K and is composition-closed.
For every fixed finite Delta, Logical_Delta is a composition-closed
predicate containing I and K; thus every K*-member is qualified.
For each k in K, the predicate of operations commuting with k contains
I and K and is composition-closed. Therefore K* commutes with K.
Repeat for a fixed member of K* to obtain pairwise commutation in K*.
Separation persists by inclusion. If K consists of idempotents, the
predicate K*(E) and E^2=E is closed under composition, now using mutual
commutation, so the same defining induction gives idempotence throughout.

This is internal higher-order induction over admitted predicates. It is
not an assertion that an external finite-word family is an admitted set.
