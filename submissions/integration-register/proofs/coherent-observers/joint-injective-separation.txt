1. THE QUESTION AND THE RESULTS

Can commuting separation and injective separation be witnessed together?
Write CoInjSep for CoSep with each current member required to be injective.
Write CoRevSep for the corresponding commuting family of qualified
reversible observers. These are working abbreviations, not new native
database records. The family is an admitted predicate K:(tt)t; membership
is current membership unless explicitly made stable.

Use CoSep_(tau,Delta) from object-language-and-finite-basis.txt, Section 1,
adding respectively

  forall E. KE -> forall p,q.(Ep=Eq -> p=q);                  (CoInjSep)

  forall E. KE -> exists F.
    Logical_Delta(F) and EF=I and FE=I.                     (CoRevSep)

Equations of operations are WHOLE identities. Full theories range over
relational output types and finite qualification lists. Over C5 the same
finite propositional/Q_t reduction applies, retaining the same K.

The original combination does not suffice:

  C5 + CoSep + InjSep does not imply CoInjSep.                (1)

The companion finite/cofinite model satisfies even necessary full Atomicity,
necessary full Rev and necessary full CoIdemSep, while no commuting
injective Boolean family separates. The exclusion holds even for external
families. See finite-cofinite-joint-separation.txt.

The positive result is:

  C + Actuality + full CoInjSep -> full CoRevSep.              (2)

The SAME family already contains a qualified inverse for each member.
No C5, necessity of Actuality, or Choice premise is needed. Under Actuality
this is an equivalence, since a two-sided inverse always gives injectivity.

In particular over C5+Atomicity, compatible injective separation is exactly
compatible reversible separation. Internal composition closure supplies an
abelian group acting regularly on world atoms: for any two atoms, exactly
one member carries one to the other. The joint condition thus describes
an admitted coherent system of world symmetries. Section 5 also verifies
it in ordinary full set models.

2. THE LOCAL INVERSE LEMMA OVER C

Assume C, Functionality at tt, and Actuality. Let K be an admitted family
of Boolean observers that currently commute, are injective, and separate
propositions. No quantifier-preservation law is needed for this lemma.

Choose an Actuality witness a. By the native definition,

  a is true, and for every p, p is true iff a<=p.              (3)

The reverse implication follows from factivity of algebraic entailment.
In particular a is nonzero. Fix a current member E of K. Injectivity and
preservation of Bottom make E(a) nonzero. Separation from Bottom supplies
a current F in K for which F(E(a)) is true. Put H=FE.

H is Boolean and H(a) is true. If p is true, (3) gives a<=p and therefore
H(a)<=H(p), so H(p) is true. If p is false, apply the same argument to
not-p and use H(not-p)=not H(p). Thus H preserves current truth on every
proposition. It commutes with every current member of K, since E and F do.

For any current G in K and any p, commutation gives G(Hp)=H(Gp). Truth
preservation by H makes this agree in truth with Gp. Separation gives
Hp=p. Functionality at tt collects these output identities into H=I.
Finally EF=FE by pairwise commutation, so

  FE=I=EF.                                                   (4)

The inverse F already belongs to the original family. Any logical
qualifications of its members therefore apply to F without a selection
or admission assumption. This proves the local lemma.

3. FULL SCHEMAS, INTERNAL GROUP CLOSURE AND NECESSITY

Full CoInjSep supplies OS and hence current Functionality, including at tt.
For each requested finite Delta, apply the local lemma to its separating
family. A family separating a relational type also separates propositions:
unequal p,q give unequal constant relations, and every tuple evaluates
those relations to p,q. The usual nonempty argument domains suffice.
The lemma retains the same family and its original qualifications, proving
the full-schema implication (2) with C's theoremhood gate unchanged.

Close K internally under composition using K* from
object-language-and-finite-basis.txt, Section 4. It contains identity,
remains qualified, commuting, injective and separating, and is admitted.
To check inverse closure, use the admitted predicate

  P(E) := exists F.[K*(F) and EF=FE=I].                       (5)

P contains I and K by Section 2. It is composition-closed: the inverse of
a product is the reverse product of its inverse witnesses, which belongs
to K*. The defining induction for K* makes every current K*-member satisfy
P. Thus its current members form an abelian group of admitted qualified
operations. This is internal predicate induction, not an assumption that
arbitrary external finite-word collections are admitted.

Equation (2) supplies full Rev. Its existing bootstrap yields C5, and its
No Pure Contingency theorem makes the closed pure Actuality and CoInjSep
instances necessary. Consequently Actuality+CoInjSep implies its own
necessary version over C. This propagation statement is a corollary of
the existing Rev theorem, not a new proof of pure-term invariance.

4. THE ATOMIC INTERPRETATION

Assume C5+Atomicity and CoInjSep. The existing C5 Atomicity theorem supplies
Actuality, so Sections 2-3 give the abelian group G of current K*-members.
Let a be the true atom. Every member of G is a Boolean automorphism and
therefore permutes the atoms.

For any atom b, separation from Bottom supplies E in G with E(b) true.
E(b) is an atom, so E(b)=a. Its inverse sends a to b, proving transitivity.
If E(a)=a, then a<=p iff a<=E(p) for every p, so E preserves current truth.
The argument of Section 2 gives E=I. The stabilizer of a is trivial;
transitivity makes every stabilizer trivial. Exactly one group member
therefore carries any given atom to any other.

This is a regular abelian action on the world atoms. Choosing a as origin
identifies those atoms with G. G comes from the admitted K*; no arbitrary
external group is being postulated as an internal object.

The direct commutative-shift representation now has a group as its index
monoid, and its proposition algebra contains every singleton. Indeed

  phi(p)={g in G : g(p) is currently true}.

Regularity gives phi(a)={I}; every other atom likewise represents one
singleton. Conversely, an abelian group shift model with all singleton
propositions and an admitted shift-family predicate supplies propositional
Atomicity and CoInjSep; the existing C5 type reduction supplies full
Atomicity. This is the atomic specialization of the earlier representation,
without elementary extension. Retain all the Henkin-domain and admitted-
family conditions in commutative-shift-semantics.txt.

5. ORDINARY FULL SET MODELS SUPPLY THE JOINT STRUCTURE

Work in ordinary ZFC metatheory. Let W be any nonempty set, D_t=P(W),
and take full actual-function domains at every permitted relational arrow,
with nonempty D_e. Boolean operations are set operations, quantifiers
literal intersections/unions, identity crisp, and truth membership of
a designated a in W.

Put an abelian group structure on W with a as identity. For finite W use
a cyclic group of its size. For infinite cardinal kappa=|W|, the direct sum
of kappa copies of the two-element group has cardinality kappa. Transport
its group structure along a bijection sending identity to a. This uses
ZFC in the metatheory; it is not an object-language Choice-free derivation.

Inverse images of translations are admitted bijective Boolean observers.
They preserve every typed quantifier because inverse image commutes with
arbitrary intersections. The endpoint predicate selecting their family is
admitted in the full domains. The observers commute and their transitivity
makes them separate at a. All these full set models therefore satisfy
CoInjSep, indeed stable CoRevSep.

Thus full availability of functions supplies a coherent global organization
of observation. The finite/cofinite Henkin model has ample reversible
observations without admitting such an organization. This standard-model
argument alone does not give an internal Choice derivation. The internal proof in
choice-implies-coherent-reversible-separation.txt establishes the stronger result: boxed Functional Choice implies stable CoRevSep, without
assuming full standard semantics or quantified Infinity.
