1. THE EXISTING MODEL

Use bernoulli-shift-injective-separation.md, Sections 2-6. Its supported
measure-algebra hierarchy and generic elementary-hull model satisfy
C5 and necessary InjSep, but necessarily fail SplitMono. Its powers
E^n of the one-sided Bernoulli shift are admitted, injective, and
qualified for every type, and already supply all the separating
witnesses. The source includes an independent ordinary audit of
its underlying proof; that audit did not check the new verdict here.

2. THE COMMUTING FAMILY IS ITSELF ADMITTED

In the source's ambient supported hierarchy define

  K(F)=Top iff F=E^n for some external n in N;
  K(F)=Bottom otherwise.

Every symmetry fixes E and all its powers, so this function is
equivariant. It therefore has empty support and belongs to the
permitted predicate domain. Include K and the power sequence among
the parameters of the countable elementary hull used by the generic
construction. Each standard n and E^n then belongs to that hull.

Faithful restriction preserves the actual distinctness of functions.
Thus the current K-members in the resulting logical model are
exactly the restrictions of these powers. There is no internal
assumption of an arbitrary set of observers or a type-quantifier.

Their whole compositions satisfy E^n E^m=E^(n+m)=E^m E^n.
The source's strong-mixing and genericity argument, Section 6, says
that for any nonzero admitted d some E^n d is currently true, at
every generic world. Therefore this admitted commuting family
separates propositions everywhere; the source's Functionality and
faithfulness lift it to every relational type and finite list.

Consequently this model satisfies

  C5 + Box CoSep + Box not SplitMono,

and hence necessarily fails Rev as well. The existing failure of
RC_(tt,t) is retained; no stronger choice verdict is claimed.

3. COMPARISON

The finite-alphabet model M_5 in
finite-alphabet-coherence-countermodels.txt satisfies C5+Box Rev
and necessarily fails CoSep. Together the two models prove that
CoSep and Rev are incomparable over C5, including at boxed scope.
Coherence and availability of qualified inverses are separate
restrictions on how enough observers may be supplied.

The present note adds a family-admission check and a new comparison
to an existing model; it does not claim a new Bernoulli construction.
