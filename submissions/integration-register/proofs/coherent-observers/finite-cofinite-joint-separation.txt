FINITE-SUPPORT FINITE/COFINITE MODEL: SEPARATE AND JOINT OBSERVER STRENGTHS
27 October 2026. Original ordinary construction and verdicts by OpenAI Codex,
independently AI-reviewed.
Direct source: misc, Sections 1–7, unpaginated.

1. EXACT VERDICTS

There is a model of the fixed relational C satisfying, at every world,

  C5, full Atomicity, Actuality, full Rev, and full CoIdemSep,

while no commuting family of injective Boolean observers separates
propositions at any world. This excludes even external families, and hence
refutes CoInjSep with an admitted predicate and any stronger qualification.
Thus CoSep+InjSep does not imply joint realization, even with the necessary
positive principles listed above.

The model uses finite/cofinite sets and finite support, not forcing or
generic evaluation. The standard finite-support Henkin construction is
background methodology; the simultaneous-observer verdict and its proof
are the new deductions. No Choice verdict is asserted or transferred from
another model.

2. THE FULL TYPED CONSTRUCTION

Let W be infinite and G=Sym(W). Put

  B=FinCof(W)={A subset W : A finite or W minus A finite}.

G acts on B by direct image. For finite S subset W, let H_S be the pointwise
stabilizer of S. An object is supported by S when H_S fixes it. Let D_e be
a singleton with trivial action and D_t=B. At each permitted arrow sigma
tau, admit ALL finitely supported actual functions D_sigma -> D_tau,
with conjugation action. Application is evaluation. Truth at a designated
a in W is membership of a.

The key observation is:

  Every H_S-invariant subset of W consists of some subset of S, optionally
  together with all of W minus S, and therefore belongs to B.             (1)

H_S fixes S pointwise and is transitive on its complement.

For F:D_sigma->B supported by S, its range is H_S-invariant as a set of
propositions. The literal intersection and union of that range are
H_S-invariant subsets of W, hence belong to B by (1). Define quantifiers
by these literal intersections/unions. Their operations are equivariant
and admitted. Finite attainment is not required.

Logical constants are equivariant. A parameterized lambda interpretation
has support contained in the finite union of its parameter supports,
so all required abstractions belong to the domains. At every type sigma
and object x, the singleton test

  y |-> W if y=x, empty otherwise

is supported by any support of x. Leibniz identity, defined using the
admitted literal quantifier, is therefore crisp at every type. Native
Box A is W exactly when A=W and empty otherwise.

These clauses have ordinary classical truth conditions at every world.
Actual functions give beta/eta and application. H theorems hold everywhere;
H-equivalent formulas give identical sets and identical abstractions.
This checks C's original Logical Equivalence gate. Crisp identity gives
every boxed ND instance, hence C5 at every world.

3. ATOMICITY AND REVERSIBLE SEPARATION

The atoms of B are the singletons. Every nonempty proposition has an atom
below it, and {a} is true and entails every truth at a. Thus propositional
Atomicity and Actuality hold at every world.

At a relational type, any nonzero relation has a tuple xbar and world w
at which its value contains w. The relation taking exactly xbar to {w}
and all other tuples to empty is admitted: xbar and w have a finite union
of supports. It is an atom below the given relation. This verifies full
relational Atomicity directly.

Each finitely supported permutation g of W induces the admitted observer
E_g(A)=g^{-1}[A], with inverse E_(g^{-1}). Its moved set supports the
operation. Inverse image preserves Boolean operations and every literal
intersection/union, so both qualify at every type. If A and B differ at
w, a transposition taking the designated a to w separates them. Actual
function domains lift this to relational separation. Hence full Rev
holds at every world.

4. AN ADMITTED COMMUTING IDEMPOTENT FAMILY

Fix a and one b!=a. Give W the flat semilattice with least point a,
greatest point b, and all other points incomparable. Write v*w for join,
f_w(v)=v*w, and

  T_w(A)=f_w^{-1}[A].

For w=a this is identity; for w=b it is evaluation at b. Otherwise f_w
sends a and w to w, and every other point to b. Its inverse images of
finite/cofinite sets are finite/cofinite; T_w is supported by {a,b,w}.
Literal inverse image again proves every typed quantifier law.

Semilattice laws give T_w T_z=T_z T_w and T_w^2=T_w. Define K(E)=W
exactly when E=T_w for some w, and K(E)=empty otherwise. Every permutation
fixing a,b permutes this collection by reindexing w. Thus K has support
{a,b} and is an ADMITTED endpoint-valued predicate. Since a*w=w, it
separates at a, including at all relational types.

The construction works at every designated world, proving necessary
full CoIdemSep. It does not say the same idempotent family separates
at every world.

5. ALL ADMITTED INJECTIVE BOOLEAN OBSERVERS ARE FINITARY PERMUTATIONS

Let E:B->B be any admitted injective Boolean endomorphism, and choose a
finite support S. For u outside S put A_u=E({u}). It is invariant under
H_(S union {u}), so consists of some subset of S, optionally u, and
optionally all of W minus (S union {u}). Equivariance under H_S makes
this pattern uniform for u outside S.

An S-point in one A_u would belong to them all, contradicting pairwise
disjointness. An outside part would make A_u and A_v overlap outside
S union {u,v}, again impossible. Thus A_u is either {u} or empty.
Injectivity and E(empty)=empty exclude empty. Therefore

  E({u})={u} for all u outside S.                            (2)

For s in S, E({s}) is disjoint from all the outside singletons, so lies
in S. The |S| such images are nonempty and pairwise disjoint, hence are
singleton sets permuting S. Every member of B is a finite union of
singletons or its complement, so Boolean preservation determines E
as inverse image under a permutation fixing W minus S pointwise.

This classifies EVERY admitted injective Boolean observer. Quantifier
qualification is not needed for the classification.

6. WHY NO COMMUTING INJECTIVE FAMILY CAN SEPARATE

Suppose such a family separates at a, and let P be its finitary dual
permutations. They commute: pullback commutation on singletons detects
commutation of the point maps. For every w, separating {w} from empty
supplies h in P with h(a)=w. Thus these orbit values cover W.

Choose g in P with g(a)!=a. For every h in P,

  g(h(a))=h(g(a))!=h(a),                                    (3)

using commutation and injectivity of h. Hence g moves every point
reached by the family, namely every point of W. This contradicts
finite support. No admission assumption about the family was used.

The model distinguishes ample reversible observations from one coherent
commuting system of reversible observations. The positive counterpart
is the abelian world-symmetry theorem in joint-injective-separation.txt.

7. EXPLICIT FAILURE OF FUNCTIONAL CHOICE

The following argument received independent AI review.

Define the endpoint-valued relation on propositions

  U(A,p) iff [A!=empty and p is an atom below A]
             or [A=empty and p=empty].

It is equivariant, hence admitted, and serial: every nonempty member of
the finite/cofinite algebra contains a singleton. Suppose an admitted
selector X:B->B exists and choose a finite support S for X. The proposition
A=W minus S is nonempty and fixed by the pointwise stabilizer H_S.
Equivariance therefore requires X(A) to be fixed by H_S. Selection requires
X(A)={w} for some w outside S. But H_S permutes the infinite complement
transitively, so no such singleton is fixed. This is a contradiction.

The relation has endpoint values, so the same argument applies at every
evaluation point. The model satisfies necessary failure of the (t,t)
Functional Choice instance. It therefore does not challenge the positive
boxed-Functional-Choice theorem in
choice-implies-coherent-reversible-separation.txt.
