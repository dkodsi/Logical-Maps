# Injective logical separation without logical left inverses

26 October 2026. New ordinary mathematical argument by OpenAI Codex. Direct source: `misc`, this note, §§1–7, unpaginated. Sections 2–7 received independent AI review on 26 October 2026. The construction uses ZFC. The supported hierarchy and its observer classification are proved here; the generic Boolean truth construction is also recalled explicitly.

## 1. Result

There is a Henkin model of the fixed relational theory C satisfying

```math
 C5+\Box\mathrm{InjSep}+\Box\neg\mathrm{SplitMono}
 \tag{1}
```

Here the positive assertion has the full relational output scope and every finite external logical-qualification list. The negative assertion already holds for propositional separation with the qualification list containing type t. Both maps in a proposed logical inverse pair must satisfy that qualification, and the inverse equation is an identity of whole operations.

More precisely, the only admitted surjective logical observer is identity. Consequently the only admitted logical observer having a qualified logical left inverse is identity. Nevertheless the positive powers of one admitted injective observer separate all propositions, and hence all relational objects, at every world.

Thus **C5 + full InjSep does not entail full SplitMono**. The same countermodel refutes the implication over bare C. This model fails the Relational Choice instance from properties of propositions to propositions, as checked in §7. The separate [rigid Souslin construction](../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/h-observer-space/rigid-souslin-injective-separation.md) subsequently refutes the implication strengthened by RC_all as well.

## 2. The measure algebra and its symmetries

Let I be an uncountable set, for example omega_1. Give

```math
 X=2^{I\times\mathbb N}
```

the product fair-coin probability measure, and let B be its complete measure Boolean algebra. Write x_(i,n) for the Boolean event that coordinate (i,n) is 1. Every element of B depends on countably many coordinates, hence on countably many columns i in I. This assertion concerns equivalence classes modulo null sets; completing the measure introduces no additional equivalence classes.

For S subset I let B_S be the complete subalgebra of events depending only on columns in S. The one-sided shift gives the injective complete Boolean endomorphism

```math
 E(x_{i,n})=x_{i,n+1}
 \tag{2}
```

Use the following group G of complete measure-preserving Boolean automorphisms. It is generated by all permutations of I and the following transformations, with distinct target and control columns. All undisplayed columns stay fixed:

```math
\begin{array}{ll}
 \text{flip:}&x_{i,n}\mapsto\neg x_{i,n},\\
 \text{add:}&x_{i,n}\mapsto x_{i,n}\mathbin\triangle x_{j,n},\\
 \text{delayed add:}&x_{i,n}\mapsto x_{i,n}\mathbin\triangle x_{j,n+1},\\
 \text{product add:}&x_{i,n}\mapsto x_{i,n}\mathbin\triangle(x_{j,n}\land x_{k,n})
\end{array}
 \tag{3}
```

Each formula is imposed at all n. Each gate is an involution and preserves product measure: conditional on the control columns it translates the target's independent fair-coin sequence. Every generator, and hence every g in G, commutes with E. This makes E invariant under conjugation by G.

An object has **countable support** if it is fixed by every g fixing pointwise some countable set P of elements of B. For functions, G acts by conjugation on input and output. Countable supports are closed under conjugation and finite unions. Every Boolean element has support consisting of itself.

We shall use this elementary fact about product measure algebras:

**Fixed-algebra lemma.** The elements of B fixed by all column permutations fixing S pointwise, for countable S, are exactly B_S.

Indeed an element b has countable column support S union T. Move T outside S to a disjoint T' by a column permutation fixing S. If b is invariant, it belongs to both B_(S union T) and B_(S union T'). These two algebras are conditionally independent over B_S. If r is the conditional expectation of the common indicator onto the S-coordinates, conditional independence and equality give r=r^2 almost everywhere. Thus b already belongs to B_S. This also proves the assertion with S replaced by S union {i}.

## 3. Classification of supported complete endomorphisms

**Classification theorem.** A countably supported complete Boolean endomorphism F of B is exactly a map of the form

```math
 F(b)=\bigvee_{m<\omega}\bigl(c_m\land E^m b\bigr),
 \qquad (c_m)_{m<\omega}\text{ a Boolean partition of }1
 \tag{4}
```

The partition is countable, and all its members consequently depend on a common countable set of columns. Zero partition members are allowed.

For the forward direction, let P be a countable support of F. Choose countable S such that P subset B_S. Every symmetry fixing B_S pointwise fixes P, so F commutes with all such symmetries in G.

For i outside S, the fixed-algebra lemma shows that F(x_(i,n)) belongs to B_(S union {i}). Commutation with permutations of the fresh columns gives one measurable function

```math
 h_n(s,y)\in\{0,1\},\qquad
 s\in 2^{S\times\mathbb N},\quad y\in2^{\mathbb N},
 \tag{5}
```

whose value represents F(x_(i,n)), using y as column i. The same h_n works for every i outside S. Similarly F(x_(a,n)) belongs to B_S for a in S. Equality of the representing functions is always modulo null sets.

Take distinct i,j outside S. Commutation with the add gate targeting i with control j, and Boolean preservation by F, give

```math
 h_n(s,y+z)=h_n(s,y)+h_n(s,z)
 \tag{6}
```

almost everywhere, with addition modulo 2. Fubini applies since S is countable. For almost every s this says that h_n(s,-) is an almost-everywhere measurable homomorphism from the compact group 2^N to 2.

Here is the required elementary form of that fact. If f=(-1)^h, then f(y+z)=f(y)f(z) almost everywhere. The finite-coordinate Walsh characters span a dense subspace of L^2(2^N), since finite-coordinate functions do. Some Fourier coefficient a_A of f is nonzero. Integrating the displayed equation against the character chi_A(z) gives

```math
 \chi_A(y)a_A=f(y)a_A
```

for almost every y. Therefore h is the parity of a finite set A of coordinates. This argument also covers A empty.

The flip gate implies that the size of A is odd. Now use the product-add gate with three fresh columns. Together with (6) its commutation equation implies

```math
 h_n(s,zw)=h_n(s,z)h_n(s,w)
 \tag{7}
```

almost everywhere. A parity on two or more coordinates fails (7): choose z to have exactly one selected coordinate in A, and w exactly a different selected coordinate in A. This has positive product measure, makes the right side 1 and the left side 0. Consequently A is a singleton. Thus there is a measurable integer-valued function k_n(s) such that

```math
 F(x_{i,n})(s,y)=y(k_n(s))\quad(i\notin S)
 \tag{8}
```

Measurability of k_n follows, for example, by testing which coordinate evaluation has zero L^2 distance from h_n(s,-). These disjoint measurable tests exhaust almost every s.

The delayed-add gate now implies

```math
 x_{j,k_n(s)+1}=x_{j,k_{n+1}(s)}
```

almost everywhere for an independent fresh column j. Distinct coordinates in a fair-coin sequence agree with probability only 1/2, so

```math
 k_{n+1}(s)=k_n(s)+1
```

almost everywhere. Intersecting the countably many conull sets gives k_n(s)=n+k(s), where k=k_0 takes values in N.

Finally use the add gate targeting a fresh i with control a in S. It fixes B_S pointwise. Its commutation equation and (8) give

```math
 F(x_{a,n})=x_{a,n+k(s)}
 \tag{9}
```

This holds simultaneously for all a in S and n because S is countable. For each individual fresh column the same conclusion is already (8); no uncountable intersection of conull sets is needed. Set c_m=[k(s)=m] in B_S. Equations (8)–(9) say that F and the right side of (4) agree on every coordinate generator. Both preserve countable Boolean operations, and every Boolean element is represented by a countable-coordinate Borel set. Hence they agree on all of B.

Conversely, a map in (4) is a complete Boolean homomorphism, by disjoint Boolean patching and distributivity of finite meet over arbitrary joins. It is supported by the countable set {c_m:m in N}, since every g in G commutes with E. This proves the classification.

## 4. Surjective maps and separating powers

**Surjectivity theorem.** The only countably supported surjective complete Boolean endomorphism of B is identity.

Write F as (4), choose countable S supporting its partition, and put c=join_(m>0)c_m. Choose i outside S. Consider the measure-preserving involution eta which flips just the input bit x_(i,0) on the event c, doing nothing off c. It fixes c and every other coordinate. If m>0, F's image of x_(i,n) on c_m uses input time n+m, never time 0. On c_0 eta does nothing. All other coordinate images in (8)–(9) are also fixed by eta. Thus eta fixes every element of F[B], by countable Boolean generation. If c is nonzero, eta is nonidentity. In that case F cannot be onto B. Therefore an onto F has c_0=1 and F=I.

In particular, if F and A are supported complete Boolean endomorphisms and F A=I, then F is onto, so F=I and A=I. This is a statement about every possible qualified inverse witness, not just about the particular shift E.

In contrast, for every nonzero b in B,

```math
 \bigvee_{m<\omega}E^m b=1
 \tag{10}
```

To prove this, the one-sided shift is strongly mixing on this product measure algebra. For finite-coordinate events a,b, their time coordinates become disjoint after a sufficiently large shift, so their measures multiply. Approximation in measure by finite-coordinate events gives

```math
 \mu(a\land E^m b)\longrightarrow\mu(a)\mu(b)
```

for arbitrary a,b in B. Apply this with a the complement of the left side of (10). Every intersection on the left of the limit is zero. Since mu(b)>0, mu(a)=0. Equation (10) follows. This proof works for the uncountable column set because each event is approximated by finite-coordinate events.

## 5. The full supported relational hierarchy

Define ambient domains recursively in the native relational type grammar:

```math
 A_e=\mathbb N\text{ with trivial G-action},\qquad A_t=B,
```

and A_(sigma tau) is the set of all countably supported functions from A_sigma to A_tau whenever tau is relational. G acts on this function set by conjugation. Application is ordinary evaluation.

Interpret Boolean connectives by the Boolean operations in B. For every type sigma define

```math
 Q_\sigma(f)=\bigwedge_{a\in A_\sigma}f(a)
 \tag{11}
```

B is externally complete, so these values exist. Each Q_sigma is equivariant, since the G-action permutes A_sigma and preserves all Boolean meets. Every parameter has countable support, and finitely many parameters have a common countable support. Structural induction on terms therefore gives every lambda abstraction in the stated function domain. Composition, application, all quantifier operations and their composites have exactly the claimed supports.

Identity is the crisp predicate with value 1 for equal objects and 0 for unequal objects. It agrees with Leibniz identity: for every a in A_sigma the haecceity y↦[y=a] is admitted, with any support of a. Thus unequal objects are separated by an admitted predicate. The same observation handles individual identity. These are genuine functions, so identities of whole operations have their usual literal meanings in this ambient algebra.

An admitted operation F in A_tt satisfies the full Boolean and type-t quantifier qualification exactly when it is a complete Boolean endomorphism. One direction follows directly from completeness and (11), and indeed gives qualification for every type at once. For the other, let (b_n) be any external countable family in B. The function

```math
 f(b)=\begin{cases}b,&b\in\{b_n:n<\omega\},\\0,&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}
```

belongs to A_tt: the countable set {b_n:n<omega} is a support. Boolean preservation and the type-t quantifier identity therefore make F preserve its range join, hence the join of every countable family. The measure algebra has the countable chain condition. In a complete ccc Boolean algebra every arbitrary join is the join of a countable subfamily: choose a maximal antichain refining the family and, for each of its countably many members, choose a family member above it. Monotonicity now upgrades countable-join preservation to preservation of every join. This proves completeness of F.

Crisp equality is preserved by every Boolean endomorphism since 0 and 1 are fixed. Thus the translated equality clauses add no restriction here. All preservation claims are equalities of the actual full functions, not just equalities of their current extensions.

It follows that the exact qualified observer class in this hierarchy is (4). The inverse conclusion of §4 therefore concerns the required logical inverse class.

## 6. Classical generic worlds and full separation

Choose a sufficiently large regular theta and countable M elementary in H_theta, containing B, G, the entire ambient hierarchy, its operations, E, and a coordinate generator. Theta can be chosen above the hereditary sizes of all these set-sized objects, their powersets and the tests used above. Put D_sigma=M intersect A_sigma, retaining the functions as codes and restricting their application. Elementarity makes restriction faithful: unequal ambient functions in M differ at an argument in M, recursively at every relational type.

Worlds are all M-generic ultrafilters on B intersect M. Interpret truth of an admitted proposition by membership in the chosen ultrafilter. Every nonzero b in B intersect M belongs to some such ultrafilter, by the countable dense-set construction.

For clarity, the quantifier truth lemma does not assume that arbitrary ultrafilters preserve arbitrary meets. If f is in M, let q be its ambient range meet (11), also in M. The Boolean set consisting of nonzero elements below q or below some not f(a) is dense and belongs to M. Genericity meets it. If q is not in the ultrafilter, elementarity supplies a witnessing a in A_sigma intersect M with f(a) false. The converse follows from q≤f(a). Thus the classical universal-quantifier clause holds exactly over D_sigma. Negation supplies the existential clause. Induction gives the truth lemma for every typed formula and lambda term.

H holds by these classical clauses and crisp substitutive identity. Logical Equivalence holds because an H-theorem holds at every generic world under every assignment; a nonzero Boolean disagreement would itself admit a generic world. Equality of the bodies at every argument gives equality of the actual abstraction functions. Necessity p=Top holds exactly when p=1, equivalently when p holds at every generic world. All identities and distinctness judgments are crisp, giving the full C5 schema. This is the same generic Boolean truth mechanism used in [Generic Boolean choice with reversible observer separation](../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/h-observer-space/generic-boolean-choice-boundary.md), §1, but the ambient function domains here are the supported domains in §5, not full function sets. Its choice verdicts are consequently not imported.

The qualification equations at a generic world are crisp identities of restricted functions. By faithfulness and elementarity, a qualified M-function is qualified on the whole ambient hierarchy. The classification and the uniqueness of a surjective qualified observer therefore apply to every admitted witness. In particular any admitted qualified pair F A=I must have F=A=I.

Every E^m is in M, supported without parameters, injective, and qualified for all types. Given distinct p,q in D_t, put d=p triangle q≠0. Equation (10) defines in M a dense set refined by the E^m d. Genericity supplies m with E^m d true at the current world. That observer separates p and q. For distinct relational objects, faithfulness supplies an admitted argument tuple on which their proposition values differ; apply the same argument to those values. This proves the entire full InjSep schema, with one observer qualified at every requested finite list. It works at every generic world, so every instance is necessary.

The SplitMono observer class is {I}. Fix an admitted coordinate proposition p with 0<p<1. At each world either p is true and agrees with 1 under I, or p is false and agrees with 0 under I. The corresponding pair is distinct. Thus propositional SplitMono fails at every world, giving its boxed failure and proving (1).

## 7. Scope and checks

The construction settles the unrestricted C5 implication and the bare-C implication negatively. Its failure of Relational Choice prevents this construction from settling that strengthening; the later [rigid Souslin model](../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/h-observer-space/rigid-souslin-injective-separation.md) settles it separately. This note does not identify internal BC_t with completeness of the ambient measure algebra, and it makes no claim about Max(C) + OS + BC_t.

**Choice boundary.** The model necessarily fails RC_(tt,t). Use the empty-supported serial relation

```math
 U(X,p):=Xp\lor\bigl(\neg\exists q\,Xq\land p=0\bigr)
```

For any proposed subrelation R, let w be the Boolean value of its being functional and contained in U. Choose countable S supporting R's support parameters; w is fixed by all column permutations outside S, so w belongs to B_S. Choose countably infinitely many columns T disjoint from S and let X_T be the admitted crisp predicate selecting exactly the distinct propositions x_(i,0), i in T. On w, the values a_i=w meet R(X_T,x_(i,0)) are pairwise disjoint and have join w: containment excludes all other outputs, and functionality supplies one. Permutations of T fix R, w and X_T and permute the a_i. They preserve measure, so all a_i have equal measure. A countably infinite disjoint family of equal positive measure cannot lie in a probability algebra; hence all have measure zero, and w=0. This applies to every ambient R. The generic truth lemma gives the asserted necessary failure. This boundary check does not refute the separate implication with RC_all as an added premise.

The decisive extra structure is the explicit symmetry group (3). Column permutations localize any supported observer's coordinate images. The two Boolean gates force each image to read exactly one input time, and the delayed gate forces a common forward time displacement. Surjective observers cannot discard the independent time-zero bits. This yields a classification of every possible admitted logical inverse, while mixing supplies the separating family.
