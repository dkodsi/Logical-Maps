1. THE EXACT CHARACTERIZATION

Over C5, propositional Atomicity is equivalent to the following single
finitary assertion:

There exists Phi:t(tt) such that, writing E_i=Phi(i),

  (i)   every E_i is Good_{t};
  (ii)  E_i^2=E_i for every i;
  (iii) E_i E_j=E_j E_i for every i,j;
  (iv)  forall p,q. [forall i. (E_i p iff E_i q)] -> p=q.

All observer equations are whole-operation identities. Good_{t}
includes whole Boolean and propositional-quantifier preservation.
The family itself is an admitted operation, not a predicate selecting
different members according to current truth.

The forward implication from a family to Atomicity works at ANY
specified admitted index type iota, with Phi:iota(tt). The converse
constructs Phi at iota=t explicitly and makes every member qualified
at every finite list of types. It supplies full relational separation.
No Functional Choice premise is required in either direction.

C5 supplies necessary ND, BF, and Functionality, with the established
native C type reductions. In particular every native identity has
value Top or Bottom. These facts are used explicitly below.

2. TRUTH-PRESERVING MEMBERS MUST BE IDENTITY

Write I=lambda p.p and define the admitted proposition

  r_i := forall p. (E_i p iff p).

If r_i is currently true, then E_i=I. To prove this, fix p and j.
Commutation gives E_j(E_i p)=E_i(E_j p). Current truth preservation
by E_i makes the right side true exactly when E_j p is true.
Separation (iv) yields E_i p=p. This holds for every current p;
Functionality collects it into E_i=I. Conversely E_i=I plainly
makes r_i true. Thus, currently,

  r_i iff (E_i=I).                                               (2)

Idempotence and propositional-quantifier preservation give the
stronger VALUE identity

  E_i(r_i)
    = forall p. (E_i(E_i p) iff E_i p)
    = Top.                                                      (3)

The first equality uses the whole quantifier and Boolean laws;
the second rewrites with the whole idempotence equation. It does
not turn merely current truth preservation into a whole equation.

3. THE FAMILY DEFINES A TRUE ATOM

Let c_i be the native identity proposition E_i=I, and define

  a := forall i. (r_i -> c_i).                                  (4)

By (2) and the actual quantifier truth clause, a is currently true.
Since C5 makes c_i an endpoint and observers fix endpoints,
E_i(c_i)=c_i. Unconditional universal instantiation gives
a <= (r_i -> c_i). Apply E_i and use (3):

  E_i(a) <= (E_i(r_i) -> E_i(c_i)) = c_i.                        (5)

Therefore:
  if E_i is not I, E_i(a)=Bottom;
  if E_i is I, E_i(a)=a.

Take b<=a with b nonzero. Separation from Bottom supplies i with
E_i(b) true. Monotonicity and (5) force E_i=I, hence b is true.
For every j, if E_j=I then E_j(b) and E_j(a) are both true; if
E_j is not I then both values are Bottom. Separation now gives
b=a. Thus a is an atom, and it is currently true.

Notice that no preservation of the index-type quantifier by E_i
was needed in (5): universal INSTantiation alone supplied its
algebraic inequality. Qualification at t suffices.

4. FROM THE TRUE ATOM TO ATOMICITY EVERYWHERE

For any atom a in C5, its principal reader

  H_a(p) := Box(a -> p)

is an admitted endpoint-valued observer qualified at every finite
type list. Here is the needed verification. An atom decides every
proposition, and C5 makes the corresponding identities crisp, so
H_a(p) is Top exactly when a<=p and Bottom otherwise. These tests
preserve Boolean operations. For a quantified predicate F,

  a <= forall x.Fx  iff  forall x. a<=Fx

by BF/Tractarianism. Thus H_a preserves each admitted universal;
existentials follow by duality. Endpoint values and Functionality
turn these pointwise value identities into the required whole
operation identities. This is the existing atom-reader theorem,
spelled out to expose its BF use. Since our a is true, H_a also
preserves current truth.

For nonzero d, separation supplies i with E_i(d) true. Then
H_a E_i(d)=Top. The composite H_a E_i is Good_t and endpoint-valued.
For any such endpoint observer H, put

  w_H := forall p. (H(p) -> p).

Quantifier preservation and H(Hp)=Hp give H(w_H)=Top, so w_H is
nonzero. Universal instantiation gives w_H<=p when Hp=Top, and
w_H<=not-p otherwise. Therefore w_H is an atom. In the displayed
case it lies below d. Every nonzero d thus has an atom below it.

This proves the forward implication. The endpoint-reader step uses
only established observer lemmas, with their hypotheses checked;
it does not assume global Atomicity before deriving it.

5. EXPLICIT CONVERSE: REPLACING WORLD READERS BY COMMUTING RETRACTIONS

Assume C5+Atomicity_t. The established consequence Actuality gives
a true atom a. If it is the only atom, Atomicity makes Top=a and
every proposition an endpoint. Take Phi constantly I.

Otherwise choose one further atom b distinct from a. No family
choice is involved. For each atom w distinct from a define

  T_w(p) := ((a or w) and H_w(p))
             or (not(a or w) and H_b(p)).                         (6)

Set T_a=I, and also assign I to every nonatom index. This assignment
is one admitted lambda operation Phi:t(tt): in (6), use the guard
q(w)=Atom(w) and w!=a, putting Phi(w)(p)=
  (not q(w) and p) or (q(w) and the right side of (6)).

Under C5 the guard q(w) is endpoint-valued. Atomhood is built from
identity/order tests, whose values are endpoints; BF preserves this
endpoint property through its quantifiers. Thus each Phi(w) is
literally the claimed observer. Formula (6) is Boolean patching of
two fully qualified endpoint observers, so it is itself fully
qualified. Identity handles the other indices.

To check commutation and idempotence, test at an arbitrary atom v.
For w!=a, H_v(T_w(p)) is H_w(p) if v=a or v=w, and is H_b(p)
otherwise. On atoms this is the flat join operation with least
element a, greatest element b, and all remaining atoms incomparable:
v star w=v when w=a; v star v=v; b is absorbing; distinct nonleast
atoms have product b. It is associative, commutative, and idempotent.

Consequently every atomic reader gives the same value to
T_w(T_z(p)) and T_z(T_w(p)), and likewise to T_w(T_w(p)) and T_w(p).
Atomicity detects equality of propositions; Functionality collects
these identities into the whole-operation equations. Nonatom
indices merely add further copies of I.

At the current true atom a, T_w(p) has exactly the current truth
value of H_w(p), also when w=a. Given unequal p,q, Atomicity supplies
an atom w under their symmetric difference, and that index
distinguishes them. Thus Phi separates propositions. Functionality
lifts the same family to every relational output type.

The range of this Phi even forms a commutative idempotent monoid.
It is not necessary to postulate this extra closure separately.

6. INTERPRETATION AND SCOPE

Over C5 this is a new characterization of Atomicity, not an
independent proposed strengthening of it. Its content is that
uniformly available compatible logical projections recover atomic
worlds without requiring the projections themselves to be endpoint
valued. In (6) their outputs can remain contingent.

The family distinction is essential: cohen-separation-model.txt
constructs an atomless C5 model satisfying the predicate-family
version, even with idempotence and composition closure. It also
shows that uniformly indexed commuting INVOLUTIONS can separate in
an atomless model. Idempotence cannot be replaced by finite order.
