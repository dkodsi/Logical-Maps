1. RESULT AND EXACT STRENGTH

The converse asked about is false over the fixed relational Classicism C:

  C + OS + .2 does not entail CoSep.

Here CoSep is the predicate-family principle of coherent-families.txt,
Section 1: an admitted predicate selects a separating family of logical
observers which commute pairwise as WHOLE operations. Failure below is
already at propositional separation with Boolean qualification alone.
Consequently neither more quantifier qualification nor operation indexing
repairs the failure.

In fact one existing model, with the new verdicts proved below, satisfies

  C + OS + IdemBoxSep + Actuality + RC_all
    + Box BC_t + Box BF_all + Box Functionality_all + Box .2
    + not CoSep(t,empty).

IdemBoxSep means separation by logical observers that are idempotent and
commute with native necessity. The model has this at every relational
output type and every finite qualification list, CURRENTLY. It does not
have necessary OS; the terminal component refutes even Boolean separation.
The source construction is ordinary ZFC. No claim of a counterexample over
C5 or over necessary OS is made.

2. THE EXISTING MODEL AND ITS CONSTRUCTION DEPENDENCIES

Use precisely Sections 1-3, not the later mixed variants, of

  "Observer separation with propositional completeness and divergent
  possibilities" (25 October 2026; direct source misc).

For clarity, the ingredients and the crucial rigidity argument are
recalled here. Let B=RO(2^N), the complete atomless regular-open algebra
of Cantor space. Every nonzero principal interval is isomorphic to B.
Let G=Aut(B). At the terminal component take singleton individuals,
propositions B, and at every relational arrow the functions with finite
B-support under the conjugation action of G. An object is supported by
a finite P subset B when every automorphism fixing P fixes it.

If c_1,...,c_m are the nonzero cells generated by P, the propositions
fixed by its pointwise stabilizer are exactly their unions. Moreover an
invariant set of proposition values has its supremum attained by finitely
many members: if some value meets c_i, an automorphism within c_i sends
that intersection to its relative complement, and two values cover c_i.
Doing this for finitely many cells suffices. Infima follow by duality.

Thus the range of every admitted predicate has finitely attained join and
meet. Every ultrafilter U on B respects all the admitted quantifiers;
this is NOT the assertion that arbitrary ultrafilters respect arbitrary
external infinite joins. The supported hierarchy is closed under lambda
abstraction and quantification. Supported singleton tests give crisp
Leibniz identity. As all ultrafilters give classical truth clauses, the
H-equivalence gate is sound. This is a C5 component with BC_t.

Crucially, its ONLY admitted Boolean observer is I. Here is the proof.
Suppose E:B->B is a Boolean endomorphism supported by P. Each E(c_i) is
a union of the cells. If c_j<=E(c_i), j!=i, the map x |-> c_j meet E(x)
from B below c_i to B below c_j is unchanged by every automorphism within
c_i. Swapping a proper nonzero x<c_i with c_i-x would make their images
equal, although the images are complementary in the nonzero algebra
below c_j. This is impossible. Hence E(c_i)<=c_i; since the E(c_i) join
to 1, E fixes each cell.

For 0<x<c_i, invariance under the stabilizer of P and x puts E(x) among
0,x,c_i-x,c_i. Homogeneity makes the same alternative apply to every
proper nonzero x in that cell. The constant alternatives contradict
complementation. The complement alternative contradicts meet preservation
on disjoint nonzero x,y with a nonzero remainder. Therefore E(x)=x and
E=I. This uses the specified supported hierarchy, not a restriction
arbitrarily imposed on a full standard model.

3. CLASSIFICATION OF ALL ROOT BOOLEAN OBSERVERS

Let E be any admitted root observer satisfying the whole Boolean laws.
Its component operation satisfies the component Boolean laws, hence is I
by Section 2. Its root action therefore has the form

  E(epsilon,b) = (chi(epsilon,b), b),

where chi:2 x B -> 2 is a unital Boolean homomorphism. Put j=(1,0).
If chi(j)=1, then chi is the projection to epsilon: the interval below
not-j is killed and chi(epsilon,b)=epsilon. Thus E=I.
If chi(j)=0, chi factors through B and is the characteristic function
of an ultrafilter U on B. Thus E has the form

  E_U(epsilon,b) = (1[b in U], b).                         (3.1)

Conversely every such E_U is admitted: its root row is unrestricted
and its component is I. Every E_U preserves ALL admitted typed
quantifiers by the finite-attainment truth lemma and onto projection
to the component. The source proves the whole equations, not merely
preservation of current truth. Identity has the same qualification.
Consequently the complete root observer list, even before imposing
quantifier clauses, is

  {I} union {E_U : U an ultrafilter on B}.                  (3.2)

Distinct ultrafilters give distinct operations. No E_U is I, since
E_U(j)=0 whereas I(j)=j!=0. Root Functionality also follows from the
source's OS theorem; alternatively whole operations are determined here
by their current row and their common component I.

4. OS, IDEMPOTENT NATIVE-BOX SEPARATION AND NECESSARY .2

Identity distinguishes different first coordinates. If b!=c, some
ultrafilter distinguishes b,c, and the corresponding E_U distinguishes
(epsilon,b),(eta,c) in current truth. This proves propositional OS.
The onto type projections and component function extensionality lift
it to every relational type, as in the source. The SAME observers
preserve every finite list of typed quantifiers.

All observers in (3.2) are idempotent. The native necessity operation
on root propositions is

  Box(epsilon,b) = (epsilon and 1[b=1], 1_B[b=1]).         (4.1)

The first coordinate tests literal root identity with (1,1); the
second tests crisp component identity with 1. Applying E_U to (4.1)
gives (1[b=1],1_B[b=1]). Applying Box to E_U(epsilon,b) gives exactly
the same pair: when b=1 every ultrafilter accepts b. This verifies
E_U Box = Box E_U on all root inputs. At the component both operations
are compared using E_U's component I, so the equality is a WHOLE
operation identity. Identity is plainly Box-commuting too.

Thus the root satisfies full IdemBoxSep, and hence BoxSep and OS.
This is a new qualification verdict for the existing model.

For a direct .2 check, let p=(epsilon,b). From (4.1),

  Diamond Box p is true at the root iff b=1;

The implication follows since B is nontrivial. It holds at every

5. NO COMMUTING SEPARATING FAMILY EXISTS

Using ordinary function composition, (3.1) gives

  E_U composed E_V = E_U,
  E_V composed E_U = E_V.                                  (5.1)

These are whole identities: the current rows have the displayed
values and the component operation is I. Hence two such observers
commute iff U=V. Any pairwise commuting family of eligible observers
is contained in {I,E_U} for some U, or contains only I (or is empty).

Atomlessness supplies a nonzero b outside U: split 1 into two
nonzero pieces and choose the one that U rejects. Then (0,b)!=0,
but both I(0,b) and E_U(0,b) are currently false, just like their
values at 0. Thus {I,E_U} fails propositional separation. A family
containing only I fails as well, using any nonzero b.

This rules out even an externally chosen commuting separating
family. It therefore rules out every admitted predicate family K,
irrespective of K's possibly contingent membership, and every
operation-indexed family. It already uses only Boolean qualification.

Combining Sections 4-5 settles the requested converse negatively.

6. POSITION IN THE OBSERVER FAMILY

CoSep differs from the following individual-observer restrictions:

  CentreSep: each selected observer commutes with EVERY qualified observer;
  BoxSep: each selected observer commutes with native Box;
  IdemSep: each selected observer is idempotent (equivalent to OS).

CoSep requires one separating family whose members commute with EACH
OTHER. It is a family condition, unlike a unary eligibility restriction.
The older central-observer note, Section 1, explicitly distinguished
centrality from a chosen pairwise commuting family without identifying
the latter with a previously classified principle.

Over C the resulting implications are strict:

  CentreSep -> CoSep -> OS + .2.

The second strictness is Sections 2-5. For the first, a full standard
two-world model has C5+Atomicity and therefore uniform commuting
idempotent separation by uniform-retractions.txt. It fails Extensionality,
whereas C5+CentreSep implies Extensionality by the existing centre theorem.

CoSep is also INCOMPARABLE with BoxSep and with IdemBoxSep. The model
above satisfies IdemBoxSep but not CoSep. Conversely the full finite
rooted P_(2,3) model has CoIdemSep by the greatest-point construction in
coherent-families.txt, Section 4, but fails BoxSep by the existing exact
classification in ../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/h-observer-space/idempotent-native-box-observers.md, Section 5.

This root example by itself does not decide C5+OS -> CoSep or
Box OS + .2 -> CoSep. The subsequent finite-alphabet construction in
finite-alphabet-coherence-countermodels.txt settles BOTH negatively,
using a model of C5 and necessary Rev. It also separates CoSep from
CoIdemSep. Those later verdicts are not attributed to the root model.
