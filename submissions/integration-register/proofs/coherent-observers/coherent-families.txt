1. EXACT TWO FORMS OF A FAMILY

Work in the fixed relational Classicism C. Use Good_Delta from
../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/logical-observer-hierarchy.md,
Section 1: whole Boolean and specified typed quantifier identities.
Composition and idempotence below are identities of entire operations.

A predicate family is an admitted K:(tt)t. For a relational output
type tau, say it separates at tau when, for all X,Y:tau,

  [forall E. K(E) -> forall zbar. (E(X[zbar]) iff E(Y[zbar]))]
       -> X=Y.                                                   (1)

Require every current K-member to be Good_Delta and require

  forall E,F. K(E) and K(F) -> EF=FE.                              (2)

CoSep(tau,Delta) asserts existence of such K. CoIdemSep adds
K(E) -> E^2=E. The full theories consist of these separate finite
instances. They do not quantify over types. They imply ordinary OS
at the same instances. Current membership is used throughout; K is
not assumed endpoint-valued or persistent.

A uniformly indexed family instead is one admitted Phi:iota(tt).
Write E_i=Phi(i). Require every E_i to be Good_Delta, every E_i,E_j
to commute, and separation with forall i in place of the guarded
forall E in (1). UCoIdemSep adds E_i^2=E_i for every i. The index
sort is an ordinary specified type. In particular iota=t gives one
finitary formula for each tau,Delta.

The indexed version implies the predicate version: take
K(E):=exists i. E=Phi(i). The converse is NOT automatic. The other
two notes in this folder prove a sharp distinction even over C5.

2. COMMUTING SEPARATION IMPLIES DIRECTEDNESS

Theorem. Over C, propositional CoSep implies

  forall p. Diamond Box p -> Box Diamond p.                       (.2)

No idempotence, quantifier-preservation clause, Choice, BF, or ND
is needed for this implication beyond the specified Boolean
qualification and propositional separation. It holds for either
form of family in Section 1.

Proof. Suppose Diamond Box p and Diamond Box not-p. Separation
from Bottom supplies current members E,F with E(Box p) and
F(Box not-p) true, respectively. This follows because each Boolean
observer sends Bottom to Bottom.

The H theorem expressing Leibniz substitution gives the algebraic
inequality

  (p=Top) <= (G(p) iff G(Top))

for every admitted G. It can be transported through a Boolean
observer by the Boolean whole identities. Take G=F and use
F(Top)=Top. From E(Box p) we obtain E(Fp) true. Similarly, take
G=E and use E(Bottom)=Bottom. From F(Box not-p), equivalently
F(p=Bottom), we obtain F(Ep) false. But EF=FE, a contradiction.

Hence not(Diamond Box p and Diamond Box not-p). By native Boolean
duality this is Diamond Box p -> Box Diamond p. All uses of logical
equivalence are the unconditional H substitution and Boolean laws;
no optional hypothesis has been necessitated. QED.

Thus order-independent observation has a concrete modal consequence:
two incompatible propositions cannot each be possibly necessary.
The separately boxed family principles imply separately boxed .2
by necessitating the discharged C implication, not its premises.

2A. ACTUAL TRUTH PROFILES DETERMINE COMMUTING OPERATIONS

Assume the family separates propositions, and assume Functionality
at tt (already supplied by full CoSep through OS). Let A,B:tt each
commute with every current family member. If forall p.(Ap iff Bp)
holds currently, then A=B as whole operations.

For any current family member E and any p, commutation rewrites
E(Ap) as A(Ep) and E(Bp) as B(Ep). Their truth values agree by
the assumed truth-profile equivalence. Separation yields Ap=Bp.
Functionality then gives A=B. Neither A nor B needs a separate
qualification hypothesis for this argument.

In particular the only operation commuting with the entire family
and preserving current truth is I. This does not say that every
observer is identity; the other family members change current truth.
It explains the first step of uniform-retractions.txt without a
special appeal to Atomicity or to C5 beyond Functionality.

3. COMPOSITION CLOSURE CAN BE ADDED TO PREDICATE FAMILIES

Every predicate family as in Section 1 can be enlarged to an admitted
commutative monoid of qualified observers without losing separation.
For CoIdemSep the enlargement consists entirely of idempotents.

Define G(E) to say that every admitted predicate P:(tt)t containing
I and K and closed under composition contains E. This is ordinary
impredicative comprehension, not an external finite-word set. It
contains I and K, and is closed under composition, by its definition.

Good_Delta is itself a composition-closed predicate containing I and
K, so all G-members are qualified. For fixed k in K the predicate
of operations commuting with k contains I and K and is composition
closed. Thus every G-member commutes with k. Now fix g in G and
repeat this argument for the predicate of operations commuting with
g. It contains K by the first argument. Hence G is pairwise commuting.

If K consists of idempotents, use the predicate G(E) and E^2=E.
It contains I and K and is closed under composition because its
members already commute. The defining induction for G therefore
makes every G-member idempotent. Separation persists since K is
contained in G. All arguments concern admitted predicates and
current whole-operation identities. No standardness of finite words
or Choice principle is being assumed.

4. EXACT FINITE ROOTED-POSET CHARACTERIZATION

Use the full relational action model on a finite poset P with least
point o and singleton individuals. The existing coordinate theorem
in ../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/h-observer-space/idempotent-native-box-observers.md, Section 3, says:

  D_t(o)=P(P), truth is membership of o;
  Good observers are exactly E_f(p)=f^{-1}(p), with x<=f(x);
  every such reader preserves every typed quantifier as a WHOLE
  operation; the model has necessary BF and Functionality.

Theorem. In this model the following are equivalent:

  (a) propositional CoSep;
  (b) full CoIdemSep;
  (c) P has a greatest point.

Proof of necessity. For every x in P, separation of {x} from the
empty proposition supplies a family reader f_x with f_x(o)=x.
All the f_x commute. Compose this finite list and put y=product f_x(o).
For any x, move f_x to the first position. The remaining composite
is inflationary, so y>=f_x(o)=x. Thus y is a greatest point. This
argument uses neither idempotence nor monotonicity of the readers.

Proof of sufficiency. If P is a singleton use identity. Otherwise
let 1 be its greatest point. Give the underlying set a second, flat
join operation star: o is its identity, 1 is absorbing, x star x=x,
and two different elements other than o have product 1. This is an
associative commutative idempotent operation. It need not be the join
for the original order. Define f_a(x)=a star x. These maps are
inflationary in the ORIGINAL order, pairwise commuting, idempotent,
and satisfy f_a(o)=a. Thus they are fully qualified readers and
separate propositions. Functionality lifts separation to every
relational type. Their finite family is admitted by the predicate
E=E_fa1 or ... or E=E_fan. QED.

Every future cone still has a greatest point, so these successful
models satisfy the separately boxed full CoIdemSep schema. Its
witness family may be chosen anew at a future evaluation. The
theorem concerns predicate families; it does not assert uniform
operation indexing in the singleton-individual model.

5. SHARP COMPARISONS AND EXAMPLES

The three-point fork o<a,b, with a,b incomparable and maximal,
satisfies necessary OS but refutes CoSep: it has no greatest point.
It also refutes .2 directly, using p={a}. Hence coherent separation
is strictly stronger than ordinary OS over C.

The four-point diamond o<a,b<1 satisfies necessary CoIdemSep but
fails C5, since the nonzero {o} becomes Bottom at a. It also fails
the usual modal linearity principle .3, using p={a}, q={b}. Thus
commuting observation forces directedness, not linearity or S5.

The seven-point four-level poset with level sizes 1,2,3,1 has a
greatest point, so satisfies necessary CoIdemSep. It fails even
native-Box observer separation by the proved P_(m,n) classification
in ../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/h-observer-space/idempotent-native-box-observers.md, Section 5 (m=2,n=3).
Consequently commuting separation need not supply observers that
commute with native necessity.

Finally, C5+Atomicity implies the stronger uniformly indexed version
by uniform-retractions.txt. Thus the new predicate-family restriction
is satisfied throughout Choice Classicism. These implications are
compatible with the atomless model in cohen-separation-model.txt.
