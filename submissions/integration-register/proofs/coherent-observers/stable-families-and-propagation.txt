1. DEFINITIONS AND RESULTS

Work over the fixed relational C5. All observers below satisfy whole Boolean
and Q_t preservation. The existing C5 reduction upgrades those SAME observers
to every typed quantifier. Use the object-language definitions in
object-language-and-finite-basis.txt; no external family is being substituted
for an admitted predicate K:(tt)t.

Write I=lambda p.p, and put

  Sep(K) := forall p,q. [forall E. KE -> (Ep iff Eq)] -> p=q;
  Stable(K) := forall E. (KE=Top or KE=Bottom);
  Persistent(K) := forall E. (KE -> Box KE);
  Inj(E) := forall p,q. (Ep=Eq -> p=q).

Here stable means that membership has endpoint values. It says nothing about
the outputs of the observers themselves. Assume current K-members commute
as whole operations and collectively separate. Then:

  Stable(K) implies
    [Box Sep(K) iff forall E.(KE -> Inj(E))].                 (A)

There are also exact characterizations:

  Atomicity_t
    iff separation by a stable commuting family of idempotent observers;
    iff separation by a persistent commuting family of idempotent observers;
    iff uniformly indexed commuting idempotent observer separation.        (B)

The last formulation can use index type t. No Functional Choice is required.
In particular the stable-family formulation is one finite sentence over C5.

  Extensionality
    iff there is a stable commuting idempotent family K with Box Sep(K).    (C)

The positive picture is precise: stable availability of enough compatible
retractions recovers atomic worlds. For a stable commuting family, remaining
separating necessarily is exactly the condition that none of its observers
identifies distinct propositions. Full relational separation is recovered
by C5 Functionality with the same family.

2. EXACT PROPAGATION FOR A STABLE FAMILY

Let A=Sep(K), and fix a current member E of K. Expand E(A) using its whole
Boolean and typed quantifier identities. C5 makes p!=q endpoint-valued, and
Stable(K) makes KF endpoint-valued. Boolean observers fix these values.
For each current F in K, use EF=FE. Consequently E(A) is currently true
exactly when

  forall p,q. [forall F. KF -> (F(Ep) iff F(Eq))] -> p=q.    (1)

By current separation of K, (1) is equivalent to Inj(E): unequal p,q must
have unequal E-images, which K then distinguishes. Conversely a witness to
the displayed truth disagreement makes the E-images unequal.

If Box A holds, A=Top, so E(A)=Top for every current E and every such E is
injective. Conversely suppose every current member is injective. Then every
current member makes A true, exactly as it makes Top true. Separation of A
from Top forces A=Top. This proves (A).

There is no hidden shift of family here: it is the same admitted K in the
premise and in Box Sep(K). Stable membership and C5's BF, NI and ND also
make qualification and pairwise commutation necessary: each relevant
membership value and each whole-operation identity is already an endpoint.
Thus (A) can equivalently be stated for necessary adequacy of the same
whole commuting-family witness. It does not replace Box exists K by
exists K Box or assume that these quantifier placements coincide.

3. STABLE AVAILABILITY OF COMMUTING RETRACTIONS CHARACTERIZES ATOMICITY

The forward proof needs only Persistent(K). For each observer E put

  r_E := forall p.(Ep iff p),     c_E := (E=I).

For any current K-member E, if r_E is true then E=I. Indeed, for every
current member F and every p, commutation gives F(Ep)=E(Fp). Actual truth
preservation by E makes this agree in truth with Fp. Separation gives
Ep=p; Functionality collects these identities into E=I.

Define the admitted proposition

  a := forall E.[KE -> (r_E -> c_E)].                         (2)

It is true by the preceding argument. Current membership persistence gives
KE=Top for each current E. Idempotence and Q_t give the value identity

  E(r_E)=forall p.(E(Ep) iff Ep)=Top.

C5 makes c_E an endpoint. Algebraic universal instantiation in (2), followed
by the Boolean observer E, therefore gives

  E(a) <= E(KE -> (r_E -> c_E)) = c_E.                       (3)

Every nonidentity current member kills a, and identity fixes a. Let b be
nonzero with b<=a. Separation from Bottom supplies a current E with E(b)
true. By monotonicity and (3), E must be identity, so b is true. For every
current F, the values F(b) and F(a) now agree in truth: both are true when
F=I and both are Bottom otherwise. Separation gives b=a. Thus a is a true
atom. Notice that no Q_(tt) clause was needed to obtain (3); universal
instantiation supplied the requisite algebraic inequality directly.

For completeness, this yields full propositional Atomicity as follows.
The principal reader H_a(p)=Box(a->p) is an admitted endpoint-valued logical
observer. An atom decides every proposition; C5's BF/Tractarianism gives

  a<=forall x.Fx iff forall x.(a<=Fx),

so H_a preserves every admitted quantifier. Because a is true, it preserves
actual truth. For any nonzero d, choose a current E with E(d) true. Then
H=H_a E is an endpoint-valued Q_t observer with H(d)=Top. Define

  w := forall p.(Hp -> p).

Q_t and endpoint-valuedness yield H(w)=Top, hence w is nonzero. For each p,
universal instantiation gives w<=p if Hp=Top and w<=not-p otherwise.
Thus w is an atom; since H(d)=Top, w<=d. This proves Atomicity_t.

Conversely Atomicity_t supplies the explicit indexed family Phi:t(tt) in
uniform-retractions.txt, Section 5. Its members commute, are idempotent,
qualify at every type, and separate all relational distinctions. Define

  KE := exists i:t.(E=Phi(i)).                               (4)

C5 makes each identity an endpoint, and BF makes its existential closure
an endpoint. Thus K is stable and has exactly the current range of Phi.
This proves the stable-family converse, and stable membership plainly
implies persistence. The prior indexed characterization completes (B).

One can also replace a persistent witness directly by the stable predicate
K'(E):=Box KE. C5 makes its values endpoints; persistence and reflexivity
give exactly the same current members. No internal choice of enumeration
or selected indexing operation is required for this replacement.

The explicit Atomicity-to-Phi construction is substantive prior work, not

4. THE EXTENSIONAL ENDPOINT

Suppose a stable commuting idempotent family separates necessarily. By (A)
each current member is injective. From E(Ep)=Ep, injectivity gives Ep=p;
Functionality gives E=I. Current separation therefore says that material
equivalence implies propositional identity: the Fregean Axiom, equivalent
over C to full relational Extensionality.

Conversely under Extensionality the singleton family of identity is stable,
qualified, commuting, idempotent and separating. Identity is injective, so
(A) gives necessary separation by that same family. This proves (C).

Scope: in (A) and (C) use stable membership, not just persistence of the
currently present members. The latter permits additional members to enter
elsewhere. For example in the full two-world C5 model at w0, with endpoint
readers E0,E1, take K(I)=K(E1)=Top and K(E0)={w1}, with other values Bottom.
Currently {I,E1} is commuting, idempotent, separating and persistent.
Sep(K) holds at both worlds, but Extensionality fails. The enlarged family
at w1 does not commute. This scope check belongs in the proof, not as a
prominent negative result in the introductory guide.

5. THE MODAL CONNECTION TO MAXIMALISM

The earlier bare-C proof CoSep -> .2 has a direct Maximalism consequence.
Let P be the pure sentence saying that all individuals are identical.
A full one-world model with one individual satisfies Box P, and one with
two individuals satisfies Box not-P. Both are C models. Hence neither
closed pure identity Box P=Bottom nor Box not-P=Bottom is a C theorem.
Pure Max(C) asserts their negations, namely Diamond Box P and
Diamond Box not-P. These contradict .2, and thus CoSep. No C5 premise or
enlargement of the reference theory is used. This is a short consequence
of the existing directedness theorem, not a new Maximalism research line.
