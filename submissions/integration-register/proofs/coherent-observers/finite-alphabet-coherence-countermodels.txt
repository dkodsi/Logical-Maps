1. RESULTS

The first separation result is:

  C5 + OS does not imply CoSep.

There is, in fact, a C5 model satisfying full necessary Rev and therefore
necessary OS, while commuting Boolean observer separation fails at every
world. Failure concerns the weakest propositional instance, with no
quantifier qualification. It excludes every admitted predicate family,
not just uniformly indexed families or a particular choice of observers.

A second model separates the idempotent requirement:

  C5 + CoSep does not imply CoIdemSep,

even with necessary Rev and necessary CoSep. Thus replacing individual
separators by idempotents need not preserve their mutual compatibility.

Both results belong to one elementary model family M_q, with an infinite
set of independent coordinates and q possible values at each coordinate.
For each finite integer q>=2 the proposition algebra is atomless and
the model satisfies C5 and necessary full Rev. Exactly:

  M_q satisfies CoSep       iff q is 2, 3 or 4;
  M_q satisfies CoIdemSep   iff q is 2.

Positive assertions have every relational output type and every finite
qualification list, and hold at every world. Negative assertions already
refute propositional separation by Boolean observers at every world.
These are classifications of THIS model family, not cardinality tests
asserted about arbitrary C5 models.

2. THE SUPPORTED HIERARCHY

Fix a countably infinite external coordinate set J and a finite alphabet
A={0,...,q-1}. Put X=A^J with its product topology, and let B be its Boolean
algebra of clopen sets. Equivalently B consists of all subsets determined
by finitely many coordinates. Coordinates are semantic indices, not an
assumed internally enumerated family of individuals or propositions.

Let G be the full wreath-product permutation group: it permits arbitrary
permutations of J and, independently at each coordinate, arbitrary
permutations of A. It acts on X and on B by direct image. For finite
S subset J, H_S is the subgroup fixing every coordinate of S individually
and acting as the identity on its alphabet. Outside S its coordinate
and alphabet permutations are unrestricted.

An object has finite coordinate support if H_S fixes it for some finite S.
Set D_e to a singleton with trivial action, D_t=B, and recursively let
D_(sigma tau), tau relational, consist of ALL finitely supported functions
D_sigma -> D_tau. The action on functions is conjugation. Application is
ordinary function evaluation. Nonempty domains and closure under application
follow immediately; supports of finitely many parameters have finite union.

The propositions fixed by H_S are precisely B_S, the finite Boolean
algebra of sets depending only on S. Indeed independent alphabet changes
outside S erase any dependence on such coordinates; the converse is direct.
This observation is used at every stage below.

FINITE-ATTAINMENT LEMMA. Every H_S-invariant subset R of B has a join in B,
attained by finitely many members of R, and dually a finitely attained meet.
For b in R, take a finite T supporting b. Apply all alphabet permutations
on the finite set T minus S. Their finitely many images of b belong to R,
and their union is the cylinder over the S-patterns having an extension
in b. That union belongs to B_S. There are only finitely many possible
S-patterns, so finitely many such finite orbits cover the union of all R.
This proves the join assertion, including the empty-set convention.
Complementation proves the meet assertion.

For a supported predicate F:D_sigma -> B, its range is invariant under
H_S for a support S of F. Define its universal and existential values
as the intersection and union of that range. The lemma puts both in B
and makes them finitely attained. The quantifier operations are equivariant
and therefore have empty support. Boolean operations are equivariant too.

At a world x in X, truth means x belongs to the proposition. The set
intersection/union definitions give exactly the ordinary classical
quantifier clauses over the same D_sigma at every x. Unlike a generic
Boolean construction, no forcing or generic-ultrafilter argument is used.

Singleton tests y |-> 1[y=a] are admitted with a support of a. They show
that defined Leibniz identity is the crisp 0/1 test of literal equality.
The interpretation of each lambda expression is a supported function:
induct on the expression and take the union of its parameter supports.
All logical operations used in the induction are equivariant. Ordinary
function application gives beta, eta and Functionality.

These facts validate H at every world. An H-provable equivalence therefore
has identical B-valued interpretations under every assignment; abstractions
are literally identical functions. This validates precisely C's fixed
H-theoremhood gate. Native Box is the test b=X, and identity and distinctness
are crisp at every type. Thus M_q satisfies C5 at every world. All domains,
including higher function spaces, have just been specified and checked;
the proof is not merely a Boolean-algebra counterexample.

Every nonempty clopen set can be split by a fresh coordinate. Consequently
B is atomless for q>=2. The world set X separates all its elements.

3. ALL-TYPE REVERSIBLE OBSERVERS SUPPLY NECESSARY OS

Every transformation g of X which permutes alphabet values on just finitely
many coordinates induces an admitted Boolean automorphism E_g of B. Its
support is contained in those coordinates, since g commutes with H_S there.
Its inverse is admitted with the same support.

It preserves every admitted typed quantifier: inverse image under a
bijection commutes with the interpreted intersections and unions of every
predicate range. The composed predicate is admitted by function closure.
These are identities of actual functions on the unchanged full admitted
domains, so they are the required WHOLE operation identities. Both E_g
and its inverse qualify for every finite type list.

Given x in X and a nonempty clopen b, choose a finite cylinder contained
in b. Finitely many coordinate alphabet permutations send x into that
cylinder. Their observer accepts b. Boolean differences give propositional
separation; ordinary function extensionality gives an argument tuple for
any relational difference and lifts the separation to all relational types.

This proves full Rev at EVERY world, and hence its separately boxed schema,
necessary OS, necessary BF and necessary Functionality. These claims do
not rely on an internal enumeration of the coordinate permutations.

4. CLASSIFICATION OF EVERY BOOLEAN OBSERVER WHEN q>=3

Let E:B->B be any admitted Boolean observer, and choose finite S supporting
it. For i in J and a in A let p_(i,a)={x:x_i=a}. Since the images of this
finite partition form a partition, define a continuous map f:X->X by

  f(x)_i=a iff x belongs to E(p_(i,a)).

The coordinate cells generate B, so E(b)=f^{-1}[b] for every b. Equivariance
of E under H_S is exactly commutation of f with the corresponding H_S
coordinate transformations.

If i is in S, E(p_(i,a)) is H_S-fixed and therefore belongs to B_S. Thus
f(x)_i depends only on x restricted to S.
If i is outside S, E(p_(i,a)) is fixed by H_(S union {i}) and so depends
only on S union {i}. For each fixed S-pattern s, the resulting function
u_s:A->A commutes with EVERY permutation of A. When q>=3 such a function
is identity: the stabilizer of any a fixes only a, so u_s(a)=a. Therefore

  f(x)_i=x_i for every i outside S.                         (4.1)

Hence EVERY admitted Boolean observer is inverse image under a finite-
coordinate transformation: an arbitrary map on A^S, extended by identity
outside S. Conversely every such transformation induces a supported
Boolean observer. It preserves every typed quantifier because inverse
image commutes with set intersections and unions. Its operation is
admitted, and these preservation identities are whole identities as above.

Thus (4.1) classifies every potentially eligible witness, not only the
explicit reversible separators. The restriction q>=3 in this classification
is essential: at q=2 a global simultaneous bit flip is also equivariant.

5. THE SUPPORT LEMMA FOR A HYPOTHETICAL COMMUTING FAMILY

Assume q>=3 and that an admitted predicate K:(tt)t selects a commuting
Boolean separating family at a world x. By a coordinatewise alphabet
permutation we can suppose x is the all-zero point. Let S support K.

Take the external monoid M of continuous coordinate transformations
corresponding to its current members, closed under finite composition
and with identity included. All members still commute and are finite-
coordinate transformations by Section 4. This external closure is used
only to analyse an assumed family; no internal monoid existence is needed.

Its orbit Mx is dense in X: separation from Bottom says that some member
sends x into every nonempty clopen set. The group

  L=H_S intersect Stab_G(x)

normalizes M. Indeed K is H_S-equivariant, and an element fixing x preserves
the current truth of K-membership. Conjugation then preserves the generated
commutative monoid as well.

EVALUATION INJECTIVITY. If f,g in M have f(x)=g(x), then for every h in M,
commutation gives f(hx)=h(fx)=h(gx)=g(hx). Continuity and density of Mx
give f=g. This is a statement about the WHOLE coordinate transformations.

NO HIDDEN COORDINATE CHANGE. If j is outside S and f(x)_j=0, then f fixes
coordinate j on EVERY input, not merely at x.
To prove this, put y=f(x), and choose a fresh k outside S, a finite
coordinate support of f, and the finite set where y differs from x.
Also choose k!=j. Let g exchange coordinates j,k. It belongs to L and
fixes y, since y_j=y_k=0. Consequently g f g^{-1} is in M and has the
same value y at x. Evaluation injectivity gives g f g^{-1}=f. For any z,

  f(z)_j = (g f g^{-1}z)_j = f(g^{-1}z)_k = z_j,

because f fixes the fresh coordinate k. This proves the assertion.
The infinite supply of fresh coordinates is important in this argument.

6. FIVE OR MORE VALUES RULE OUT EVERY COMMUTING SEPARATING FAMILY

Assume q>=5. Choose j outside S. Density supplies f in M with f(x)_j=a!=0.
Conjugate f by all permutations of the nonzero labels at coordinate j,
fixing 0 and all other coordinates. They belong to L. For each nonzero
b this gives f_b in M with value y_b=f_b(x), where y_b and y_c differ
only at coordinate j, with respective values b,c. Evaluation injectivity
makes f_b independent of the chosen conjugating permutation. It also gives

  sigma f_b sigma^{-1} = f_(sigma b).                      (6.1)

Take distinct nonzero b,c and h_bc=f_b f_c. Its value t_bc at x is fixed
by permutations of all the other nonzero labels, since those fix both
f_b and f_c by (6.1). At least TWO such labels remain. Therefore
(t_bc)_j can only be 0,b or c. The transposition of b,c fixes h_bc by
commutativity, so it fixes t_bc too. It excludes b,c, leaving

  (t_bc)_j=0.                                             (6.2)

Permuting the nonzero labels carries any distinct pair b,c to any other
such pair. It sends t_bc to the corresponding product value but changes
only coordinate j; (6.2) says that coordinate is fixed. Thus every t_bc
is the SAME point t. Evaluation injectivity implies every h_bc is the
SAME transformation h.

Choose three distinct nonzero labels a,b,c. Associativity gives

  h f_a = (f_b f_c)f_a = f_b(f_c f_a) = f_b h = h f_b.     (6.3)

But h(x)_j=0, so the support lemma says h fixes coordinate j everywhere.
The two sides of (6.3), evaluated at x in coordinate j, are therefore a
and b respectively. This contradiction rules out K.

It rules out even Boolean CoSep(t,empty), hence every full logical CoSep.
The argument applies at every world, by the initial symmetry reduction.
Together with Section 3 this proves

  M_5 satisfies C5 + Box Rev + Box not CoSep(t,empty).

In particular the requested C5+OS converse and the stronger converse
from C5+necessary OS are both false. Reversibility itself does not supply
a mutually commuting separating family.

7. THREE OR MORE VALUES RULE OUT COMMUTING IDEMPOTENT SEPARATION

Now assume only q>=3 and additionally suppose the selected family consists
of idempotents. Its commuting monoid M then consists of idempotents too.
Use f_b for every nonzero label as in Section 6 and put

  h = product_(b!=0) f_b.

Every permutation of the nonzero labels preserves this product, so h(x)_j
is fixed by their full symmetric group. There are at least two nonzero
labels, so the sole fixed label is 0. The support lemma therefore makes
h fix coordinate j everywhere. On the other hand idempotence and
commutativity give h f_a=h for every nonzero a. At x in coordinate j,
the left side is a and the right side is 0, a contradiction.

This proves failure of CoIdemSep(t,empty) for every q>=3, including the
models with positive CoSep in the next section.

8. THE POSITIVE SMALL-ALPHABET CASES ARE ADMITTED FAMILIES

For q=2,3,4 the symmetric group on A has a canonical normal abelian
regular subgroup N_q:

  q=2: S_2;
  q=3: A_3, the cyclic group of order 3;
  q=4: {identity and the three double transpositions}, the Klein group.

Regular means that some member sends any specified letter to any other.
Let T be the group of finite-coordinate transformations which use members
of N_q independently on their finitely many active coordinates. It is
abelian. Because N_q is normal in S_q and the definition treats coordinates
uniformly, T is invariant under conjugation by the full group G.

Define K on admitted proposition operations to have value Top exactly
on the inverse-image operations of members of T, and Bottom otherwise.
This function is G-equivariant, hence is an admitted predicate with empty
support. Its members are the fully qualified reversible observers of
Section 3, commute, and send any world into any specified finite cylinder.
Thus this ONE admitted family separates at every world and at every
relational output type. It proves necessary full CoSep for q=2,3,4.

For q=2 there is also an admitted commuting IDEMPOTENT family. For each
finite partial binary assignment s let r_s overwrite precisely those
coordinates and let E_s be inverse image under r_s. Put

  K(E_s)=[bar s],    K(E)=Bottom for other E,

where [bar s] is the cylinder prescribing the opposite bit on each
coordinate of s. Distinct s give distinct operations, so this is a
function. Binary complement commutes with every alphabet relabelling;
consequently this K is G-equivariant and has empty support.

At a world x its members overwrite selected coordinates with the unique
opposite values. All current assignments are compatible and their resets
commute; every reset is idempotent and fully qualified. Given a finite
target pattern t, reset just the coordinates where x differs from t.
This is a current K-member and sends x into its cylinder. Thus necessary
full CoIdemSep holds. This is the binary-reset argument from the earlier
Cohen model, here in a clopen finite-support model without forcing.

Combining Sections 6-8 gives the exact classifications in Section 1.

9. CONSEQUENCES, SCOPE AND SOURCES

The q=5 model separates Rev from CoSep even when both are considered
over C5 and with their necessary instances. The q=3 model separates
CoSep from CoIdemSep under the same robust modal background. By the
earlier uniform-retraction theorem, uniformly indexed commuting
idempotent separation would imply Atomicity; the q=2 predicate-family
example is atomless and illustrates again why indexing is stronger.

The complete proof is internal to ordinary set-based supported-model
mathematics above. It uses no forcing, large cardinals, tree construction,
prohibited individual-output function, or external type quantification.
The finite-alphabet labels are semantic values, not assumed extra logical
constants. Truth at every world and the actual admitted higher domains
were checked before deriving the observer classification.
