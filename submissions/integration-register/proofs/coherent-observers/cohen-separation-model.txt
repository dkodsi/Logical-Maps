1. RESULTS AND THE EXISTING MODEL CONSTRUCTION USED

There is an ordinary-ZFC Henkin model satisfying C5 in which:

  (a) an admitted predicate family of pairwise commuting, idempotent,
      fully qualified observers separates at every relational type;
      its current members form a commutative idempotent monoid;
  (b) a single admitted operation Phi:t(tt) indexes a separating
      family of pairwise commuting INVOLUTIVE logical observers;
  (c) there are no propositional atoms, and no family as in the
      uniformly indexed IDEMPOTENT characterization;
  (d) all-type Relational Choice, Transversal Choice and Zorn hold.

All these verdicts hold at every world, hence their separately boxed
forms hold. In (a) the same predicate parameter works at every world,
but which reset maps belong to it can vary with the world. In (b)
the actual range of the indexed operation consists of fixed finite
bit flips. The two witness families are different.

Use the existing generic Boolean Henkin construction with
B=RO(2^N), the complete atomless algebra of regular open subsets
of Cantor space. The full ambient relational hierarchy has
A_e=N, A_t=B, and all functions at the permitted relational arrows.
Take a sufficiently large H_theta and countable elementary M
containing this hierarchy and the additional maps defined below.
Restrict to D_sigma=M intersect A_sigma. Worlds are M-generic
ultrafilters; the existing truth lemma interprets all admitted
quantifiers and abstraction and gives C5 with literal identity.

These are construction dependencies, not a claim that arbitrary
ultrafilters preserve infinitary meets. A complete Boolean
endomorphism in M is an admitted observer qualified at every
finite list. See ../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/h-observer-space/generic-boolean-separation-test.md, Section 2.
The construction's boxed RC, TC and Zorn verdicts follow from
../../../classicism-results-2026-10-24-v2/proofs/h-observer-space/generic-boolean-choice-model.md, Sections 1-4.

2. FINITE RESETS GIVE COMPLETE IDEMPOTENT ENDOMORPHISMS

Let s be a finite partial map from N to {0,1}. Define r_s on
Cantor space by overwriting the coordinates in dom(s) with s and
leaving every other coordinate unchanged. It is continuous and open
as a map into Cantor space: its image is the clopen cylinder [s],
and the image of each basic cylinder is a basic cylinder or empty.

For a continuous open map r, inverse image commutes with both
closure and interior. For closure, a neighborhood of x has open
image, which meets U whenever r(x) belongs to cl(U); the reverse
inclusion uses continuity. For interior, map a witnessing open
neighborhood forward for one direction and use continuity for the
other. Thus r^{-1}(int cl U)=int cl(r^{-1}U).

Consequently r_s^{-1} maps regular opens to regular opens and
preserves their regular-open Boolean complements and arbitrary
joins and meets. Put E_s(U)=r_s^{-1}(U). It is a complete Boolean
endomorphism and E_s^2=E_s. If s and t agree on their common domain,
their resets commute and E_s E_t=E_t E_s=E_(s union t).

Distinct finite assignments give distinct maps on B, as can be
checked on one-coordinate clopens. In particular E_empty=I.

3. AN ADMITTED PREDICATE CHOOSES A COHERENT RESET FAMILY

For s let bar(s) assign 1-s(n) on the same domain. Define the ambient
function K:A_tt -> B by

  K(E_s)=[bar(s)] for finite s;
  K(E)=0 for every other E.

The uniqueness just proved makes this well-defined. It is an
ordinary member of the full ambient predicate domain, definable
from the coded reset sequence, so arrange K and that sequence in M.
Its restriction is therefore admitted in the Henkin model.

Every current K-member is some E_s, hence fully qualified and
idempotent. If s,t conflict on an overlap, [bar(s)] and [bar(t)]
are disjoint, so K(E_s) and K(E_t) cannot both be true at a world.
Otherwise their maps commute. Thus the whole-operation commutation
condition holds for every pair of current members at every world.
Identity belongs since K(I)=1. If two members are current, their
compatible union is current too because

  [bar(s union t)]=[bar(s)] intersect [bar(t)].

The current family is therefore a commutative idempotent monoid.

To prove separation, take any nonzero b in B and choose a basic
cylinder [t] contained in b. For each subset J of dom(t), write
s_J=t restricted to J. The finitely many clopen sets

  K(E_sJ) intersect E_sJ([t])

cover Cantor space. Indeed, for any point x let
J={n in dom(t): x(n)!=t(n)}. Then x belongs to [bar(s_J)] and
r_sJ(x) belongs to [t]. Therefore

  join_J (K(E_sJ) meet E_sJ(b)) = 1.                            (7)

At every ultrafilter, at least one term of this FINITE join is
true. For b in M the chosen cylinder and finite assignments can
be taken in M by elementarity (or by the standard finite cylinder
codes). Hence every nonzero admitted proposition is accepted by
a current K-member. Symmetric difference proves propositional
separation; C5 Functionality lifts it to every relational type.
Every reset has all the requested whole quantifier laws, so the
same K works at every finite qualification list.

This verification avoids postulating the generic point as an
admitted object or parameter. K is defined using finite cylinders
already in the ground hierarchy. Membership varies through their
Boolean truth values.

4. A UNIFORMLY INDEXED COMMUTING INVOLUTION FAMILY

For each finite subset S of N, flip the bits in S. This is a
homeomorphism h_S; its inverse-image map A_S on B is a complete
Boolean automorphism. All A_S commute and A_S^2=I.

The finitely many flips with support inside dom(t) carry a cylinder
[t] through every bit pattern on that domain. Their inverse images
of [t] cover the whole space. Thus for every nonzero b,

  join_{S finite} A_S(b)=1,

and a finite subjoin already witnesses this for each b. Consequently
the flip family separates at every generic world.

It has an admitted operation index of type t, not just a predicate
presentation. Enumerate the finite subsets as S_n, and fix distinct
nonzero clopens b_n=[0^n 1]. In the full ambient hierarchy define

  Phi(b_n)=A_(S_n);   Phi(b)=I for every other b.

This is an actual set-function A_t -> A_tt and hence belongs to
the full ambient function space. Include it and its defining
sequences in M. All standard n and b_n belong to M. The admitted
restricted operation has exactly the desired range on the
restricted domain, and its whole qualification, commutation and
involution equations hold. The finite-cover argument gives
propositional separation; Functionality again gives the relational
instances. These facts hold at all generic worlds.

This is no use of Functional Choice IN the model. The ambient ZFC
construction admits this explicitly described particular operation.

5. ATOMLESSNESS AND THE STRICT SEPARATIONS

For every nonzero b in M intersect B, atomlessness of B provides
0<c<b. Elementarity returns such c in M. Algebraic order and
identity are literal in the construction, so there are no admitted
propositional atoms at any world.

By uniform-retractions.txt, C5 rules out EVERY uniformly indexed
commuting idempotent separating family at any admitted index type.
Thus the admitted predicate K from Section 3 cannot be reindexed
by an admitted operation which retains qualification, commutation,
idempotence and separation. It is not merely the displayed list
of reset codes which fails to provide a convenient indexing.

The model proves both that predicate-family coherence does not
force Atomicity, and that idempotence in the uniform theorem
cannot be replaced by involution. Its strong relational-choice
verdicts survive, so relational selection does not remove the
distinction between these two forms of family.

For a converse contrast, the full standard three-world Gallin model
satisfies Atomicity and hence uniform commuting IDEMPOTENT separation.
It admits no commuting INVOLUTIVE separating family. Its Boolean
observers are inverse images of self-maps of the three worlds;
involutions are permutations. Commuting involutions generate an
elementary abelian 2-group. Separation at a world requires its
orbit to contain all three worlds, but every finite orbit of such
a group has power-of-two size. This contradiction uses no restriction
on how the proposed family is indexed.
