1. RESULTS

  C5 + full CoSep does NOT imply necessary CoSep.

More strongly there is a C5 model satisfying full current CoIdemSep and
having an endpoint-valued predicate of commuting fully qualified observers
which separates every relational type, but refuting even

  Box OS_(t,empty).

The same model satisfies necessary full BF and Functionality. Its failure
of propagation is not caused by changes in family membership: the specified
commuting family has rigid membership. It loses separation at an accessible
isolated world. Its current idempotent family is a second witness and need
not have rigid membership.

The construction uses ordinary clopen sets and finite support. It requires
neither forcing, generic filters, countable models of set theory, nor large
cardinals. All quantifiers have their ordinary truth clauses at every world.

2. WORLD SET, SYMMETRIES AND ALL HIGHER DOMAINS

Let J be a countably infinite external set and X={0,1}^J. Adjoin an isolated
point *, and put W={*} disjoint-union X. The proposition algebra is

  B=Clop(W)=2 x Clop(X).

Write a=(1,empty) for the isolated atom and b=(0,X) for its complement.
Both are nonzero, and a or b=Top. A proposition (epsilon,c) is true at *
iff epsilon=1, and true at x in X iff x belongs to c.

G is the full binary wreath group: independent flips of coordinate values
and arbitrary permutations of J. Every g fixes *. For finite S subset J,
H_S fixes coordinates in S and their values, and is unrestricted outside S.
An object is supported when some H_S fixes it. Set D_e to a singleton,
D_t=B, and at every permitted arrow let D_(sigma tau) contain ALL supported
actual functions D_sigma -> D_tau, with conjugation action.

The support filter is invariant under conjugation; finite unions of supports
are supports. Application preserves support. Every proposition is supported.

3. FINITE ATTAINMENT, QUANTIFIERS, AND C5

The H_S-fixed algebra is the finite algebra 2 x B_S, where B_S consists of
binary clopens depending only on S. Every H_S-invariant subset R of B has
its literal union and intersection in B, each attained by finitely many
members (with the empty-family conventions).

For the union, take r=(epsilon,c) in R. The orbit of c under flips of a
finite support outside S is finite. Its union is the cylinder over the
S-patterns extending into c. The first bit epsilon is unchanged. There
are only finitely many S-patterns and two possible first bits. Finitely
many such finite orbits therefore attain the union of R. Apply complements
for the intersection. This is the finite-attainment lemma from the earlier
finite-alphabet construction, with one additional invariant Boolean bit.

For supported F:D_sigma->B, the range of F is invariant under a support
stabilizer of F. Define Q_sigma(F) as its actual intersection and existential
quantification as its actual union. The lemma puts these in B. Quantifier
maps are equivariant and admitted with empty support. Boolean operations
are equivariant too. The literal set operations give exactly the classical
quantifier truth clauses at EVERY point of W.

Parameter-sensitive term induction proves lambda closure: the union of
the finitely many parameter supports supports the term denotation. Beta,
eta and Functionality hold as identities of actual functions. Every object
u has the supported endpoint test v |-> Top if v=u, Bottom otherwise.
Thus Leibniz identity is literally Top for equal objects and Bottom for
distinct objects, at every type. Native Box tests equality with Top.

H is sound at every point. H-theorem equivalences have identical B-values
under every assignment, hence their lambda abstractions are identical
actual functions. This validates precisely C's fixed H-theoremhood gate.
Crisp identity gives boxed ND at every type, so C5 holds at every world.
Literal quantifier intersections also make all BF instances Top: both
forall u.Box F(u) and Box forall u.F(u) are Top exactly when every F(u)
is Top, and are Bottom otherwise. Functionality is likewise necessarily
true. No optional-premise equivalence is used as a C identity rule.

4. ONE RIGID COMMUTING FAMILY SEPARATES AT EVERY x IN X

For finite T subset J let f_T fix * and flip exactly the coordinates T on X.
Let E_T be inverse image under f_T. Also let r:W->W be the constant map
with value *, and E_* its inverse-image operation:

  E_*(epsilon,c)=(epsilon, epsilon X).

Each map is continuous. E_T is supported by T; E_* is equivariant. Inverse
image preserves the literal Boolean operations and all admitted quantifier
intersections and unions. Function closure admits the composed predicates.
Consequently every E_T and E_* is qualified at every type as a WHOLE
operation identity. Their compositions obey

  E_T E_U = E_(T symmetric-difference U),
  E_* E_T = E_T E_* = E_*,        E_* E_*=E_*.

Define K(E)=Top exactly when E=E_* or E=E_T for some finite T, and Bottom
otherwise. This is an actual function on the whole admitted tt-domain.
The specified set is invariant under G: coordinate permutations permute
the finite flip sets, arbitrary flips commute with them, and E_* is fixed.
Hence K has empty support and is an admitted endpoint-valued predicate.
Its members commute at all worlds, and its membership is rigid.

Fix x in X. Every nonzero proposition d=(epsilon,c) is accepted by a current
member: use E_* if epsilon=1; otherwise c is nonempty, and finitely many
flips send x into a finite cylinder contained in c. Apply this to the
Boolean symmetric difference to separate unequal propositions. Functionality
lifts it to all relational types. Thus this one K witnesses EVERY finite
CoSep instance at x, with all-type qualification already proved.

5. A COMMUTING IDEMPOTENT FAMILY ALSO SEPARATES THERE

For each finite partial binary assignment s let r_s fix * and overwrite
the indicated coordinates on X with s. Its inverse-image operation E_s is
supported, idempotent, and fully qualified by the same set-operation proof.
Distinct s give distinct operations, and none is E_*.

Define L on the entire admitted operation domain by

  L(E_*)=Top;
  L(E_s)=(0,[bar s]);
  L(E)=Bottom otherwise,

where [bar s] prescribes the bit opposite s on its finite domain. This
definition is G-equivariant, since binary complementation commutes with
all binary value relabellings. Thus L is admitted with empty support.

At x in X its current reset members set every selected bit to the opposite
of x's bit. Overlapping assignments agree, so these reset operations commute.
They also commute with E_*, since all r_s fix *. Every member is idempotent.
To reach a specified finite cylinder, reset just those coordinates where x
differs from its pattern. E_* handles the isolated atom. Thus L witnesses
full CoIdemSep at every x in X.

6. AT THE ISOLATED POINT NO BOOLEAN OBSERVERS SEPARATE

Let E:B->B be ANY admitted Boolean observer and choose finite S supporting
it. The function h_E(d)=1 iff * belongs to E(d) is a Boolean homomorphism
B->2, hence defines an ultrafilter U_E. Because * is fixed by G and E
commutes with H_S, this ultrafilter is H_S-invariant.

Suppose b=(0,X) belongs to U_E. Choose j outside S and partition b into

  p=(0,{x:x_j=0}),    q=(0,{x:x_j=1}).

An ultrafilter containing b contains exactly one of p,q. But flipping j
lies in H_S and interchanges p,q, so H_S-invariance says it contains either
both or neither. Contradiction. Thus b is not in U_E. The ultrafilter
contains a, the isolated atom, and is its principal ultrafilter. Therefore

  * belongs to E(epsilon,c) iff epsilon=1.                       (3)

Every admitted Boolean observer preserves actual truth at *. In particular
none distinguishes the different propositions b and Bottom there.
Even OS_(t,empty) fails, and therefore every stronger propositional CoSep
instance fails. This excludes ALL admitted witnesses, not just K and L.

The ordinary CoSep_(t,{t}) and CoIdemSep_(t,{t}) sentences consequently
have exact B-value b: true at every point of X and false at *. Their boxes
have value Bottom, since b!=Top. Choosing any x in X as the designated
world proves all non-entailments in Section 1. The two evaluations use
the SAME higher domains and logical operations; * is a native possibility
because its atom a is nonzero.

7. INTERPRETATION AND SOURCE BOUNDARIES

The coherent family can reach both components from a binary world, but
the isolated point is fixed by every admitted observer. Necessary identity
does not make these observation routes reversible. A stable family can
therefore lose its ability to distinguish propositions at another world.

This is an original isolated-point variant of the finite-support clopen
hierarchy in finite-alphabet-coherence-countermodels.txt, Sections 2-3 and 8.
It replaces the heavier generic-point construction previously used for
current OS without necessary OS. That older construction is not needed
for this proof. The binary reset idea is retained from the earlier model;
the new ingredients are the isolated point, its absorbing reader, the
rigid commuting family, and the universal failure argument at that point.

A positive boundary follows from an existing theorem: full Rev implies
No Pure Contingency. Every CoSep instance is a closed pure formula, so
Rev + CoSep DOES imply separately necessary CoSep (and similarly for
CoIdemSep when it is assumed). This is a corollary of the existing Rev
theorem, not a new proof of No Pure Contingency. The present countermodel
necessarily lacks Rev, as its pure CoSep sentence is contingent.
