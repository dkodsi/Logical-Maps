1. THEOREM

Let M be any pointed general model of Classicism C in the fixed relational
type system. Let E:tt be an admitted observer satisfying whole Boolean
preservation, whole preservation of each typed universal quantifier, and
the whole idempotence identity E composed E=E. Let K=Core(M,E) be its
canonical all-sort fixed-test quotient core.

For EACH single admitted pair of types sigma,tau, with tau relational,

  M satisfies Box FC_(sigma,tau)
       implies K satisfies Box FC_(sigma,tau).                (1.1)

Consequently every such core of a model of Choice Classicism

  C + Box Functional Choice

is again a model of Choice Classicism. No BF, ND, Atomicity, C5 or
Functionality is needed as a separate assumption for the instancewise
theorem. A single boxed choice instance suffices for the corresponding
boxed instance in the core; the other choice instances are not used.

The proof gives the stronger comparison of ambient proposition values

  E(FC_(sigma,tau)) <= (FC_(sigma,tau))^r,                    (1.2)

where the right side is the normalized interpretation of the core choice
formula, BEFORE taking the quotient, and <= is the ambient algebraic
Boolean order. Hence the weaker condition E(FC_(sigma,tau))=Top already
implies the boxed instance in K. Current truth of E(FC_(sigma,tau))
implies current truth of that choice instance in K.

These are sufficient conditions. No reflection or equality of the two
propositions in (1.2) is asserted.

2. THE EXISTING CORE AND ITS EXACT MODAL MEANING

Use the completed bare-C construction in

  research/2026-10-26/observer-core-without-barcan/general-core.txt,
  especially Sections 2-6.

It defines normalizers recursively by

  r_e=id externally,   r_t=E,
  r_(alpha beta)(f)=lambda x. r_beta(f(r_alpha(x))).          (2.1)

At an individual input omit the notation r_e. No forbidden ee object is
introduced. The whole identity r_alpha composed r_alpha=r_alpha follows
equationally. Put S_alpha=Fix(r_alpha). Fixed functions are invariant
under input normalization and have fixed outputs as WHOLE identities:

  f composed r_alpha=f,       r_beta composed f=f.           (2.2)

Translate a core term T to T^r by normalizing every variable and logical
constant, preserving application and lambda abstraction. For each finite
collection of terms and formulas, all its normalization identities are
C-derivable from a finite conjunction P(E) of the required WHOLE observer
identities. In particular,

  r_type(T)(T^r)=T^r.                                      (2.3)

Translated Boolean connectives are the original connectives on translated
bodies. Translated quantifiers have the exact term identities

  (forall x.A)^r=forall x.A^r,
  (exists x.A)^r=exists x.A^r.                              (2.4)

The right side still quantifies over original objects, but each bound
variable occurrence inside A^r is normalized. Its range therefore has
the right fixed-domain truth clause. These are term equations furnished
by the qualified observer laws, not an assertion that arbitrary current
families have external Boolean infima.

Fixed objects are then quotiented by agreement in current truth under
all fixed predicates. The resulting K is a C model with literal current
identity. Its interpretation of any formula A is [A^r]. In particular,

  K satisfies Box A iff [A^r]=[Top].                        (2.5)

The ambient equality A^r=Top is sufficient for (2.5). We will prove that
STRONGER equality. We do not identify native core Box with ambient Box,
and we do not replace translated Leibniz identity by ambient identity.

3. THE CURRENT CHOICE ARGUMENT IS AN INTERNAL DERIVATION

Fix sigma,tau with tau relational. Write F for the closed formula

  forall U^(sigma tau t).
    (forall x^sigma. exists y^tau. (U x)y)
      -> exists f^(sigma tau). forall x^sigma. (U x)(f x).    (3.1)

Let P(E) include the finitely many whole observer laws needed for the
normalizers and formula (3.1). Besides E-idempotence and Boolean laws,
only finitely many typed quantifier laws can occur, including the
quantifiers at the relation, input, output and function types. The finite
normalization lemma of Section 2 supplies all auxiliary equations from
this same finite P. No object-language quantification over types is used.

We prove the unconditional C theorem

  P(E) -> (F -> F^r).                                     (3.2)

Here is the derivation explicitly. For an arbitrary original U, set
R=r_(sigma tau t)(U). Equations (2.2) imply both input invariances

  (R(r_sigma(x)))y=(R x)y,
  (R x)(r_tau(y))=(R x)y.                                 (3.3)

By (2.4), the antecedent of the translated instance for U is literally

  forall x. exists y. (R(r_sigma(x)))(r_tau(y)),             (3.4)

and (3.3) identifies this with ordinary ambient seriality of R. Apply
the assumed formula F to the admitted relation R. Obtain an original
f:sigma tau with

  forall u. (R u)(f u).                                   (3.5)

The translated existential quantifier over functions permits the same
original f as its witness; its occurrences normalize to

  g=r_(sigma tau)(f).

For each original x, put a=r_sigma(x). Idempotence and (2.1) give

  g(a)=r_tau(f(a)).                                       (3.6)

The translated concluding matrix is

  (R a)(g(a))=(R a)(r_tau(f(a)))=(R a)(f(a)),                (3.7)

which holds by (3.5) instantiated at a. Universal generalization over x,
existential introduction using f, and generalization over U establish
F^r. Every operation in this argument is an admitted term; every step
is ordinary internal C reasoning from the displayed assumptions.

In particular, this is not merely an external observation that one
specific model has a selector. It is a uniform derivation of (3.2).
There is no need to choose representatives of quotient classes.

4. NECESSITATE THE DERIVATION, NOT THE OPTIONAL CHOICE AXIOM

Every conjunct of P(E) is a whole object identity. Native Necessity of
Identity and finite conjunction therefore give the C theorem

  P(E) -> Box P(E).                                       (4.1)

Necessitate the UNCONDITIONAL C theorem (3.2), then use K and (4.1):

  C proves P(E) -> Box(F -> F^r).                          (4.2)

For full syntactic precision, universally close E and any remaining
parameters before applying the theorem-Necessitation rule. Necessitated
universal elimination then gives the desired instance of the boxed
conditional for the specified E. No Barcan movement of Box through a
universal quantifier is involved.

Since C identifies algebraic order p<=q with Box(p -> q), (4.2) says

  F <= F^r                                                (4.3)

in every model satisfying the whole observer laws. If M satisfies
Box F, native literal identity says F=Top. Equation (4.3) forces
F^r=Top. Passing to the fixed-test quotient gives [F^r]=[Top], and (2.5)
proves K satisfies Box F. This proves (1.1).

Alternatively, modus ponens under Box in (4.2) and Box F gives the
ambient Box F^r directly. Its native meaning is again F^r=Top.

This does not infer Box F from current F. The boxed hypothesis is used
only in the final step. Necessitation was applied solely to a theorem
of C, and stability was supplied solely by identity premises.

5. THE STRONGER OBSERVED-CHOICE SUFFICIENT CONDITIONS

E is a Boolean homomorphism, so it preserves algebraic order. Apply E
to (4.3), then use the fixedness of the translated formula:

  E(F) <= E(F^r)=F^r.                                    (5.1)

If E(F) is currently true, upward closure of the ambient truth filter
makes F^r true. Quotient truth then makes F true in K. If E(F)=Top,
(5.1) forces F^r=Top and hence Box F in K.

Ordinary ambient Box F implies E(F)=Top because E(Top)=Top. Thus the
main theorem is an immediate special case of (5.1). This comparison is
stronger than the existing current-FC descent result, but the central
new consequence is the necessary-FC closure of all logical cores.

6. GENERAL STABLE-PREMISE TRANSFER LEMMA

The proof isolates a reusable principle. Suppose A and B are formulas
closed over their displayed object variables, and a finite conjunction
P(E) of whole observer identities yields an internal C derivation

  P(E) -> (A -> B^r).

Suppose P also supplies fixedness E(B^r)=B^r. Then C proves

  P(E) -> Box(A -> B^r),

and in the associated fixed hierarchy

  E(A) <= B^r.

Therefore current observed A suffices for core B, and E(A)=Top suffices
for core Box B. In particular ambient Box A suffices for core Box B.
This is a transfer lemma for an INTERNAL uniform derivation; it does
not turn arbitrary external current inheritance arguments into modal
inheritance claims. The FC derivation in Section 3 meets its hypotheses.

7. SCOPE, SOURCES AND SIGNIFICANCE

The theorem includes general Henkin models, arbitrary image observers,
and cores whose all-sort quotient is nontrivial. It uses no external
fullness, countable completeness, choice of quotient representatives,
or metatheoretic selector construction. Named constants can be included
in the ambient language and normalized as usual; the choice formulas
themselves are pure and unaffected by that expansion.

For polyadic Functional Choice, repeat Section 3 with a finite tuple of
inputs and its componentwise normalizers. Alternatively use the native
proved equivalence of the unary schema and its polyadic form, together
with its necessitated version. There is no output operation returning e.

The main proof is a short but substantive corollary of the more involved
bare-C core construction: the new point is that its current selector
argument has an internal uniform proof with stable whole-identity
premises. No appeal to Atomicity inheritance or No Pure Contingency is
needed. Current Functional Choice or current Plenitude alone must not
be used to infer Atomicity.
