# Pure Maximalism and ordinary current analysis

25 October 2026. OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi.

## The compatibility claim

There is a model of pure Maximalist Classicism with a current real number
structure at type t whose analytical interpretation is the full standard
real field. In fact every current real number structure in that model has
the standard truths of first- and second-order analysis. This is
compatibility with ordinary current analysis, not its necessity.

The real structures use the database's relational vocabulary: addition
and multiplication are total unique graphs on the field. The result does
not assert that these graphs have ambient operation representatives, or
that coextensive higher-type entities are identical.

## Construction

List all C-consistent closed pure sentences as phi_i, indexed by external
natural numbers. This list need not be effective; the language is
countable. Choose a countable typed Henkin C model M_i satisfying phi_i.
The countable BBK completeness theorem supplies these models. Every
proposition carrier has at least two members, bottom and top.

Put D_e at the new root equal to the external natural numbers and choose
a map from it to each component individual carrier, for example a
constant map. For every relational type tau=sigma_1 ... sigma_k t,
recursively put

    D_tau(root) = P(D_sigma_1(root) × ... × D_sigma_k(root))
                  × product_i D_tau(M_i).

At t the first factor is the two current truth values. Application slices
the root extension and applies each component coordinate to the projected
argument. Lambda abstraction combines the current extension of its body
with the entire component lambda denotation under projected free
parameters. A component-bound variable ranges over the whole component
domain. Quantifiers likewise use the whole local domain in each
coordinate. Equality is literal identity currently and component identity
in each component coordinate.

These are the free-current coalescence clauses. They validate H, beta and
eta coordinatewise. For Logical Equivalence use its actual H-theorem
certificate: equivalent abstractions have the same current extension and
are identical in every component, hence are literally the same root
entity. No equivalence depending on an optional premise is used.

For each phi_i, its root denotation has a true i-th component. It therefore
differs from bottom, so diamond phi_i holds at the root. Thus the root
satisfies pure Maximalism in its possibility presentation.

## The real field and its full analytical interpretation

The root proposition carrier is 2 × product_i D_t(M_i). Each factor is
countable and has at least two members, so the carrier has cardinality
exactly continuum. Choose a bijection between it and the external reals.
Use this bijection to give the root carrier the usual order, addition and
multiplication graphs, zero, one and natural-number predicate. Every one
of these extensions is represented by a root relation: its component
coordinates can be arbitrary, for example bottoms.

Every external subset of the field, and every external relation of each
fixed finite arity, is a current extension of some admitted root entity.
Consequently bounded completeness quantifies over every actual subset,
not merely a Henkin subcollection. The field is a real number structure
in the exact sense of the database. Its least inductive subproperty is
the external standard natural-number subset, because that subset itself
is available and ordinary external induction proves minimality.

Induction on an analytical sentence now identifies its current truth
with its usual truth in the full standard real field. Multiple intensional
representatives of one relation cause no problem: the analytical language
tests these relation variables by application, and their quantifiers range
over all extensions. Higher relation identity is not an extra atomic
formula in this language. Ordinary element identity is literal equality.

The same fullness makes every other current real number structure a
genuinely Dedekind-complete ordered field externally. It is isomorphic to
the standard reals, with the same finite-arity relation ranges, and hence
agrees on every first- and second-order analytical sentence. The argument
also applies to such structures at other fixed types when they exist.

## Consequences and boundary

Pure Max is compatible with all the standard-true current analytical
sentences simultaneously. This does not say that Max proves all of them.
Nor does it produce a necessary real structure. The chosen current graphs
have unconstrained component behavior, and component Henkin structures
can disagree about arithmetic and analysis.

This therefore fits the upstream results that Max refutes Necessity of
Analysis and non-contingency of CH. The distinction is between ordinary
current mathematics and its modal invariance, and between graphs and
ambient operation representatives. It is not a lack of ordinary current
real analysis.
