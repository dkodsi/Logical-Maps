# Numerical stability, higher-type recursion and finite realization

**Contribution:** New equivalences and representation theorems  

At a fixed counting type, predecessor existence is equivalent to necessary separation of every current number from the bottom cardinality, and hence to numerical noncollapse plus numerical ND. The guarded transport, inverse and section principles have this numerical basis; it is weaker than full ND or Plenitude in the specified models.

A single proposition-valued bit alternating along successor yields primitive recursion into every relational output type, including operation types, without Functional Choice; conversely recursion supplies the bit. The construction covers internal numbers, including nonstandard ones.

On an internally Frege-finite domain, a total single-valued graph has an operation representative exactly when it respects every possible identification of inputs and outputs. The proof uses internal cardinality induction.

The operational real-field examples show both the limitations under Max and a positive infinite-chain model with rich stable analysis while ND fails. Scope restrictions on arbitrary ambient representatives and on the canonical numerical core are retained in the proofs.

**Proof length:** Long (about 12,100 words across the linked proofs). The substantial components are higher-type recursion, internal cardinality induction and the model constructions.
