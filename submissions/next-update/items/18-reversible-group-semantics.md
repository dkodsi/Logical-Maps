# Rev has a direct group representation

**Contribution:** Direct representation of every C + Rev model, with sound and strongly complete group semantics.

Propositions are subsets of a group; higher types contain ordinary Henkin functions, quantifiers are intersections, and admitted group shifts supply the reversible observers. Every C + Rev model has this presentation **without an elementary extension or enlargement of its typed domains**.

The key is that an admitted invertible meet map preserves every admitted quantifier infimum once Rev supplies BF. The same operation therefore qualifies at every type. Its inverse makes equality crisp at every index and induces coherent changes of coordinates at higher types.

In a countable language, countable models indexed by the free group on countably many generators suffice for strong completeness. The representation also explains Rev ⇒ No Pure Contingency: pure terms are invariant under every change of coordinates.
