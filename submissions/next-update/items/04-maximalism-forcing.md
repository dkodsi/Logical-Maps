# Strict Forcing Possibilism is independent of pure Maximalism

**Contribution:** New verdicts for existing models  
**Depends on:** 01-explanation-corrections; both amend the definable-root record.

The definable-profile root satisfies strict unboxed Forcing Possibilism at every carrier type; the pinned-interpretation and pinned-substitution roots refute it. These positive and negative witnesses settle the compatibility question without changing pure Maximalism's reference theory.

The proof establishes infinitely many Fregean atoms in the countable definable root and realizes every eligible finite Boolean algebra as a proper interval. Its infinite-algebra clause is inapplicable. This compatibility is therefore not a result about realizing infinite forcing.

The proof also gives pure Max ⇒ ¬☐FP, the non-strict variant in the countable definable root, and qualified continuum-sized constructions. These remain in the proof: there is no existing boxed-FP principle ID, and the unqualified product families do not inherit a size-restricted verdict.

**Proof length:** Medium (about 2,500 words across the linked proofs). The finite-interval criterion, Fregean atoms and the two support arguments are separate ingredients.
