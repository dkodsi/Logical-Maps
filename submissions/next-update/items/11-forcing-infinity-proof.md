# Forcing Possibilism implies quantified Infinity: alternative proof

**Contribution:** Alternative proof of an existing result  

The proof establishes the existing implication from unboxed Forcing Possibilism to Axiom of Infinity (type t) over bare C. Two internal inductions show that a finite proposition algebra is complete and graph-Dedekind-finite. FP would identify that whole algebra with a proper interval, contradicting graph-Dedekind-finiteness.

**Proof length:** Short (about 700 words).

[Full proof](../proofs/forcing-possibilism-internal-infinity.md) · [Native patch](../patches/06-forcing-infinity-proof.patch)

Only FP's finite disjunct and proper-interval requirement are used. No ND, Choice, Actuality or external enumeration is assumed.
