# Exact finite-collapse classification under pure Max

**Contribution:** New external representation theorem  

A set-sized finitary many-sorted algebra, with at most one carrier at each admitted type and no positive-arity e-valued operations, has a freely parameterized operational realization under pure Max preserving specified necessary distinctions exactly when one common quotient respects all operation tables, separates those pairs, and has at most cτ elements at type τ. Here ce = 1, ct = 2 and cαρ = cρ raised to cα.

The converse includes base type t. One extra full S5 component supplies injective current profiles; the remaining components implement the finite quotient. The result is external over ZFC, not an internal quotient-existence principle. Preassigned logical operations have the necessary condition, but do not receive the unrestricted converse.

The companion examples include finite operational fields with protected zero/one, type-sensitive integer division, pairing, stacks and shifts. Ordinary graph-based analysis can remain available.

**Proof length:** Long (about 10,000 words across the linked proofs). Simultaneous finite-diagram compactness and whole-object coalescence, including the separating S5 component.
