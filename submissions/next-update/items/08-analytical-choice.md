# Functional Choice: conservation and analytical separation

**Contribution:** New comparison with imported classical separator  
**Depends on:** 07-analytical-shadow for the conditional independence from Max only.

C + ☐Functional Choice + quantified Infinity at e,t and the corresponding ☐Plenitude package have the same parameter-free Π¹₄ analytical consequences. The comparison also reaches the stated weaker numerical bases in the proof.

The equivalence does not extend to all analytical sentences. The explicit constructible-countable-collection principle has a Π¹₅ universal presentation and a fixed-empty-parameter Σ¹₄ instance. Functional Choice proves it; the Feferman–Lévy interpretation refutes it in the boxed-Plenitude package. Under Con(ZFC + TrueΠ⁰₁), it and its negation are both consistent with pure Max, using the unrestricted shadow theorem.

The classical constructibility, conservation inputs and symmetric model are imported. The contribution is the precise finite-type interpretation and preservation argument. The stated complexity bounds are presentations, not a claim of minimal complexity.

**Proof length:** Long (about 4,400 words across the linked proofs). The relative-constructibility real-part bridge and the explicit choice-free analytical coding.
