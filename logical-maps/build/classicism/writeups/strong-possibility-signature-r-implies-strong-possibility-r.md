# Strong Possibility (signature Σ) ⇒ Strong Possibility (pure)

<p class='cert'>Conjecture — Source: Misc.; produced by Cian Dorr, suggestion of 22 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 22 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Strong Possibility (signature Σ).** Every closed sentence of the fixed signature consistent with its Maximalist Classicism is possible under the distinctness-preserving modality.

## Conclusion

- **Strong Possibility (pure).** Each closed pure sentence consistent with Maximalist Classicism is possible under the distinctness-preserving modality.

## Notes

The restriction of the signature schema to pure P gives ◇≠P for every pure P consistent with Max(C(Σ)); the pure schema asks for every pure P consistent with Max(C). So the arrow holds iff Max(C(Σ)), equivalently Max(C) plus Separated Structure, is conservative over Max(C) for pure sentences: every pure sentence consistent with Pure Maximalist Classicism must remain consistent when Separated Structure is added. Adjoining constants is conservative for C, which is why Possibility Maximalism (signature Σ) implies Possibility Maximalism (pure), but for the maximalization a model of Max(C) + P would need its constants interpreted so that each realizes every consistent role at some truncation, which is not automatic. A proof of that conservativity, or a pure sentence consistent with Max(C) but not with Max(C) + Separated Structure, would settle it.

Correction of 23 October 2026 to the historical iff guidance above: for each fixed admitted Σ containing a relational constant, the completed ordinary argument gives [SP(Σ) entails pure SP] iff [SP(Σ) is inconsistent]. Let A₁ be Fregean ∧ ExactlyOneIndividual and U=Atom_t(A₁). The pure-profile model proves Con(Max(C)+U), whereas two signature-Max instances split A₁ and yield Max(C(Σ))⊢¬U. Universal-identity reflection gives C⊢◇_≠U→U; thus signature SP refutes a licensed pure-SP instance. In general the earlier consistency-preservation condition CP is sufficient, while the correct unconditional relation is implication iff (CP or inconsistency of SP(Σ)); the earlier iff needs Con(SP(Σ)). Here CP is explicitly false. This is completed ordinary mathematics, not a conjectured intermediate lemma. Final SignatureSplit verification certifies the signature splitting and its two concrete consistency models; it does not certify the separate pure-profile Max+U construction or the full incompatibility argument. Signature-SP consistency is not established, so this implication remains conjectured. Pure-SP consistency is separately exposed at [[strong-possibility-r-inconsistent]]. See the matching write-up for precise scope and evidence; no infinitary language or consistency premise is silently added.
