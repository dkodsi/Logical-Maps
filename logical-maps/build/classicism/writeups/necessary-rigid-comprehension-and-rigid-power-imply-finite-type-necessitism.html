Boxed Rigid Comprehension and current Rigid Power give Finite-Type Necessitism

OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 29 October 2026. This is the finite-generator argument of Simple generators for finite-type mathematical non-contingency, §§1–3, using the definitions and rigidity lemmas of GPT-6 Astra, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, Finite-Type Necessitism and Analytic Non-Contingency, §§2–3.

Statement and definitions

In C, assume boxed Rigid Comprehension and current Rigid Power. For every source type \sigma and externally specified finite n\geq0, we prove

\Box I^*_\sigma\ \to\ \operatorname{RepPower}_1(H_{\sigma,n+1},H_{\sigma,n}).

Here H_{\sigma,0}=\mathbb N_\sigma, the canonical Frege-number carrier, and H_{\sigma,i+1}=\mathsf P(H_{\sigma,i}), where \mathsf P(X)=\lambda A\, .\,\operatorname{Rigid}(A)\land A\leq X. The order means necessary inclusion. Rigidity has its two leading boxes. A carrier is fixed when it is rigid and its current members are necessarily distinct. Necessary fullness of a power means

\Box\forall F\exists A\in\mathsf P(X)\, \forall x\in X\,(Fx\leftrightarrow Ax). \tag{1}

The type of \mathsf P(X) is the next native predicate type. We do not require all levels to lie in a common host.

Fullness and iteration

First let X be rigid. At the current point, apply Rigid Comprehension to \lambda x\,(Xx\land Fx). Its rigid representative A is currently included in X. Persistence of X makes every current member of A necessarily belong to X; inextensibility of A therefore gives A\leq X. Thus A\in\mathsf P(X) and represents the prescribed predicate on X.

This proves in C the conditional from current RC and rigidity of X to current fullness. Necessitate that theorem, then use boxed RC and \operatorname{Rigid}(X)\to\Box\operatorname{Rigid}(X), which follows from the leading boxes and 4. This gives (1). No choice of a representative uniformly across possibilities has been made.

Now assume the displayed guard \Box I^*_\sigma. The existing theorem [[necessary-rigid-comprehension-implies-arithmetic-necessitism]] says exactly that H_{\sigma,0} is fixed. Its rigidity argument uses RC at each possibility to represent the least number carrier: canonical numberhood is persistent, the representative contains zero and is successor-closed necessarily, and leastness identifies it with numberhood. Its ND conclusion uses the necessary Peano guard.

Suppose H_{\sigma,i} is fixed. Current Rigid Power makes H_{\sigma,i+1} rigid; (1) makes it necessarily full. Its members are necessarily distinct as well. Indeed, two distinct rigid properties A,B\leq H_{\sigma,i} cannot be currently coextensive: persistence of either and inextensibility of the other would give mutual necessary inclusion and hence identity. Choose a current point of disagreement x. Its membership in one property persists. Every current member of the other is necessarily different from x, by ND of the fixed base; inextensibility of that property therefore gives its necessary absence at x. Thus the two properties remain distinct.

External induction now supplies every required finite level and (1) at each step. Rigidity plus (1) is exactly the canonical represented-power clause, proving FTN. Each use of RP is current. No optional RP premise is necessitated, and no infinite sequence of witnesses is collected.

Scope

Boxed RC is used to obtain fullness at every possibility. Current RC alone supplies only the current fullness established in the first paragraph. The necessary arithmetic guard is retained in every instance. Consequently this result asserts no unconditional arithmetic existence, no cumulative hierarchy and no same-host finite coding theorem. Boxed Plenitude already implies both premises through existing results, so its FTN consequence requires no additional edge.
