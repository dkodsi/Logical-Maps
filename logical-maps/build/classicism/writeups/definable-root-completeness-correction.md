# Definable-root completeness: preserved source and corrected argument

The construction is unchanged. This replaces the atomlessness explanation in the inherited lindenbaum argument, preserving its original text and attribution. Connecting proof: OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 25 October 2026, independently reviewed 28 October 2026; the [full FP proof](maximalism-and-forcing-possibilism.html), §§3–4, verifies the atom claim and all-type admission.

For n≥1 let A_n be the false-at-root proposition with profile Fregean Axiom and exactly n individuals. Such a profile is nonzero in a finite full extensional component. Fregean forces all relations to be extensional, and over n individuals the finite equality tests define every relation at every finite type. The resulting pointed finite hierarchy is unique up to isomorphism, with permutations transitive on the marked individual. Every marker-dependent term therefore has a single truth value in all A_n components, including transported targets. Consequently A_n∧T is either A_n or Bottom as a whole profile. A_n is an atom. Its actual bit can be chosen false independently; in fact the Fregean sentence itself is false at this root because its proposition algebra is infinite. The root's individual domain remains a singleton.

The A_n are pairwise disjoint. The proposition algebra is countably infinite, its profiles being finite terms. If every subfamily of this atom sequence had a join, disjointness would make the powerset of N inject into that countable algebra. Hence some subfamily has no join. Every external subfamily is an admitted current property by the unconstrained actual coordinate. Complementing the subfamily gives the missing GLB required to refute Boolean Completeness_t.

For the native countability proviso use counting type t. The usual numeral terms are distinct: admitted current properties with exactly n members distinguish their actual evaluations. Each belongs to the least inductive numerical family by finite induction. The current graph sending A_n to its numeral is admitted, as is its restriction to any chosen subfamily. Thus the missing-join family is countable in the native schema, and complementing it refutes Countable Boolean Completeness. This argument does not assert that the entire impredicative numerical field is externally standard.

Atomicity's failure follows independently from the recorded [pure Maximalism/Atomicity_t incompatibility](maximalist-distinctness-incompatible-with-atomicity-t.html). No atomlessness assertion is retained as evidence. This is a correction of the explanation, not a withdrawn Atomicity or completeness verdict.
