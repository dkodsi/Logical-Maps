Pure Maximalism and Forcing Possibilism

25 October 2026. Ordinary mathematical proof developed in this research task. Direct source of the connecting results: misc. Metatheory: external ZFC. This note is not a Lean certificate, a human review, or a literature-priority claim. No database record is changed.

Result. There is a model of C + pure Maximalism + the database’s strict, unboxed Forcing Possibilism, simultaneously at every admitted carrier type, including e. The existing one-individual definable-profile root is such a model. Conversely, the pinned-substitutions root satisfies pure Maximalism and refutes FP. Thus FP is relatively independent of pure Maximalist C. Pure Maximalism nevertheless entails the failure of boxed FP, already at carrier type t.

1. Exact scope

C is the fixed relational theory in topics/classicism/topic.yaml: base types e,t, with σ→τ admitted only when τ≠e. Logical Equivalence retains its H-theoremhood condition. Pure Maximalism has the fixed C side condition:

\Diamond P\qquad(P\text{ closed and pure},\ C+P\text{ consistent})

Its equivalence with the official distinctness form is the equivalence in distinctness-schema-r. No signature Maximalism or Strong Possibility is assumed.

The target is the upstream forcing-possibilism record at commit a89a5058ebbd5ebe93aec07d47a1a32e3b93f8bb:

\forall X^{\sigma t}\,\forall R^{\sigma\sigma t}. \bigl(\mathrm{CBA}(X,R)\land (\mathrm{Fin}(X)\lor X\prec D_t)\bigr) \to\exists p^t\ne\top\; (X,R)\cong([0,p],\le) \tag{FP}

Here D_t abbreviates the universal property of propositions. Fin is Dedekind finiteness using admitted injection graphs; strict smallness requires an admitted injection forward and none backward. CBA tests every admitted subproperty. Isomorphisms are admitted binary relations. All quantifiers are unboxed. The order on propositions is the Boolean order defined by whole-entity equality, not current material implication. The one-element Boolean algebra is permitted.

2. A general criterion at extensionally full evaluation points

Call an evaluation point extensionally full here if every external subset of every finite product of its admitted carriers is the current extension of an admitted relation. This is a semantic hypothesis, not Functional Choice or an assertion that coextensive relations are identical.

Externalization lemma. At such a point, a represented CBA is an externally complete Boolean algebra on its current carrier. Admitted injections and order isomorphisms exist exactly when the corresponding external graphs exist. In ZFC, internal Dedekind finiteness is therefore ordinary finiteness.

Indeed, the unboxed first-order order and Boolean-algebra clauses use literal carrier equality and the current extension of R. Every external subset of the carrier occurs among the properties in the completeness clause. Every required function graph is a current binary relation, including mixed-type graphs. Multiple intensional representatives of one extension do not change these graph predicates, which test their arguments by application. For proposition intervals the order is still the actual Boolean-algebra order on proposition entities.

Cardinal lemma. Every infinite externally complete Boolean algebra has at least continuum many elements in ZFC.

To prove it, choose disjoint nonzero elements b_0,b_1,…. If there are infinitely many atoms, take distinct atoms. Otherwise the complement of the join of all the finitely many atoms is nonzero: if it were zero, the algebra would be finite. That complement is atomless; repeatedly split the remaining nonzero part, retaining one part as b_n. Completeness gives s_U = sup{b_n : n∈U} for every U⊆ℕ. If k∈U, b_k≤s_U; if k∉U, every summand is below ¬b_k, so s_U≤¬b_k. Thus U↦s_U is injective.

Criterion. Suppose the evaluation point is extensionally full and its proposition Boolean algebra B is infinite with |B|≤2^ℵ₀. Then strict FP holds there if and only if B has infinitely many atoms.

For sufficiency, the externalization and cardinal lemmas show that every qualifying CBA is finite. If it has n atoms, choose distinct B-atoms a_1,…,a_n and set p=a_1∨…∨a_n. A further atom witnesses p≠top. Every q≤p is the join of exactly those a_i below q, since finite distributivity gives q=∨_i(q∧a_i). Hence [0,p] is the Boolean algebra of all subsets of an n-element set. The finite CBA is isomorphic to it, and the mixed-type graph is admitted. When n=0 use p=bottom.

For necessity, for each external natural number n choose 2^n distinct members of B and represent the finite Boolean algebra with n atoms on them. The relations are admitted, and this is a finite CBA at carrier t. FP gives an interval with n atoms. An atom of [0,p] is an atom of B: anything below it is already below p. Thus B has arbitrarily many atoms.

For countably infinite B, the strict-smallness part of the sufficient direction is even simpler and needs no completeness cardinal bound. If X injects into B and is infinite, X is countably infinite; a reverse injection exists and is admitted. Thus X≺B already implies X finite. The CBA cardinal lemma remains useful for continuum-sized roots and the non-strict variant in §5.

3. The definable-profile root

Use the construction already proved in research/2026-10-22/sp-structural-proof-package/profile-root-construction-20261022.md, §§1–5. Its pure reduct is exactly the upstream coalesced-definable-root construction. The following details make the present use self-contained.

Take a set of pure action models complete for C, with disjoint component categories. Add a fresh root o with one individual . For every component object V and a∈D_e(V), include an arrow h_(V,a):o→V carrying to a. Composition is k h_(V,a)=h_(V’,k·a). No nonidentity arrow returns to o.

For every relational type τ, let B_τ be the families of denotations of pure terms T(z):τ, with at most one free variable z:e, over all (V,a). Two terms determine the same profile exactly when these families agree. For τ=σ_1…σ_k t put, recursively,

D_\tau(o)=\mathcal P\bigl(D_{\sigma_1}(o)\times\cdots \times D_{\sigma_k}(o)\bigr)\times B_\tau \tag{1}

For t the first coordinate is a truth bit. Transport of (S,[T]) along h_(V,a) is T^V(a). Application slices S and applies the component term profiles; the individual * has profile z.

Closure and C. A term with finitely many root parameters obtains a profile by capture-avoiding substitution of their pure profiles into it. At each outgoing arrow it denotes the corresponding component entity. Abstraction has its arbitrary actual root extension and this term profile, hence belongs to (1). Bound variables at a component range over that component’s entire domain, not just the image of a root transport. Beta and eta follow from slicing and component beta/eta. The current truth, quantifier and literal-equality clauses validate H. H-provably equivalent abstractions have identical root extensions and identical profiles, since every component validates C. This verifies Logical Equivalence with the required theoremhood restriction. It does not restrict an old model to its term-denotations or assume a surjective transport.

Pure Maximalism. If C+P is consistent, a component object V makes P true. Any a∈D_e(V) gives an outgoing arrow at which P is true. Its root denotation therefore differs from bottom. Every original pure Maximalism instance holds at o, with its side condition still relative to C.

Countability and extensional fullness. There are countably many finite pure terms T(z), so B_t and D_t(o)=2×B_t are at most countable. There are infinitely many profiles, as the sentences “exactly n individuals” are separated by finite extensional C models. The proposition carrier is therefore countably infinite. Every current relation is admitted: pair its chosen extension with a bottom profile of the appropriate relational type. This includes every carrier type, e included, and every mixed-type graph. Higher carriers need not themselves be countable.

4. Infinitely many atoms in that root

Let F be the Fregean Axiom and E_n say that exactly n individuals exist, for each external n≥1. Put A_n=F∧E_n, a closed pure proposition.

At any component object satisfying A_n, F makes the proposition domain two-element and entails Extensionality at every admitted relational type. Inductively all type domains are finite and full: every subset of a finite product is definable with parameters by a finite disjunction of equality tests. Extensionality makes the resulting extension map injective. Hence the local typed logical structure is the full extensional hierarchy on n individuals, uniquely up to isomorphism. Its individual permutations act transitively. Therefore any pure proposition term T(z) has one fixed truth value over all pointed A_n-objects (V,a), independent of V and a.

This uniformity gives a statement about whole profiles, not merely current coextension. At every component object, A_n∧T(a) has either always the same truth as A_n, or always the same truth as bottom, with the choice fixed for T and n. The same is true after every component arrow, because the target with its transported individual is again one of the pointed objects in the family. Equality of these truth profiles is equality of propositions in the action model. Thus [A_n∧T] is [A_n] or [bottom].

F is false at o because its proposition domain is infinite. Consequently A_n has false root truth bit. For any root p=(b,[T]), the meet p∧A_n has false truth bit and profile either [bottom] or [A_n]. It is therefore literally bottom or A_n. The latter is nonzero: C+A_n has the full finite extensional model, so a component realizes A_n. This proves Atom_t(A_n).

The A_n are pairwise disjoint, since E_n and E_m are incompatible for n≠m. We have infinitely many distinct global Boolean atoms. Applying §2 proves FP at o for all admitted carrier types simultaneously.

An explicit witness for a finite CBA with n atoms is p=A_1∨…∨A_n. It is proper, since A_(n+1) is a disjoint nonzero atom. Choose an enumeration x_1,…,x_n of the given finite CBA’s atoms and map x to the join of A_i for which x_i≤_R x. The graph is an admitted σt t relation by (1). This supplies precisely the isomorphism requested by FP, including when σ=e. For the degenerate CBA use the unique map to bottom.

5. Further conclusions, with scope kept explicit

The non-strict version is also compatible with pure Max. In this same countable root, even a CBA whose carrier merely injects into D_t must be finite: an infinite complete Boolean algebra cannot be countable by §2. Thus replacing strict smallness by the non-strict bound also holds here. This is an ancillary strengthening, not a replacement of the target definition. It does not assert compatibility with the source’s additional boxed Rigid Comprehension, Intensional Choice, and CH conditions.

FP is not necessary under pure Max. Consider the closed pure instance FP_t. A full one-world extensional C model has precisely two propositions. Its whole proposition carrier with Boolean order is a finite complete Boolean algebra. The only p≠top is bottom, and its interval has one element. Thus this model falsifies FP_t. Therefore C+¬FP_t is consistent. Pure Max supplies ◇¬FP_t, equivalently ¬□FP_t. This proves the incompatibility of pure Max with boxed FP, without strengthening the reference theory in Max’s side condition.

Strict FP and ordinary current analysis can coexist with Max. There is also a continuum-sized variant. Enumerate all C-consistent pure sentences φ_i and choose countable-domain BBK C models M_i satisfying them, using countable completeness. Form the free-current root with any nonempty individual set E and

D_\tau(o)=\mathcal P\bigl(\textstyle\prod_jD_{\sigma_j}(o)\bigr) \times\prod_i D_\tau(M_i) \tag{2}

Choose arbitrary individual maps E→D_e(M_i). The profile-free closure proof is the same coordinatewise proof as §3: lambda and quantifiers use the entire domain within each coordinate. Every φ_i has a true component, so the root satisfies pure Max. The proposition algebra is 2×∏_i D_t(M_i), of cardinal continuum, since each factor is countable and nontrivial. For infinitely many distinct i, φ_i is F∧E_n, forcing D_t(M_i) to be two-element. The element supported only on each such factor is a global atom. Formula (2) is extensionally full, so §2 proves strict FP here as well.

Taking E=ℕ gives the very construction in research/2026-10-25/max-analysis-review/current-analysis.md. As proved there, it admits the full current real field on its continuum-sized proposition carrier using relational operation graphs and all external finite-arity relations. The present argument adds strict FP to that same construction. No operation representative, necessary analysis, or non-strict FP claim for this continuum-sized variant is being asserted.

6. Negative models and the six requested families

Pinned interpretation root. This root has exactly one Boolean atom, δ, the proposition true only at its identity arrow. To see that there are no others, let p have a nonzero component part and finite support S. Choose a component arrow (V,I) at which p is true and a propositional constant c outside S. Vary I(c) between top and bottom, fixing S. Both arrows remain in p. The admitted entity ĉ splits p into two nonzero parts; it lands in each component by construction and is pinned by {c}. Any p without a component part is bottom or δ. A four-element finite BA is representable on four distinct root individuals using equality-defined finite relations. FP would require an interval with two atoms, hence two global atoms. Thus coalesced-pinned-interpretations-root refutes FP.

Pinned substitution root. If a nonzero p has a component witness, the preceding fresh-constant argument splits it. Otherwise it has a root self-map witness f. Let S support p and choose two distinct fresh individual constants c,d outside S. Extend f|S once with c,d mapped to the same individual constant and once with different images. Both maps remain in p, and the equality proposition c=d distinguishes them. That proposition is admitted by C and supported on {c,d}. Thus every nonzero p splits: the proposition algebra is atomless. A two-element BA on two distinct root individuals is a finite represented CBA. FP would make it an interval below an atom, which is impossible. Therefore coalesced-pinned-substitutions-root refutes FP. Its already established pure Maximalism proves the negative half of the independence claim.
