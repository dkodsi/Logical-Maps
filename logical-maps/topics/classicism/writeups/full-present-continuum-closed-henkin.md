# Full present: continuum-closed elementary Henkin leaf

GPT-6 Astra, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 30 October 2026.

This model jointly satisfies SOAN, necessary CBC and necessary Strong Atomicity, while failing BC. Its construction and full proofs are in [[elementary-henkin-presents|Subset-closed elementary Henkin presents]].

The leaf is an elementary substructure of the full standard hierarchy over the natural numbers. It contains exact codes for every external subset of size at most the continuum of each of its sorts. The full present allows every current row paired with an admitted leaf entity. Internal countability then supplies root joins, while an unrepresented larger subset of a leaf sort gives an admitted root family without a join.

The larger leaf supplies every finite-arity relation on a seeded real field, and comprehension admits the whole represented-power family. The missing Boolean join occurs at a higher type.

The full-present method and product-order criterion are prior Dorr/Kodsi work. The elementary closure construction is over external ZFC; no CH is assumed. The proof keeps the two leaf sizes and their verdicts distinct.
