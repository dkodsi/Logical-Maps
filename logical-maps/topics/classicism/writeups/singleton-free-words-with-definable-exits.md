# Free words with definable exits

Possibility+ in the pure language and Witnessed Possibility hold together here. Maximalism for the signature fails even with a single propositional name. More generally, the same construction works for every signature containing a relational name or at least two individual names.

## The action

Take a set of disjoint pointed pure action models complete for C. Include any full finite models used below. Let $o$ be a new object with one individual $*$ and let its endomorphisms form the free monoid $W$ on $\max(\aleph_0,|\Sigma|)$ letters. They fix $*$. For each word $w$, each old object $V$ and each $a\in D_e(V)$ include an exit $(w,V,a):o\to V$, sending $*$ to $a$. Precomposition by a word $v$ gives $(wv,V,a)$, and postcomposition by an old arrow $k:V\to V'$ gives $(w,V',ka)$. There are no arrows from old objects to $o$.

At a relational type $\bar\sigma t$, a source entity has at each word $w$ an arbitrary current tuple row $E_w\subseteq\prod_iD_{\sigma_i}(o)$ and a term $u_w(z)$ of its type in the pure language with one free individual marker. Its value at $(w,V,a)$ is the old entity $u_w(a)$. Identify terms agreeing at all the old pointed interpretations. They then agree after old continuations as well. An endomorphism $v$ acts by reading $(E_{wv},u_{wv})_w$. At the identity use $E_1$ for application. This inductive definition on types includes propositions, whose word rows are bits. It combines [Dorr's definable exits](coalesced-definable-root.html) with [signature gluing on free words](full-free-monoid-glued-constants.html).

For a lambda abstract, compute each word row from the body at the transported parameters and all current tuples. At an exit over $w$, substitute the parameters' terms $u_w(z)$ in the body, substituting $z$ for each individual parameter. Abstraction gives a term of the required type in that same one-marker language, uniformly at every old object and interpretation of the marker. The old term calculation commutes with postcomposition. Induction on terms therefore gives the prescribed transports, including the modal and quantified clauses. Distinct entities differ on some word row or on some old value, where the old action distinguishes them. This gives the Henkin action conditions for C.

## The two positive schemata

A consistent closed pure sentence is witnessed in an old component. For a one-individual pure matrix $P(x)$ consistent with C, choose $V,a$ witnessing it and take $(1,V,a)$, which realizes $P(*)$. An instance with at least two displayed individuals has a false distinctness antecedent. Thus Possibility+ (pure) holds.

For each pure matrix with an actual witnessing tuple and each list of distinct matching signature constants, choose such a tuple and reserve a different letter $v$. There are at most $\max(\aleph_0,|\Sigma|)$ requirements. For each corresponding relational name, copy the witness's row and its entire exit term at $w$ to that name at $wv$, for every $w$. The sectors for distinct final letters are disjoint. Fill unused entries with empty rows and bottom terms. The only source individual interprets every individual name. At $v$ the transported names now coincide as whole entities with the selected tuple. Since the matrix is pure, its truth at $o$ with that tuple gives its truth after $v$ with the named parameters. This proves Witnessed Possibility.

## Failure in the signature

Two different individual names always agree, including at every exit. Their disequality is C-consistent. With these two named positions, a pair of distinct individuals is possibly witnessed at an old object, so Possibly Witnessed Possibility also fails.

For any relational name $c^{\bar\sigma t}$, put $U(c)=\forall\bar x\,c(\bar x)$, using $c$ itself in the propositional case. Write $E_1^e$ for having just one individual and use the single pure guard
$$
G=\Box\mathrm{ND}_t\land\mathrm{Atomicity}_t\land\Box E_1^e.
$$
The closed signature sentence $G\land U(c)\land\Diamond\neg U(c)$ is C-consistent. Use a full two-world S5 cluster with one individual, constant domains and all relational intensions. Interpret $c$ by the full row at the first point and the empty row at the second. The scalar algebra has four elements and all transports are bijective.

At $o$ the proposition true only on the identity, with every exit term bottom, differs from bottom and becomes bottom after any exit. Thus $G$ is false at the endpoint of every word. At an exit $(w,V,a)$ satisfying $G$, the name $c$ is given throughout that cone by $u_w(a)$. Since there is necessarily exactly one individual, $U(c)$ there agrees throughout with the closed pure sentence $\forall z^e\,U(u_w(z))$. Necessary ND at type $t$ gives necessary ND at all types. Together with Atomicity at $t$ it gives [No Pure Contingency](c5-and-atomicity-imply-no-pure-contingency.html). Hence the displayed signature sentence holds at no exit. Its existential abstraction is nevertheless possible, by the same full S5 component. This proves failure of Maximalism for the signature and of Possibly Witnessed Possibility. The former also refutes its stronger Possibility+ schema using the empty displayed tuple.

The signature restriction is sharp for the two implications in the opening paragraph. With only an individual name $a$, pure Possibility+ gives signature Possibility+: abstract $a$, if it occurs in the matrix, into one additional individual argument. The signature antecedent already supplies every distinctness required by pure Possibility+. Even ordinary pure Maximalism gives ordinary Maximalism for this signature. If $P(a)$ is consistent, choose a pointed model and witness $b$ realizing the pure abstraction $P(b)$. Prepend a singleton-individual object with an entrance sending its sole individual to $b$; include all old continuations of this entrance. Give the new object arbitrary current rows and free whole pointed-target coordinates, as in the ordinary coalescence. This is a C-model of the pure sentence $E_1^e\land\forall x^e\,\Diamond P(x)$. Pure Maximalism therefore reaches such a singleton in any model satisfying it. The actual name goes to its unique individual and then to a point realizing $P(a)$. In the empty signature the two language versions coincide.

## Further verdicts at the source

An arbitrary current well-order or transversal is an allowed identity row. For inextensible comprehension copy the given actual row at the identity and make every other word row and exit term empty. At a proper arrow this relation becomes bottom. At the identity any tester necessarily true of its current members thus bounds the whole relation. The identity-only scalar truth is a strong atom, becoming bottom along every proper arrow. It also witnesses ND-necessity of any truth, since it never becomes possible again after a proper arrow. There is just one actual individual.

The scalar algebra is the product $(2\times B)^W$, where $B$ is the countable Lindenbaum Boolean algebra in the pure language with one individual marker. Indeed pointwise implication throughout every old exit is precisely the order in $B$, and each word bit and term can be chosen independently. In one $B$-coordinate use the pairwise disjoint nonzero cells “the Fregean Axiom holds and there are exactly $n+1$ individuals”, for standard $n$. Take zero at all the other coordinates. Distinct subfamilies with joins would give distinct elements of $B$ in this coordinate, since the complement of any omitted cell is an upper bound. So some subfamily has no join. It is admitted as an identity row. Its injection into the standard Frege numerals at $t$ is an admitted current graph; the numerals differ by testing on finite sets of propositions. This refutes even Countable Boolean Completeness.

For revised Mathematical Possibilism the [finite ordinal thresholds](coalesced-definable-root.html) are pairwise distinct members of the actual inexact ordinal benchmark $H$: each pair separates in an old extensional model of appropriate finite individual size. Current graphs give an injection of a countably infinite set into $H$. All external current sets and graphs are admitted, and the independent word bits supply continuum many propositions. On a set of that size code the complete atomless Boolean algebra of regular open sets of Cantor space. Its carrier is revised-small. Indeed, if a subcarrier $Y$ exceeds $H$, then $Y$ is uncountable. A countably infinite $A\subset Y$ is strictly smaller, but all its subsets are represented, so an exact power code for $A$ requires at least continuum many elements, at least $|Y|$. No smaller subcarrier can code it.

Every nonzero externally complete principal interval of $(2\times B)^W$ contains an atom. Such an interval is a product of principal factor intervals; its completeness gives completeness of each factor interval by taking subsets supported only in that factor. Any nonzero factor interval is either the two-element algebra or a nonzero countable complete Boolean algebra. An atomless one would contain a sequence of disjoint nonzero elements whose subfamilies have distinct joins, contradicting countability. An atom in a nonzero factor gives an atom in the product. The coded complete atomless algebra consequently has no representing principal interval. Full current rows ensure that external completeness and these order comparisons have their current mathematical meanings.
